## Opening paragraph

Azsuna was once a gem of the night elf empire. Now much of it lies in ruins, a home for vengeful elven spirits and hidden enclaves of blue dragons. What remains of this haunted land has come under threat from conflict among Azeroth's defenders, an army of serpentine naga, and the Burning Legion.
Azsuna (/æzˈsuːnə/ az-SOO-nə) is an island and zone in the southwestern part of the Broken Isles. It holds the ruins of the night elf civlization that existed before the Great Sundering. The ghosts of ancient night elves roam this area, along with remnants of the blue dragonflight. The Nightfallen maintain a presence here. Azsuna was not destroyed in the Great Sundering, but was actually devastated several years before that by Queen Azshara herself.
This is where one of the Pillars of Creation, the  [Tidestone of Golganneth], was contested by the heroes of Azeroth and the minions of Azshara.

## History

Azsuna was originally populated by more than ten thousand elves.

Queen Azshara's pact with the demon lord Sargeras went mostly uncontested by her subjects. One of the few that dared to defy her was Prince Farondis.

As one of the queen's trusted advisors, Farondis was in regular contact with the Highborne of Zin-Azshari, but his palace in Azsuna was several leagues away from the elven capital. As such, he was able to witness Sargeras' influence on the Highborne court without falling sway to the Legion lord's temptation.

Farondis' plan was bold, but simple: destroy the Well of Eternity in Zin-Azshari. Doing so would close the portal to Sargeras' realm and stem the tide of evil before it even started. In order to accomplish this task, he would use the power of an artifact stored deep within Nar'thalas Academy: the Tidestone of Golganneth. 

Unfortunately, the queen's influence in Farondis' palace was already deep-seated. Vandros, a young noble in Farondis' court, caught word of Farondis' plans to defy the Queen, and reported the insurrection to Queen Azshara herself.

Azshara wasted no time in punishing Farondis and his people. In a frightening display of arcane power, she destroyed the Tidestone of Golganneth, and in doing so, released a wave of dark energy over all of Azsuna and its inhabitants.

Since that day, the elves of Azsuna have been unable to experience the release of death, and their spirits wander the land indefinitely.

At some point during the invasion, the Order of the Silver Hand sent their troops into the region after discovering a small demon force in the east, and hearing rumors that they report directly to Balnazzar. However, they were lead by Pitlord Argamemm who was killed by the paladins.

## Notes and trivia

- Some quests seem to name it "Aszuna".
- The Eye of Azshara island was originally called "Aszuna Isle".
- In the Legion World Panel during BlizzCon 2015, the devs mentioned that Azsuna had a "Greek isle theme, with stylized olive and cypress trees".
- Azsuna appears in the Crystalized Leyline rare mage spell in the Restoration of Azeroth Class Set for HS. Its flavor text reads: "This crystal has healing properties.... and this crystal summons an army of minions."

**Temple of a Thousand Lights** — Temple of a Thousand Lights is a large temple dedicated to Queen Azshara found south of Shackle's Den in southeastern Azsuna. Perhaps symbolically, it towers over the Garden of Elune.

**Vault of the Wardens** — The Vault of the Wardens is the Warden maximum security facility holding among the greatest threats to Azeroth, which once included the body of Illidan Stormrage, his Illidari demon hunters, and other terrifying monsters.

**Shackle's Den** — Shackle's Den is a small camp controlled by Mr. Shackle just north of the Temple of a Thousand Lights in Azsuna.

**Azurewing Repose** — Azurewing Repose is an area in Azsuna held by the Azurewing brood of the blue dragonflight. It is a dragon pool with ley energy.

**Ruins of Nar'thalas** — Ruins of Nar'thalas (/ˈnɑɹ.θə.lɑːs/ NAHR-thə-lahs) is a subzone found in Azsuna. The city is built around Nar'thalas Academy and the El'dranil Shallows that surround it. The Crumbled Palace and its Drowned Gardens are at the western edge of the area. The city was protected by the Nightwatchers.

**Prince Farondis** — Prince Farondis is a Highborne night elf and the ruler of Azsuna. During the War of the Ancients, Farondis was one of the few Highborne rulers who dared defy Queen Azshara, resulting in the destruction of his city Nar'thalas and most of Azsuna at her hands.

**Maiev Shadowsong** — Maiev Shadowsong (/ˈmaɪ.ɛv/ MY-ehv) is a legendary night elf warden and former priestess of the moon. Ruthlessly dedicated to her own sense of justice, she has been a part of many important events spanning the past 10,000 years.

**Court of Farondis** — The Court of Farondis are a group of night elven ghosts cursed by the destruction of the Tidestone of Golganneth in Azsuna.

**Azurewing** — The Azurewing (or the Brood of Senegos) are a brood of blue dragons of the blue dragonflight originally led by Senegos, based in Azurewing Repose in Azsuna on the Broken Isles.

**Nazjatar Empire** — The Nazjatar Empire is the naga civilization beneath the Great Sea, formed by Queen Azshara and her followers in the wake of the Great Sundering and the birth of the naga race.

**Highborne** — The Highborne (or quel'dorei, meaning "children of noble birth" in Darnassian), were the upper class and a sect of the ancient night elf civilization composed of the favored servitors of Queen Azshara. They had an aptitude for magic.
