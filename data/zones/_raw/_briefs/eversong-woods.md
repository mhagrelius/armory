## Opening paragraph

Eversong Woods (also known as Eversong Forest) is a woodland that stands in perpetual spring, comprising much of the blood elven kingdom of Quel'Thalas, which lies in the northern part of Eastern Kingdoms. At the north end lies the capital of Silvermoon City, and beyond it the Isle of Quel'Danas, where the Sunwell can be found. The blood elves have called the resplendent Eversong home for millennia, but the stewardship of this forested region has not been without its perils. Prince Arthas and the Scourge carved a path of destruction during the Scourge invasion of Quel'Thalas, leaving a tainted scar across Eversong Woods and corrupting the southern reaches into the haunted Ghostlands, but recent years have seen the forest restored by the power of the Sunwell.
In World of Warcraft: Midnight, Eversong Woods has been revamped to combine the original Eversong Woods zone with the Ghostlands, and is now connected to the rest of the Eastern Kingdoms.

## History

===Early history===

thumb|Eversong burned by the enslaved red dragons, as seen in [[Alleria: Light and Shadow.]]
Thousands of years ago, these forests were claimed by the Highborne, eventually naming themselves the high elves, who battled the resident Amani populace and took the land for their own. After creating the Sunwell, the Highborne became nourished, which enabled them to create their cities and shape the forests. The elves established the kingdom of Quel'Thalas, and northern Eversong contained the heart of their empire for millennia; the elves' capital of Silvermoon City was established at the forest's northernmost tip, near the ocean.

In time, the elves interwove their architecture to complement Eversong's natural landscape, blending the natural and their arcane-empowered structures together in beautiful ways. They lived in relative harmony with the creatures of the woodlands. The magi erected numerous runestones throughout Eversong, to serve as a dampening field that would limit the potency of all non-elven magic. They also magically locked Eversong into a state of perpetual springtime; the trees having gold leaves.

Shortly after Quel'Thalas was founded, the elves created a legendary bow named Thas'dorah, carved from the bark of the mother tree of Eversong. Named Thas'alah, or Light of the Forest, this tree was the oldest in all Quel'Thalas' woodlands, and its roots absorbed the energy of the Sunwell. From its enchanted branches, the entire region seemed to be bathed in the glow of an eternal spring.

===Second War===

Although the woods have seen their share of battle between the elves and their relentless neighbors, the forest trolls, the region retained a harmonic and peaceful feel for much of its history, standing strong for over 7,000 years. Its defenses were tested during the Second War, in which the edges of the forest mysteriously began to burn. During the war, a fully third of the forest was burned down by red dragons enslaved by the Old Horde, who managed to reach the northern forest but were unable to overcome Silvermoon's defenses, and the violence was soon quelled. In its aftermath, the Amani, who had allied with the fel-crazed orcs, were mercilessly hunted down within Eversong and beyond.

===Third War===

thumb|left|The Dead Scar left after the Fall of Quel'Thalas.
During the Third War in 20 ADP, Eversong lay witness to the horrors that the death knight Arthas Menethil brought upon the land during his invasion of Quel'Thalas, and the forest had since been permanently scarred by the death wrought by the hordes of undead. A huge, plagued tract of land was torn through the center of Eversong, known as the Dead Scar, which stood in stark contrast to the rest of the forest. Countless elves were slaughtered in Eversong, including their ranger-general, Sylvanas Windrunner. She was succeeded by Grand Magister Belo'vir Salonar, who took over the defense of Silvermoon after her death, but the capital was also unable to withstand the Scourge's onslaught. After Arthas had reached his goal on Quel'Danas and abandoned Quel'Thalas to its fate, the remnants of elves in the woods gathered under the banner of Ranger Lord Lor'themar Theron, who safeguarded the ruined kingdom while searching for any other survivors.

After High King Anasterian Sunstrider and the Convocation of Silvermoon had been slain during the onslaught, Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider became the rightful ruler and rushed back home from Dalaran to witness the damage to his kingdom with his own eyes. After he destroyed the corrupted Sunwell, he reunited with his people and renamed them sin'dorei, "blood elves" in thalassian, in honor of all those who had perished. Before his departure, the prince placed Lor'themar in charge of Quel'Thalas, then took their remaining forces to battle alongside the Alliance resistance in Lordaeron, and later under Illidan Stormrage in Outland.

Most of the blood elves remained behind with their regent, seeking to reclaim their land. Downtrodden and weakened by an addiction to magic that had become apparent after the destruction of the elves' Sunwell, the elves made little progress with this until the return of Grand Magister Rommath, who reinvigorated his people with the use of a technique taught to Kael on Outland, the mana draining. The elves gathered their strength and began reclaiming and rebuilding; fortunately, most of Eversong was not beyond healing, and the Scourge's defilement that had ruined the Ghostlands to the south was halted at Elrendar. Most of the blood elves remained in Eversong to continue rebuilding, while others turned their attention to the Ghostlands, hoping to drive the Scourge out of their beloved kingdom forever. Large portions of Eversong were reclaimed, and Silvermoon City rose again, but other threats, some old and some new, were looming on the horizon.

===The Burning Crusade===

thumb|Silvermoon City after being rebuilt.
By the Year 26 ADP, much of Eversong has been reclaimed, but not without cost. At this time, Eversong was split between Eversong Woods to the north, and the Ghostlands to the south. The destruction of the Sunwell adversely affected some of Eversong's wildlife, along with the elves' own arcane creations, driving many erratic and hostile. Dealing with this, along with understanding every blood elf's innate addiction to magic, was among the first things blood elf adventurers had to take care.

Eversong was also troubled by the Wretched, a group of disheveled, violent mana-addicts who could not balance their dependency on the arcane. Having grown bold with the advent of the elves' recent losses to the Scourge, the Amani forest trolls also made incursions into Eversong, diverting the Farstriders' attention. A south-western portion of Eversong, now known as the Scorched Grove, was burned by the elves in an effort to halt the corruption and the Scourge's advance; while successful, it also turned the local treants hostile.

## In the RPG

The Blackened Woods were once Quel'Thalas, where the elven kingdom of the same name makes its home. Razed by dragonfire in the Second War and ravaged by undead in the Third War, the land is a dead, ghostly realm. No living creatures, not even animals, remain in this haunted region. The spirits of slaughtered elves roam the landscape like mist given form and voice.

When the forest trolls of Zul'Aman raised again, the Farstriders stopped them in Eversong Forest in southern Quel'Thalas.

## Notes and trivia

- Sylvanas Windrunner was named after the woods of her homeland, and she considered the forest to be beautiful.
- The Tranquillien Archer's Bow was carved from the pristine white branches of Eversong Woods.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Fairbreeze Village** — Fairbreeze Village is a blood elf village in southern Eversong Woods just west of the Dead Scar and close to the Sunsail Anchorage. Fairbreeze is the place where many young adventurers learn their trades, before venturing forth into the Ghostlands and Tranquillien.

**Goldenmist Village** — Goldenmist Village is located in the northwestern area of the Ghostlands. Originally a high elven village, the Salonar sisters lived here, and Sylvanas Windrunner had friends here. Its inhabitants were slain during the coming of the Scourge, and for many years haunted the village ruins as ghosts.

**Saltheril's Haven** — Saltheril's Haven is a small enclave found in Eversong Woods hosted by Lord Saltheril, a blood elf who likes to throw elaborate parties in spite of the imminent threat of the Scourge. He is always happy to give invitations to his party table for those who can help him get supplies for his parties.

**Ruins of Deatholme** — The Ruins of Deatholme are all that remain of the Scourge citadel of Deatholme in Eversong Woods, at the southern end of the Tranquil Repose (formerly the Dead Scar). The Twilight's Blade have established themselves here, particularly within the Shadow Enclave beneath.

**Daggerspine Point** — Daggerspine Point is an island on the western shore of Eversong Woods that has been claimed by the Daggerspine naga. It was formerly named Shalandis Isle.

**Sanctum of the Moon** — The Sanctum of the Moon is an arcane sanctum located just to the west of Tranquillien. It has been overrun with arcane elementals and mana wyrms and has been partially destroyed.

**Silvermoon City** — Silvermoon City (or the City of Silvermoon and just Silvermoon) is the crown jewel of the blood elves, once the high elves, and the capital city of the kingdom of Quel'Thalas.

**Quel'Thalas (kingdom)** — The kingdom of Quel'Thalas (meaning High Home in Thalassian) is the realm of the blood elves, led by Regent Lord Lor'themar Theron since the fall of the Sunstrider dynasty.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Silver Covenant** — The Silver Covenant was a militant faction of high elves founded and led by Ranger-General Vereesa Windrunner. They rejected the admission of the blood elves into the Kirin Tor, and took it upon themselves to serve as a military deterrent for any potential Horde uprising in Dalaran City.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.

**Amani tribe** — The Amani tribe is a tribe of forest trolls who founded the Amani Empire in the northeastern side of ancient Kalimdor, with Zul'Aman as their capital. In modern times, their empire is a shadow of its former self, with most of their territory stretching only as far as the borders of Zul'Aman itself in Quel'Thalas.
