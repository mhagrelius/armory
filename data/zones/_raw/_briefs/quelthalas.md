## Opening paragraph

Quel'Thalas (/ˈkwɛlθəˌlɑːs/ KWEL-thuh-lahs) (Thalassian for "High Home") (sometimes spelled as Quel'thalas) is a forested area in northern Lordaeron. Known as the Land of Eternal Spring, in the northwest it is ruled by the blood elves, the descendants of the kingdom's Highborne founders. Its capital is Silvermoon City, the crown jewel of the kingdom of Quel'Thalas. In the southeast, it is ruled by the forest trolls, with their capital being the temple city Atal'Aman.
Around 6800 BDP, centuries after their exile from night elven society, Dath'Remar Sunstrider and his Highborne followers created the Sunwell, using a stolen vial of water from the Well of Eternity at a nexus of ley lines on the Isle of Quel'Danas. With the fount's power, Dath'Remar founded the kingdom of Quel'Thalas, while the land was magically bathed in an eternal springtime. Now calling themselves the high elves, they embraced the Sunwell as the very heart of their culture, since their kingdom was only made possible because of it. However, the elves earned the enmity of the forest trolls of Zul'Aman, under the Amani Empire, who wished to reclaim their ancestral lands conquered by the elves. After several long-spanning conflicts, High King Anasterian Sunstrider presided over the Troll Wars, which dealt the Amani a defeat they would never truly recover from. In its seclusion, Quel'Thalas continued to prosper with the protection of the Sunwell for millenia, repelling the Amani invasions and even the Orcish Horde during the Second War, until the kingdom's formidable defenses were bypassed by the corrupted death knight Arthas Menethil and his undead Scourge during the course of the Third War.
During the Scourge invasion of Quel'Thalas, Anasterian perished before Arthas's dark powers, the Sunwell was destroyed, and the bulk of the high elven people was slaughtered by the undead. In the wake of this devastation, Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider proclaimed the survivors reborn from the ashes of their shattered kingdom, as the blood elves. For several years, the blood elves worked tirelessly to reclaim their homeland from the lingering undead, and eventually succeeded in taking back much of their country, the capital of Silvermoon included. In a dramatic turn of events, a twisted Kael'thas returned to Quel'Thalas and attempted to summon the Burning Legion via the Sunwell. The prince's dark ambitions were not to be realized, however, and the Sunwell itself would ultimately be returned to the blood elves by the Prophet Velen. With the Sunwell restored, a bright future now lies ahead for Quel'Thalas. Following the end of the Sunstrider dynasty, Quel'Thalas is now instead led solely by its regent lord, Lor'themar Theron.
Quel'Thalas was originally introduced in Warcraft II, and its fall was first depicted during its sequel, Warcraft III. In World of Warcraft, it was initially only present in the form of an obscure, unmarked zone during the game's launch. It was later fully integrated during The Burning Crusade expansion, first with two starting locations for the Horde-aligned blood elves: Eversong Woods and the Ghostlands. A third additional level cap zone was later added during Fury of the Sunwell, the Isle of Quel'Danas. It was revisited as the primary setting for the Midnight expansion.

## Notes and trivia

- During the Second War, Turalyon wanted to sail straight to Quel'Thalas, but Alleria assured him that would be impossible as elves would never allow human ships up their portion of the river. Instead, they disembarked near Stratholme.
- In modern World of Warcraft geography, Thondroril River is near Stratholme and it continues it continues to the closed northern Lordaeron. As such, it could be speculated it continues into Quel'Thalas itself.
- In Warcraft III geography, however, there was a river going from the Darrowmere Lake up to Quel'Thalas, dividing Zul'Aman (which in the RPG books was named Greenrush River).
- During the Fourth War, the Horde sent a massive force to march on Arathi and take Stromgarde Keep in order to use it as a launching point for Gilneas. The Alliance in return sent their armies to protect the Stromic lands and continue their conquest of the Eastern Kingdoms, by cutting off the north, especially Silvermoon City.
- The demonym for a citizen of Quel'Thalas is Thalassian, sometimes Quel'Thalassian.
- It is hinted that the aqiri empire stretched as far as the lands that would much later be known as Quel'Thalas.
- Snowing was possible in the southern border of the kingdom, though it is possible that it is only possible after the fall of Quel'Thalas.
- The map published in the Warcraft II manual shows several regions including Khaz Modan, Quel'Thalas, and Lordaeron, each sharing the same font size. Azeroth is shown with a larger font size.
- The name in the Warcraft II manuals was written as "Quel'thalas". This is also the name of the unused zone in the northern part of the Eastern Kingdoms in WoW.
- According to a non-canon RPG source, Quel'Thalas is implied to be a separate continent.
- It has not been confirmed what the region was called before the high elves settled it and named it Quel'Thalas.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Sunwell Plateau** — The Sunwell Plateau is a raid instance located on the Isle of Quel'Danas north of Silvermoon City.

**Amani'Zar Village** — Amani'Zar Village is the capital of the Amani tribe and Amani Empire.

**Maisara Deeps** — Maisara Deeps is a location in Zul'Aman.

**Revantusk Sedge** — The Revantusk Sedge was the home of the Revantusk tribe, who lived in Zul'Aman.

**Sunstrider Isle** — Sunstrider Isle is a small island located in northwestern Eversong Woods. It is the starting area for blood elven players.

**Fairbreeze Village** — Fairbreeze Village is a blood elf village in southern Eversong Woods just west of the Dead Scar and close to the Sunsail Anchorage. Fairbreeze is the place where many young adventurers learn their trades, before venturing forth into the Ghostlands and Tranquillien.

**Sun's Reach** — Sun's Reach is a blood elven town and the staging area for the Shattered Sun Offensive on the Isle of Quel'Danas.

**Silvermoon City** — Silvermoon City (or the City of Silvermoon and just Silvermoon) is the crown jewel of the blood elves, once the high elves, and the capital city of the kingdom of Quel'Thalas.

**Atal'Aman** — Atal'Aman is an area located in Zul'Aman. Known as the temple city, it contains shrines to Nalorakk, Akil'zon, Jan'alai, and Halazzi.

**Quel'Thalas (kingdom)** — The kingdom of Quel'Thalas (meaning High Home in Thalassian) is the realm of the blood elves, led by Regent Lord Lor'themar Theron since the fall of the Sunstrider dynasty.

**Amani Empire** — The Amani Empire (also called the Amani Empire of Zul'Aman) is a forest troll empire located in northern Lordaeron. Established long before the Great Sundering, it took its name from the strongest tribe of forest trolls and the founders of the empire at that time, the Amani.

**Scourge** — The Scourge (sometimes called the Undead Scourge or Scourge Army) is a faction of undead created by the Burning Legion as the precursor for their Second invasion of Azeroth.
