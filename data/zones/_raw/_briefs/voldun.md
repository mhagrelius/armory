## Opening paragraph

The unforgiving desert of Vol'dun (/voʊl.ˈduːn/ vol-DOON) is one of the three regions of Zandalar. Previously a vibrant and lush jungle, Vol'dun is now a virtually uninhabitable, vast sea of dunes dreaded by all Zandalari, for to be exiled to its endless wastes is a sure sentence of death. And yet somehow, the wily nomads known as the vulpera have managed to eke out an existence here, despite facing a constant threat from the sethrak, a brutal snakelike race determined to reign over this arid wasteland.

## History

Thousands of years ago, this vast desert with rolling sand dunes was once a dense, vibrant jungle, described by Meerah as the jewel of the troll empire. The Zandalari trolls and the snake-like sethrak lived in harmony in this region, and together they worshiped the snake loa Sethraliss.

During the war between the trolls and the aqir roughly 16,000 years ago, the C'Thraxxi warbringer Mythrax the Unraveler was tasked with invading Vol'dun and descended upon the region, slaughtering troll and sethrak alike. Sethraliss sacrificed herself to defeat Mythrax, but not before the C'Thrax was able to destroy the disc at the heart of Atul'Aman. Due to Sethraliss' death and the various cataclysms that occurred in the region as the years progressed, the jungles of Vol'dun were transformed into a vast desert. As a result, most of the trolls abandoned the region. This left only the sethrak, who continued to survive and thrive in Vol'dun, which they considered the holiest of holy grounds.

After witnessing the destruction wrought by Mythrax firsthand, the sethrak knew that something had to be done to prevent anyone from raising the C'Thrax again. Sulthis, Vorrik, and Korthek worked together to seal Mythrax within the great pyramid. Years later, Korthek grew power hungry and sought to free Mythrax to conquer Zuldazar. He gathered like-minded sethrak and declared himself emperor of the Faithless.

Up until the end of the Fourth War, Zandalar sent its criminals here to die, and most of them end up perishing. The area was mostly inhabited by Zandalari vagabonds, sethrak, and vulpera. The Ashvane Trading Company also used to have a presence in the southern edge of Vol'dun, mining for Azerite.

In Exploring Azeroth - Islands and Isles, it is established that Vol'dun's exiles have been called back to Zuldazar by Talanji. In addition to that, it is written that the Ancient Burrows are being reconstructed and that the Vulpera have an outpost or settlement there alongside the Vulpera Hideaway, and plans to make a joint market with the Devoted in the Scorching Sands Outpost. The conflict between the Devoted and the Faithless seems to have ended, given the introduction to Akunda that many of their respective peoples have had to move on from the war. Atul'aman is also used by young Vulpera in a new rite of passage.

## Geography

thumb|Bonetrail Gulch.
Located in the north west of Zandalar, Vol'dun is a vast and unforgiving land. Dunes of sand, salt flats, and bone breaking rocky terrain stretch from coast to coast, meeting up with Nazmir to the east and Zuldazar to the south. Danger here comes as much from the arid and unrelenting landscape as it does from the creatures who eke out a living here. The sand-bleached bones of giant beasts litter the land, long since picked clean and polished to a gleaming smooth by the shifting sands. Brief moments of reprieve from the heat can be found to the north in the small stretch of marsh and in the caverns and oasis tucked away from the unrelenting sun.

## Notes and trivia

- Vol'dun's backstory is highly inconsistent, with different sources giving contradictory explanations for why the zone turned from a jungle to a desert:
- At BlizzCon 2017, the zone's state was explained as being due to the aqiri armies of the Old Gods attacking the region thousands of years ago. However, the aqir are never even mentioned in the live version of the zone. Several models of silithid fossils exist in Vol'dun's game files but go unused in-game, suggesting that the zone's backstory was changed sometime between its announcement at BlizzCon and the release of Battle for Azeroth.
- At the beginning of the Temple of Sethraliss dungeon, Vorrik says that it was Sethraliss' death that caused Vol'dun's jungles to wither to sand.
- In The Keepers' Keys (Alliance), Vorrik says that Mythrax destroyed the entirety of Atul'Aman and implies that it was the power unleashed from this event that caused Vol'dun's transformation. However, this is visibly contradicted by the fact that most of the pyramid is still standing, and in the Horde version of the same quest, Vorrik only says that the disc at the center of the pyramid was destroyed.
- In an interview at BlizzCon 2018, Alex Afrasiabi stated that it was Sethraliss' death combined with various cataclysms that happened in the region as the years progressed that caused Vol'dun's desertification.
- The children of Zandalari who were banished to Vol'dun used to be exiled alongside their parents, but this is no longer the case.
- A chain of exemption is required for being allowed to leave Vol'dun, to prevent exiled trolls from escaping. 
- Neutral "sleeping" lashers can be found in large quantities in Vol'dun. Area of Effect attacks must be used carefully.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Port of Zem'lan** — The Port of Zem'lan, also known as Zem'lan's port or simply Zem'lan, is a large, ruined Zandalari port town on the southwestern coast of Vol'dun. It is a cursed place, ruled over by Pirate-King Zem'lan and his crew of the eternal damned. In ages past, the port was attacked by the Faithless sethrak.

**Atul'Aman** — Atul'Aman is a titan pyramid in central Vol'dun.

**Redrock Harbor** — Redrock Harbor is a bay near the southwestern edge of Vol'dun, currently occupied by Ashvane Trading Company forces that have arrived to launch an Azerite-mining operation at the nearby Redrock Mines. The ship The Siren's Call is docked in the bay.

**Abandoned Burrows** — The Abandoned Burrows was the former main vulpera settlement in northeastern Vol'dun, recently destroyed by a force of Faithless sethrak led by Fangcaller Sraka. To the southwest lies the Brine Basin, while heading north out of the burrows takes one to the Whispering Crag.

**Goldtusk Inn** — The Goldtusk Inn is an inn owned by Rhan'ka and staffed by the Goldtusk Gang inside a cave on the northeast side of Whistlebloom Oasis in Vol'dun. According to Rhan'ka, "All exiles are welcome to rest at de Goldtusk Inn, de only luxury lodge in dese harsh dunes". Guests at the inn need to earn their keep.

**Camp Lastwind** — Camp Lastwind is a recently-established vulpera camp directly northeast of the Port of Zem'lan and southwest of the Exiles' Enclave in Vol'dun. Seemingly led by Norah, the vulpera at the camp plan to be the first to succesfully steal treasure from the Port of Zem'lan.

**Forward Camp** — The Forward Camp is a small Devoted sethrak camp directly northeast of the Terrace of the Devoted in Vol'dun. Some of the Devoted from the terrace tried to get ahead of the Faithless siege by attacking the Faithless' nearby Temple Incursion in the dead of night.

**Devoted** — The Devoted are a faction of sethrak led by Vorrik that are still loyal to the deceased serpent loa Sethraliss, unlike Emperor Korthek and his Faithless.

**Zandalari Empire** — The Zandalari Empire (or the Zandalar Empire) is one of the great troll nations, home of the Zandalari trolls, ruled by Queen Talanji. It is located on the island of Zandalar, formerly a mountain range where the first trolls settled around over 16,000 years ago.

**Faithless** — The Faithless, also known as the Faithless empire or the sethrak empire, (sometimes capitalized as the Sethrak Empire) are a force of militant sethrak led by Emperor Korthek.

**Ashvane Trading Company** — The Ashvane Trading Company (or simply Ashvane Company and later Ashvane Loyalists) is a Kul Tiran shipping and manufacturing company based out of the city of Boralus and owned by House Ashvane.

**7th Legion** — The 7th Legion is an elite special operations regiment composed of the Alliance's most seasoned veteran fighters. Initially serving behind the scenes, the Legion became the face of the Alliance military during the Fourth War, leading Alliance incursions into Zandalar.
