## Opening paragraph

The Undermine, or just Undermine, is the subterranean capital city of the goblins, located in the bowels of Mount Kajaro on the Isle of Kezan in the South Seas. Weaving through the heart of Kezan in a dizzying network of tunnels, vaults, and lava tubes, Undermine epitomizes the goblins' complex, unpredictable mindset. The city has many sluicetowns such as Pyrix, and its air is stale and oily. Troll slaves work in the city. The city of Bilgewater Port, located on the surface of Kezan, linked to the underground metropolis.

## History

===Background===
The Zandalari trolls originally enslaved the goblins and forced them to mine kaja'mite ore from the bowels of Mount Kajaro. After the goblins' intelligence was heightened by the magical mineral and they revolted against the trolls to claim Kezan for themselves, the mines that had been their prison, their slave camp, and the base of their rebellion now became the city of Undermine. It was there that they built the foundations of an empire in which their inventions would help them rule the world, but as the supply and effects of kaja'mite dwindled and the goblins' intelligence waned, they ultimately turned to mercantilism instead. Prior to the cartels of today, during the early cartel wars there were the Muckers Gang, Steambuilders Crew, and Dustwater Ring who fought for supremacy once freed from the Zandalari.

After Deathwing caused Kajaro to erupt during the Cataclysm, the molten lava threatened to incinerate the island. The fate of Undermine was initially unknown after the eruption, and the city was presumably heavily damaged, being as it was under Kajaro. Immediately after the disaster, some goblins believed all of Kezan to have been destroyed, though this later proved to be incorrect.

===The War Within===

After Jastor Gallywix struck a deal with Xal'atath to repair the Dark Heart, he was granted access to the Black Blood in exchange for restoring the relic. Struggling to fix it on his own, she directed him to Undermine, where he could find the assistance he needed. With the help of Darkfuse Enforcement, he orchestrated the kidnappings of Sprinkle Noggenfogger and Fleet Master Seahorn to force the Steamwheedle Cartel and the Blackwater Cartel to cooperate. Seeing the potential for profit, Nikki the Fixer and other top brass of the Venture Company backed Gallywix's efforts. Gallywix also manipulated the friends of Laslo Overbite, pressuring him to offer the Bilgewater Cartel's support as well. With the Cartels of Undermine under his control, and supported by his private army, Gallywix declared himself the "Overboss of Undermine" and stoked internal conflict among the goblins by offering rewards to those who sided with him.

Eventually, Laslo Overbite grew sick of Gallywix being boss and tried to stand up to him, demanding a new deal. However, due to the leverage Gallywix held over the other cartels, their leaders were too afraid to back Laslo. Left without support, Gallywix had Laslo executed to set an example. Because of Laslo's defiance, Gallywix placed extra pressure on Bilgewater.

When Monte Gazlowe and Renzik "The Shiv" uncovered Gallywix's schemes, they returned to Undermine to find it overrun with Darkfuse soldiers enforcing Gallywix's rule. Renzik urged Gazlowe to rally the cartels against Gallywix and tried to meet with each leader to gain their support. While Pamsy and Grimla Fizzlecrank supported them, Nikki the Fixer remained loyal to Gallywix. Baron Revilgaz and Marin Noggenfogger offered discreet help but remained cautious, fearing retaliation against their captured loved ones. Revilgaz revealed the location of the Blackwater Cartel's outgoing Black Blood shipments in Hovel Hill, allowing them to disrupt Gallywix's operations. Meanwhile, Marin informed them of the Venture Company and Darkfuse's activities along the Kaja'Coast.

Ethereals had also begun stalking the streets of Undermine in search of the Dark Heart. Now racing to secure the relic, Marin Noggenfogger revealed Gallywix's hiding place in the Heaps. Gazlowe and his allies tracked Gallywix to a secret warehouse, where they freed his hostages before confronting the Overboss. Cornered, Gallywix activated a barrier to shield himself. However, Phase-Thief Azir suddenly appeared beside him and took the Dark Heart. While Gazlowe tried to warn Gallywix about the dangers of dealing with Xal'atath, Gallywix ignored him and vowed to bury him for challenging his rule over Undermine.

thumb|Jastor Gallywix
With their friends safe, Gazlowe was prepared to leave Undermine once again, but Renzik urged him to stay and deal with Gallywix. As the two argued, Nikki the Fixer prepared to assassinate the Trade Prince on Gallywix's orders. Renzik spotted her lining up the shot and pushed Gazlowe out of the way, taking the hit and being reduced to ash. Renzik's death inspired Gazlowe to finally take a stand against Gallywix, now backed by the cartels. They spread word of Renzik's sacrifice, turning him into him into a martyr and rallying the people of Undermine. Baron Revilgaz and Marin Noggenfogger headed to Blackwater Marina to free it from Darkfuse's grasp, while Pamsy, Orweyna, and Grimla Fizzlecrank went to the Venturewood to take out Gallywix's loyalists within the Venture Company and destroy his Black Blood stockpiles. The citizens of Undermine rallied behind Gazlowe and took to the streets, marching on the Gallagio. Gallywix ordered Nikki and the Darkfuse to stop their rebellion, but she was killed in the ensuing battle, forcing Gallywix to retreat into his hotel.

While he hunkered down in the Gallagio, Gallywix called in every favor he was owed, sending his goons into the streets of Undermine. With the help of Azeroth's champions, Gazlowe and the cartel leaders defeated Gallywix's forces and salvaged their tech to build a tank capable of breaching the Gallagio's defenses. Inside, they fought their way through his opulent casino and eventually cornered the Overboss. In a last-ditch effort to stop them, Gallywix piloted his own mech and faced them himself. After being defeated, Gallywix furiously exited his mech and began kicking it, until it eventually gave way, crushing him to death.

In the aftermath of Gallywix's death, Gazlowe and the cartel leaders gathered to build a new tomorrow for Undermine.

## In the RPG

thumb|The Undermine in Lands of Mystery.
Undermine  is the nefarious capital of the goblin's trade empire, the Trade Coalition. Located beneath and within Kezan, Undermine is the center of goblin civilization. It is connected to the island via a series of volcanic tunnels which were created by Mount Kajaro's volcanic and tectonic activities.

The trade princes each control their own private armies and trade fleets. As a result, Undermine is a busy place, as the goblin city goes about its business.

There are some Horde and Alliance ambassadors present in the Undermine.

===Description===
The main centers of population in Undermine are in volcanic caverns beneath Kezan. Secondary island caverns exist farther away, and the goblins have constructed tunnels of thick glass that lead to these smaller areas. Passing through these tubes means walking along the ocean's floor and looking out to see the colorful fish and hungry sharks nearby. Bilgewater Port is linked directly to Undermine.

As the capital of goblin culture, Undermine teems with chaotic activity. Goblin alchemists and tinkers practice their crafts and set off explosions. Slave markets bustle in out-of-the-way places. Shops and businesses of all kinds are based here, and the headquarters of the Trade Coalition, the Venture Company and myriad other enterprises are within this city. In Undermine, you can find the extremes on both ends: reeking slave pens in one area, the trade princes' lavish palaces in another. Zovzik's shop is located in the city.

Of particular interest is the fact that, wandering around Undermine, is a strange breed of a creature called hobgoblins. They resemble goblins, but are much taller and have purple skin.

## Geography

The explorable part of Undermine in-game is the downtown part of the city. More regions of Undermine exist in lore, connected to downtown via rockets at Slam Central Station, but are inaccessible to players. Uptown also exists.

## Notes and trivia

- Vinny Slapchop sold Undermine Remnant after Deathwing attacked Kezan.
- It was originally stated that jungle trolls were responsible for enslaving the goblins of Kezan, but  later changed them to Zandalari trolls. Moreover, the game portrays them as forest trolls.
- The goblin creation screen description states that the trolls forced the goblins to mine kaja'mite out of the Undermine, but other sources state that the city was created from the camps in the mines after the goblins overthrew the trolls.
- Blizzard originally wanted to include Undermine in the original World of Warcraft along with goblins as a playable race, with Johnathan Staats describing it as "tinker town" and "crazy". However, as the team did not have a lot of goblin art assets, building the goblin homeland would have required "a ton" of work, leading to both Undermine and playable goblins being cut from classic WoW.
- Note that the Undermine was already concepted in the early World of Warcraft beta not as a city on an island, but as a continent southeast of Kalimdor.
- There is an American server called Undermine and a Spanish server called Minahonda, which means Undermine (Minahonda is more precisely translated as Deepmine).
- During a build of the Battle for Azeroth alpha, the dungeon The MOTHERLODE!! was called "The Undermine".
- Similarly, it was said that Mogul Razdunk and his Venture Company took control over the city and its Azerite to become a trade prince.
- Goblin NPCs in Undermine will occasionally deviate from their pathing to insult gnome player characters.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Trade Coalition** — The Trade Coalition is the name of the goblins' merchant empire. The Venture Company is part of the coalition, as are presumably all goblin organizations and cartels, including the Trade Fleets.

**Cartels of Undermine** — The Cartels of Undermine is the fifth Major Faction of The War Within.

**Bilgewater Cartel (Undermine)** — Bilgewater Cartel is a faction of The War Within, based out of the Incontinental Hotel in Undermine.

**Blackwater Cartel (faction)** — Blackwater Cartel is a faction of The War Within, based out of Blackwater Marina in Undermine.

**Steamwheedle Cartel (faction)** — Steamwheedle Cartel is a faction of The War Within, based out of Steamwheedle Laboratories in Undermine.

**Venture Company (faction)** — Venture Company is a faction of The War Within, based out of Venture Plaza in Undermine.

**Darkfuse Solutions** — Darkfuse Solutions (or Darkfuse Cartel) is a goblin cartel that has operated in numerous theatres across Azeroth and beyond. Originally known as the Blackfuse Company, it was led by the eponymous Siegecrafter Helix Blackfuse and provided advanced weaponry for the True Horde under the direct order of Warchief Garrosh He…

**Tinkers' Union** — The Tinkers' Union is a goblin organization led by Director Riddlevox. Their headquarters is in the Undermine, but they also have workshops in Gadgetzan.

**Demolition Dome** — The Demolition Dome  is an arena in the Undermine. It is guarded by the world boss, The Gobfather.

**Bilgewater Cartel** — The Bilgewater Cartel is one of the largest and most powerful goblin cartels found on Azeroth, run by the shrewd Trade Prince Monte Gazlowe. From their headquarters on Kezan at Bilgewater Port, the cartel dominated the trade all over the South Seas under the leadership of Jastor Gallywix, until it was destroyed due to …

**Mount Kajaro** — Mount Kajaro is a still-active volcano on the Isle of Kezan. According to Al'tabim the All-Seeing, Kajaro is an area plagued by volatility - marked by violent and oftentimes magical volcanic eruptions. Kaja'mite was mined underneath it. The Undermine is believed to be situated deep in the bowels of the mountain.

**MOTHERLODE!!** — The MOTHERLODE!! (sometimes spelled with varying capitalization or number of exclamation points) is a maximum-level dungeon located on the Isle of Kezan. The goblins have heard word of Azerite and its value and have started digging deep into Azeroth to gain more of it.
