## Opening paragraph

The Isle of Dorn is a zone on the surface of Khaz Algar and introduced in World of Warcraft: The War Within. The island is located west of Pandaria and southeast of Kalimdor. It is a lush and vibrant island with sweeping vistas, cascading waterfalls, and majestic mountains.

## History

When the Elemental Lords dominated the planet, the land that would become the Isle of Dorn (then part of the Kalimdor supercontinent) was under the control of Al'Akir.

The titans' forces came to call this area Sector AR-938. After the Ordering of Azeroth, a contingent of earthen was sent by the titan keepers to investigate a geological anomaly in a fissure detected on Sector AR-938. In time, these earthen began to display behaviors similar to those who had fallen under the influence of the flesh, despite being separated by a great distance and sharing no cultural ties whatsoever. To the keepers, the similarities were uncanny. Although both groups remained physiologically distinct, the earthen had started to refer to Sector AR-938 as Khaz Algar, and their behaviors, language, and demeanor showed many similarities to other former earthen groups. The keeper Archaedas assigned the watchers Dornic and Galan to oversee the earthen. The earthen would eventually rebel against Dornic for his cruel treatment.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Dornogal** — Dornogal  (/ˈdɔːrnəɡɔːl/ DORN-ə-gawl) is the capital city of the earthen dwarves of Khaz Algar and serves as the player capital of The War Within. It is located in the center of the Isle of Dorn.

**Council of Dornogal** — The Council of Dornogal is the ruling council of the earthen of Khaz Algar.

**Dornogal (nation)** — Dornogal is a nation of earthen located on the continent of Khaz Algar. Under the leadership of the Council of Dornogal, the Algari earthen society has been isolated for thousands of years, gradually developing their own behaviors, language, and demeanor with many similarities to other former earthen groups.

**Freywold Village** — Freywold Village  is the home of the Unbound earthen located in the southwestern part of the Isle of Dorn. **Boskroot Basin** — Boskroot Basin  is a fungarian-infested area in south-central Isle of Dorn. The Fungal Folly delve is found at  and the Basin Bypass skyriding race is at .

**Boulder Springs** — Boulder Springs  is found in the southeastern part of the Isle of Dorn. Boskroot Basin and Opalcreg are found to the west, and Dornogal lies to the north via Crossroads Plaza.

**Cinderwold** — The Cinderwold (once known as the Cindercreg) is an elite area in the eastern part of the Isle of Dorn. This ironwood forest is primarily inhabited by cinderbees, though storm wolves can be found here as well. The Ironwold lies to the west and the Cinderbrew Meadery lies to the east.

**Dhar Kazhad** — Dhar Kazhad  is found in northern Isle of Dorn. With War Mode enabled, the area is a Free For All and  [80WQ] Along for the Ride is sometimes available.

**Mourning Rise** — Mourning Rise  is a graveyard overtaken by the Candlelit kobolds in northeastern Isle of Dorn. The Kriegval's Rest delve is found at  on the lowest level.

**Opalcreg** — The Opalcreg  is a nerubian-occupied mine on the north shore of Isle of Dorn, south of Dornogal.

**Three Shields** — The Three Shields  is a set of three islands off the northeastern shore of Isle of Dorn. A Dragon Glyph is found at  atop the highest peak of the middle island. It is home to the Shimmerfin gorlocs and a flock of cloudrooks. It was also home to Olbarig, where he lived as a hermit after he left the Stormriders.

**Thunderhead Peak** — Thunderhead Peak  is a mountain peak found in northern Isle of Dorn.
