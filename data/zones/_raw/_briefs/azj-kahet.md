## Opening paragraph

Azj-Kahet (/ˈɑːʒ.kəˌhɛt/ AHZH kə-HET) is a subterranean zone located in the deepest part of Khaz Algar, south and below Hallowfall and the Ringing Deeps. The high capital of nerubian society, Azj-Kahet is home to the last kingdom of living nerubians. Unlike Azjol-Nerub, which was consumed by the Scourge, Azj-Kahet represents nerubian society as it once was—deadly survivors of mythic wars that have played out time and again over millennia. The zone is dominated by the sprawling City of Threads, which has been built on top of itself over and over as its districts decay, with the highest locations reserved for the wealthy and those who have the favor of Queen Ansurek. Azj-Kahet also features areas of dark and beautiful wilds, inhabited by other creatures who have found ways to live alongside the nerubians.
With the Black Empire destroyed and the Old Gods dead, Xal'atath, the Harbinger of the Void, has traveled to Azj-Kahet to conscript its inhabitants into a new army to bring Azeroth to its knees. She has entered into a bargain with Ansurek, offering her people the opportunity to rise from their isolation and become a powerful kingdom once again, and in return for their loyalty has provided a way to evolve chosen nerubians into a new kind of powerful soldiers. The nerubians now harvest the Black Blood of the Old Gods within Nerub-ar Palace and are engaged in a continuous assault against the Arathi of neighboring Hallowfall. However, not all nerubians agree with Ansurek, and they have begun to maneuver against her and Xal'atath's plans.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Weaver's Lair** — The Weaver's Lair is found in Azj-Kahet. It is the headquarters of the Severed Threads and possesses a portal to Dornogal.

**Mmarl** — Mmarl is a niffen settlement found in Azj-Kahet. Mmarl has been around for a long time, longer than Loamm. The niffen there have managed to set up trade with the nerubians of Azj-Kahet and some of them are even able to trade in the City of Threads.

**City of Threads** — The City of Threads is the capital of the nerubian kingdom of Azj-Kahet, taking up the southernmost third of the Azj-Kahet zone. A sprawling, decaying city that falls away into the depths, the capital has been built on top of itself many times over.

**Azj-Kahet (kingdom)** — The Kingdom of Azj-Kahet is the last nation of living nerubian on Azeroth, which is found in Azj-Kahet, an underground region located in the deepest part of Khaz Algar.

**Severed Threads** — The Severed Threads is the fourth Major Faction of The War Within. They represent the conspiracy aimed at dethroning Queen Ansurek and restoring Azj-Kahet to its former glory.

**Wildcamp Ul'ar** — Wildcamp Ul'ar is an area found in Azj-Kahet.

**Faerin's Advance** — Faerin's Advance is found in Azj-Kahet. It is a camp established by Faerin Lothar after the Reckoning's crash. Survivors of Dalaran went here to rest after escaping from Rak-Zakaz.

**Siegehold** — Siegehold is found in Azj-Kahet.

**Wildcamp Or'lay** — Wildcamp Or'lay is found in Azj-Kahet.

**Rak-Ahat** — Rak-Ahat is an area found in Azj-Kahet. Most of the silk in Azj-Kahet is produced here, from the finest veils to the strongest warsilk. The Severed Threads also uses the facility to smuggle military secrets woven into the silks.

**Rak-Ush** — Rak-Ush, also known as Rak-Ush Ranch, is an area found in Azj-Kahet.
