## Opening paragraph

Zul'Aman (/ˈzuːl əˌmɔn/ ZOOL-ə-mawn) is a fortress and a temple city that serves as the capital of the Amani tribe, located in the southeastern portion of Quel'Thalas, on northern Lordaeron. The main temple area is known as Atal'Aman.
It was revisited as a zone in World of Warcraft: Midnight.

## History

===Founding===
thumb|Zul'Aman in World of Warcraft.
During the Aqir and Troll War circa 16,000 BDP, atop the site where the Zandalari and their allies had killed the C'Thrax Kith'ix, they established a new settlement which would one day grow into a sprawling temple city known as Zul'Aman.

===Empire's Fall===
By the time of the Troll Wars in 2,600 BDP, the Amani Empire in Zul'Aman was the most powerful empire in the Eastern Kingdoms. The high elves of Quel'Thalas eventually joined with the humans of the Empire of Arathor in an immense battle that signaled the end of the forest trolls' empire. The Amani would never recover enough to extend their land beyond their home province of Zul'Aman, but they continued to be an enemy of humans and elves for thousands of years.

Silvermoon City itself was constructed over the site of sacred troll grounds, burial grounds of the Hash'ey Atal'Kindo, which earned the elves further enmity from the neighboring Amani tribe.

===Second War===
thumb|Zul'Aman besieged by the Alliance of Lordaeron.
During the Second War in 6 ADP, Zul'jin and the Amani joined the Orcish Horde, while the high elves joined the Alliance of Lordaeron. The Warlord of Zul'Aman eventually disappeared mysteriously after the orcs were defeated. In truth, Zul'jin had been captured and tortured by the Farstriders, and retreated into Zul'Aman to recuperate after an Amani war party saved him.

===Fall of Zul'jin===

During the invasion of Outland in 26 ADP, Zul'jin saw the opportunity to take revenge against Quel'Thalas when most of the blood elves and Horde were busy fighting in Outland. Not wanting to fight a war on two fronts, the Horde sent a task force of some of their strongest champions to kill the Amani leadership within Zul'Aman. At the entrance to the city, the adventurers encountered Budd Nedreck, who convinced them to also steal the treasures within as well.

===Cataclysm===

thumb|The elves and trolls reunion at the Amani Pass.
With Zul'jin dead, the halls of Zul'Aman become quiet until the Cataclysm in 28 ADP. With the aid of the Zandalari, the remaining Amani united under the leadership of Warlord Daakara, who sought to avenge Zul'jin's death and realize his dreams of rebuilding the troll empire. When all tribes were invited to become a part of this new empire, Vol'jin of the Darkspear declined this offer, and instead joined forces with the Farstriders and Silver Covenant. Along with Halduron Brightwing and Vereesa Windrunner, he could be found planning their next move on the outskirts of Zul'Aman.

Heroes of the Alliance and the Horde eventually entered Zul'Aman and thwarted the Zandalari plans by killing Daakara.

===Midnight===

During the War of Light and Shadow, the Twilight's Blade launched an incursion into Zul'Aman. To combat their Void magics, Zul'jan led the Amani tribe into Eversong Woods to harvest Lightwood to craft weapons. They were driven back by the blood elves and the Vanguard of the Light, but lingered at the Amani Pass to eventually resume their operations. Zul'jarra arrived at the Pass to reinforce her brother, but after the costly battle against the blood elves, they did not have the strength to defend Atal'Aman from the Twilight's Blade, resulting in it being captured.

To defeat the Blade, Zul'jarra decided to become a hash'ey to bring back the aid of the loa. While she journeyed across Zul'Aman to garner favor with the various loa, Zul'jan led the Amani warriors into another costly battle to reclaim their Lightwood. Despite being scolded by his sister, he once more led their forces to drive out the Twilight's Blade. Though initially overwhelmed, Zul'jarra came with reinforcements from the loa and other Amani tribes. Together, they killed Mor'duun and weakened the cult's presence in Zul'Aman.

## In the RPG

The forest troll kingdom of Zul'Aman is a dark, primal woodland and the only place of life in the Ghostlands of Quel'Thalas. The trolls' voodoo energies permeate the climate, and ancient, stepped ziggurats rise above the canopy. Warlord Jin'zakk is rumored to command the trolls, and his witch doctors and shamans provide him with magic support.

===History===
Once, forest trolls controlled a vast swath of Lordaeron. Their empire was called Zul'Aman, and the area currently known as Zul'Aman was their power center. Their dominion stretched across the continent, and they threatened the nascent human nations and the fledgling kingdom of Quel'Thalas with extinction. Eventually, the high elves traded arcane knowledge to the humans for help in defeating the trolls, and the two races bludgeoned the forest troll empire. Now, this tiny area in northeastern Lordaeron is all that remains of the trolls' continent-spanning empire.

In the years after their defeat, the trolls largely gave up their imperialist mindset, though they protect their small territory zealously. They have never forgiven the humans or the elves for their near-genocide. Warbands patrol the forest, slaying all living and undead creatures that are not trolls or natural forest beasts. They worship ancient deities, though whether these deities are the mythic Old Gods or some strange creation of their voodoo witch doctors is unknown. With Lordaeron and Quel'Thalas in ruins, the trolls move out to reclaim their kingdom.

Any trolls encountered try to kill on sight. Explorers need to be careful but are not totally without allies in Zul'Aman. Several bands of high elves, survivors from Quel'Thalas, fled south and now wander throughout the forest. Those who stay one step ahead of the trolls survive; those who do not will learn first-hand what the cannibalistic trolls do with their victims. Scourge patrols, hunting the elven refugees, also stalk through the land.

The Scourge group in Zul'Aman is led by Necromancer Ru'sad, and they are having difficulties trying to end the elves as they know the forests better than the undead. Incorporeal troops such as shades or ghosts would be more helpful as the ghouls and zombies make a fearsome amount of noise trudging through the overgrowth alerting the elves. The gargoyles are somewhat more useful; they fly through the clearings and dense tangles of the jungle and allow the undead to discover refugee groups which they later kill as they sleep. The Scourge are confident that it is only a matter of time before they finally destroy all of the elves. They intend to prevent them from joining with their allies in Hillsbrad or Khaz Modan, or rally together. They intend to stop them from reviving the Sunwell.

A great witch doctor, Ral'jin, rose to prominence in Zul'Aman. Ral'jin sent out hunters to capture hundreds of prisoners for use in a massive sacrifice, planning to use this ritual to summon Ula-Tek, the Serpent Goddess. This information is bad news, but the Ironforge dwarves are particularly concerned: they have seen the devastation that an elemental lord's rebirth brings, and a bona fide Old God must be even more powerful. The Dark Iron dwarves, however, are jubilant. Ula-Tek, perhaps an Old God, could serve as a companion and ally to Ragnaros. The Dark Iron sent a contingent to Zul'Aman to wish their goodwill and to protect Ral'jin as he completes the lengthy ritual.

===Culture===
thumb|Zul'Aman in Lands of Conflict.

Forest trolls are vile creatures. They practice evil voodoo magic and worship primal beings they call the Forest Gods. Violent cannibals, forest trolls eat their own kind as well as their enemies with equal zest. The trolls joined forces with the Horde in the Second War, as the orcs promised them vengeance against Lordaeron and Quel'Thalas, but the trolls' savagery was too much even for the orcs. Due to this, the trolls also hate orcs now, who they feel betrayed them. In short, forest trolls hate everyone — including each other.

Witch doctors control Zul'Aman, with shamans and warchiefs in lesser leadership positions. The exception to this tradition is Warlord Jin'zakk, who rules the entire kingdom. The voodoo witch doctors wield arcane magic, while shamans provide divine support. Forest troll society revolves around hunting, spear fishing, shamanism, and voodoo. They make their capital at the Shrine of Ula-Tek, a massive ziggurat they hold sacred. Forest troll society is patriarchal, though cunning and aggressive females occasionally hold important positions. Forest trolls are primitive, possessing few metalsmithing skills and no knowledge of steam technology though they are masters with thrown weapons, be they axes or spears.

With both Lordaeron and Quel'Thalas fallen, the forest trolls see a chance to regain their old empire. They push the borders of their realm ever outward. Warbands strike south, north and west, seeking to reclaim more of their land. The trolls are myriad — almost 20.000 total, and most of them skilled hunters. They have been fighting for centuries and are competent tacticians. Alone, Zul'Aman has little chance against the Scourge. However, if approached in the right manner (by a tauren or goblin, perhaps) and promised their empire, they may be willing to join an alliance.

Like all trolls, Zul'Aman's forest trolls are lean, strong and tough. They possess long ears and noses and jutting canines. Their faces are hard and feral. Forest troll hide ranges from dark brown to light green. Many forest trolls wear primitive jewelry, often wood or bone, on their clothing, strung around their necks or thrust through their noses and earlobes. The males often maintain tall, dyed mohawks. Some paint themselves in tattoos. Forest trolls are organized into tribes, with names such as Blackleaf and Treeblight, and members of the same tribe are relatively loyal to each other.

## Notes and trivia

- After the war against the Primalists, the void elf Riftwalkers noticed Void activity in the northern Eastern Kingdoms, and the trail went cold near Zul'Aman.
- Benjari Edune once ventured deep within the forests of Zul'Aman.
- The Hatchet Hills flight path is labeled as Zul'Aman.
- Early in World of Warcraft development, the Zul'Aman area was in the game's files.
- The ritual to open the gates of Zul'Aman requires several people to hit a Strange Gong together.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Amani'Zar Village** — Amani'Zar Village is the capital of the Amani tribe and Amani Empire.

**Revantusk Sedge** — The Revantusk Sedge was the home of the Revantusk tribe, who lived in Zul'Aman.

**Maisara Deeps** — Maisara Deeps is a location in Zul'Aman.

**Broken Throne** — The Broken Throne, also known as Atal'Kaldan, is an Amani ruin located in southwest Zul'Aman that has been taken over by the Twilight's Blade as their base of operations in the region. It is here that chieftain Kol'anji of the Vilebranch tribe challenged Zarama for leadership before being slain by Zul'jarra.

**Camp Stonewash** — Camp Stonewash is an area located in Zul'Aman.

**Den of Nalorakk** — The Den of Nalorakk is a dungeon in Midnight. It will have adventurers prove themselves to earn Nalorakk's favor.

**Atal'Aman** — Atal'Aman is an area located in Zul'Aman. Known as the temple city, it contains shrines to Nalorakk, Akil'zon, Jan'alai, and Halazzi.

**Amani tribe** — The Amani tribe is a tribe of forest trolls who founded the Amani Empire in the northeastern side of ancient Kalimdor, with Zul'Aman as their capital. In modern times, their empire is a shadow of its former self, with most of their territory stretching only as far as the borders of Zul'Aman itself in Quel'Thalas.

**Amani'shi** — The Amani'shi is a group within the Amani. They are located mostly within Zul'Aman. Some Amani'shi can be found within the Throne of Thunder, allied to Zul and his Zandalari.

**Amani Empire** — The Amani Empire (also called the Amani Empire of Zul'Aman) is a forest troll empire located in northern Lordaeron. Established long before the Great Sundering, it took its name from the strongest tribe of forest trolls and the founders of the empire at that time, the Amani.

**Zandalari Empire** — The Zandalari Empire (or the Zandalar Empire) is one of the great troll nations, home of the Zandalari trolls, ruled by Queen Talanji. It is located on the island of Zandalar, formerly a mountain range where the first trolls settled around over 16,000 years ago.

**Empire of Zul** — The Empire of Zul was a powerful coalition of troll tribes formed during the Aqir and Troll War, around 16,000 BDP.
