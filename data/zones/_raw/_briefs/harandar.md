## Opening paragraph

Harandar is a zone introduced in World of Warcraft: Midnight. A bioluminscent primordial jungle, it is an underground cavern where the roots of all World Trees on Azeroth converge, and home to the haranir. The Rift of Aln is also located here.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Har'alnor** — Har'alnor is a haranir village in southwestern Harandar. It is the closest settlement to the roots of Shaladrassil.

**Har'mara** — Har'mara is a haranir village in northwestern Harandar. It is the closest settlement to the roots of Teldrassil, whose roots still smolder after it was burned during the Fourth War.

**Har'athir** — Har'athir is a haranir village in southeastern Harandar. It is the closest settlement to the roots of Amirdrassil.

**Har'kuai** — Har'kuai is a haranir village in northeastern Harandar. It is the closest settlement to the roots of Nordrassil. The roots of Nordrassil carry water from the Well of Eternity to Har'kuai. From Har'kuai, it then flows into the Vale of Mists and then the Rift of Aln.

**Den (Harandar)** — The Den is an area located in Harandar.

**Haranir** — The haranir are a reclusive humanoid race that resides in Harandar, a primordial jungle that lies where the roots of the World Trees converge. The haranir have been described as subterranean guardians of sacred and hidden lands.
