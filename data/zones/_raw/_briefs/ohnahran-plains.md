## Opening paragraph

The Ohn'ahran Plains (formerly known as the Emerald Plains) are a verdant grassland zone on the Dragon Isles, inhabited by the green dragonflight and the Maruuk Centaur. It is the second zone that players venture through, after the Waking Shores. The region is named for the eagle Wild God Ohn'ahra, who long ago guided Maruuk and Teera centaur to the Dragon Isles. The centaur initially warred with the green flight, but after realizing that they were evenly matched, the two races agreed to share the region, with the green dragons inhabiting the groves while the centaur dominated the plains. However, as the dragons left the isles after the Great Sundering, the centaur have been left to develop their culture in isolation for ten thousand years.

## Description

The Ohn'ahran Plains are a verdant region filled with sweeping plateaus, mesas, groves, and valleys abundant with life. Named after the Wild God of the Wind, Ohn'ahra, these plains are a viridescent grassland that rise gently upward from the Waking Shores, with mellow hills and steppes worn smooth by wind and river. This region boasts prominent and dramatic elevations that become more mountainous, and geysers are alive with erupting columns of boiling steam. Once home to the green dragonflight, nature thrives in the Ohn'ahran Plains. Magnificent, lush greenery abounds, and calm waters flow through the Emerald Gardens, an area alive with natural wonder drawn from the magic of the Emerald Dream.

Vast plateaus and mesas are home to the growing clans of centaurs, each a unique culture unto itself and masters of different specialties. The centaur clans are deeply connected to the land, maintaining their traditions, and those seeking to assist can earn their trust. This region is teeming with life. High grasses surround watering holes that draw in large wildlife that provide ample prey for the hungry proto-dragons soaring through the skies.

===Geology===
The large riverbankOhn'ahran Plains that bends above Cloverwood Hollow feeds into the Waking Shores collects sediment &mdash; soft, spongy silt washes up and buries various items in the soil. The layers of silt, buried plant matter, and chilled glacier water causes the banks to be anaerobic, preserving the items within - even those that easily decay, like fabric and wood. Due to large quantities of centaur artifacts being recovered from the area, the locals have dubbed it the "Treasury of the River".

## Places and people this zone is about

**Forkriver Crossing** — Forkriver Crossing is a small Maruuk Centaur trading village in south eastern Ohn'ahran Plains.

**Emberwatch** — Emberwatch is a Dragonscale Expedition camp within some dragon ruins located near the Sundapple Copse in northern Ohn'ahran Plains.

**Carving Winds** — The Carving Winds is located in southeastern Ohn'ahran Plains.

**Green dragonflight** — The green dragonflight (or the Emerald Dragonflight) is a draconic organization led by the eponymous green dragons to uphold their creed of preserving nature. During the ordering of Azeroth, the titan Eonar charged Ysera and her flight with the protection of the natural world and the Emerald Dream, a pristine realm of …

**Clan Aylaag** — Clan Aylaag is a Maruuk Centaur clan. Nomads, their caravan makes its way across the Ohn'aran Plains.

**Clan Ohn'ir** — Clan Ohn'ir is one of the four major Maruuk centaur clans of the Ohn'ahran Plains. It consists of mystics who feel a special connection their god, Ohn'ahra, and serve as spiritual advisors to the other clans. Their khan is Tigari Khan and their main seat of power is the Ohn'iri Springs.

**Clan Shikaar** — Clan Shikaar is one of the four major Maruuk centaur clans of the Ohn'ahran Plains. They specialize in honing their skills and hunting game across the plains, and are led by Sansok Khan.

**Clan Teerai** — Clan Teerai is one of the four major Maruuk centaur clans of the Ohn'ahran Plains. They are responsible for upholding the traditions of the ancestors and guiding the Maruuk according to their ways.

**Clan Toghus** — Clan Toghus is one of the minor Maruuk centaur clans of the Ohn'ahran Plains. It was formerly one of the four major centaur clans, before they were usurped by Clan Nokhud.

**Primalists** — The Primalists are the followers of the Primal Incarnates, ancient proto-dragons that chose to side against the titans and the Dragon Aspects. They recruit people by offering them elemental powers. The Primalists have been known to reshape the land, whether by lava or by ice, like at the Flashfrost Enclave.

**Clan Kaighan** — Clan Kaighan is one of the lesser maruuk centaur clans, located within the Ohn'ahran Plains. The clan plays relatively no role in the story, only serving as the captors of Initiate Radiya when she wanders too close to their camp.

**Clan Nokhud** — Clan Nokhud is one of the four major Maruuk centaur clans of the Ohn'ahran Plains. They are the newest clan to rise to power and maintains the largest standing military force. Led by Balakar Khan, the Nokhud have allied with the Primalists and turned against the rest of the Maruuk. Their seat of power is Nokhudon Hold.
