## Opening paragraph

Frostfire Ridge is a harsh land of ceaseless winter and volcanic peaks, where travelers encounter molten lava as often as painfully cold snow. Rock-hewn Bladespire Fortress, built to grandiose heights by the ogres who occupy its halls, is one of the few structures able to remain standing amidst this inhospitable wilderness. It's the perfect target for the survivalist orcs of the Frostwolf clan.
"Like the great wolves that roam here, we survive in this land according to its rules."
—Chieftain Durotan
Frostfire Ridge is a zone in north-western Draenor. It is home to the Frostwolf clan and the Thunderlord clan. The ogre stronghold-turned Horde capital, Bladespire Citadel is located here. When Draenor shattered and became Outland, Frostfire Ridge and Gorgrond were pressed together to become the Blade's Edge Mountains. Several recognizable landmarks in Blade's Edge Mountains can be found in Frostfire, such as the Circle of Blood and the rock spires jutting from the earth. The Blade's Edge Mountain, the namesake of the future mountain range that Frostfire would become, can be found near Bladespire Citadel.
Frostfire Ridge is the starting zone for Horde characters in Draenor. Its Alliance counterpart is Shadowmoon Valley.

## History

In the past, the Gorian Empire ruled over Frostfire Ridge, Bladespire Citadel serving as far-flung outpost. During those times, when the orcs were still enslaved by the ogres, the legendary Thunderlord hero Brakor wielded the mighty spear Gronnsbane.

## Geography

Frostfire Ridge is an area of extremes - snow and ice-covered glaciers in one area, active volcanoes spewing lava in another. The region is defined by the volcanic activity of late, so be wary of gigantic cliffs in otherwise normal plains.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Frostwall** — Frostwall is the Horde Garrison, located in Frostfire Ridge. A fully-upgraded Frostwall garrison is considered to be a fortress.

**Grom'gar** — Grom'gar is the ancestral home of the Thunderlord orc clan located in Frostfire Ridge. According to legend, Grom'gar was built in the skeletal remains of the last colossal, which was supposedly killed by the Thunderlords many generations ago.

**Iron Siegeworks** — The Iron Siegeworks is the Iron Horde's main siege weapons complex in Frostfire Ridge, and the origin of most of the weapons that threaten the Frostwolves there.

**Bloodmaul Slag Mines** — Bloodmaul Slag Mines (or the Bloodmaul Slave Mines) is a dungeon in Warlords of Draenor. Set in northern Frostfire Ridge, the dungeon features 4 bosses in a non-linear layout.

**Bloodmaul Stronghold** — The Bloodmaul Stronghold is a stronghold of the ogre Bloodmaul clan located at the Stonefury Cliffs of Frostfire Ridge.

**Darkspear's Edge** — Darkspear's Edge is a Horde camp in Frostfire Ridge. The forces stationed here watch over the Cracking Plains, Colossal's Fall and Iceblister Den for threats.

**Colossal's Fall** — Colossal's Fall is a Thunderlord settlement in northern Frostfire Ridge, north of the Cracking Plains. Much like the capital, Grom'gar, Colossal's Fall is built in the corpse of an earth giant.

**Agurak's Fall** — Agurak's Fall is a subzone consisting of a colossal skeleton in southeastern Frostfire Ridge. It is accessible from the Bones of Agurak.

**Grimfrost Hill** — Grimfrost Hill is a Grimfrost ogre village in eastern Frostfire Ridge. From here these ogres worship a creature at the Pit of the Devourer and have been capturing frostwolves from the Frostwolf clan.

**Bladespire Citadel** — Bladespire Citadel (or Bladespire Fortress) is the Bladespire clan capital located in Frostfire Ridge. Later, the Frostwolf Orcs aided by commanders and Thrall are able to conquer the citadel and drive the ogres out. It is built into Blade's Edge Mountain.

**Frostwolf Orcs** — The Frostwolf Orcs, or Frostwolf clan, are renowned for their prowess as one-on-one combatants. Once led by Chieftain Durotan in alternate Draenor, they are a strong, close-knit clan whose bond with wolves only adds to their strength—though their numbers remain smaller than most clans.

**Breakers** — The Breakers are a group of various species of stone giants and other rock-like beings native to Draenor who intend to transform the entire planet into a desolate landscape. They are the children of Grond, the great giant crafted by the titan Aggramar to battle the Evergrowth.
