## Opening paragraph

The Azure Span is a zone of the Dragon Isles covered in snowy peaks, mist-covered forests, and icy tundra. It stretches from coastal tuskarr villages through sprawling forest tundra to looming spires of ice and snow. Regal blue dragons once roamed these lands, but with the passage of time, several tribes of tuskarr, gnolls, and furbolg now call these ancient lands their home. It is the location of the Azure Archives. Much of the zone is filled with varied biomes covered in snow and ice, giant frozen waterfalls, ice rivers, and forests shrouded in mist.

## History

Expand on the questlines' stories and information.
Before the War of the Ancients, the region was home to the blue dragonflight. When the blue dragons departed, the Brackenhide gnolls settled in the Azure Span, specifically in areas rich with ley crystals, claiming them for their own. However, they failed to recognize the crystals' true potential.

Following the return of the dragons to the Dragon Isles during the war against the Primalists, the Kirin Tor arrived to study the region's magical energies. As in other regions, the Primalists launched an invasion of the Azure Span, attacking Camp Antonidas. Meanwhile, the Dragonscale Expedition established outposts in the southern areas. Adventurers from the Alliance and Horde assisted the Kirin Tor and Dragonscale Expedition in their struggles against the Primalists and local threats. They also played a crucial role in aiding the Iskaara tuskarr, who were battling the decay-corrupted Brackenhide gnolls and their minions.

The Brackenhide gnolls were gradually defeated across the region. Brena called upon adventurers to venture into the heart of Brackenhide Hollow and confront Decatriarch Wratheye, but Wratheye managed to escape. Kalecgos returned to his ancestral home and formed a bond with the tuskarr. He collaborated with the simulacrum of Sindragosa to restore the Azure Oathstone and to reunify the blue dragonflight. Additionally, Sonova Snowden enlisted heroes to help the Winterpelt tribe.

As the conflict escalated, Zaqali djaradin and their Primalist allies set up Suffusion Camp: Frostburn at the center of the Upper Frostlands. However, they were repelled by the local inhabitants and their allies.

Near the conclusion of the war, members of the Azerothian Archives began their excavation at the Traitor's Rest digsite.

## Geography

The Azure Span is a sprawling, mountainous zone in the southern Dragon Isles defined by its arctic climate, towering forests, frozen lakes, and ancient arcane ruins. The landscape transitions from dense woodlands and rolling, snowy hills in the south and west to rugged ice-covered cliffs and glacial plateaus in the center, north, and east. The region contains arcane leyline nexuses.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Azure Archives** — The Azure Archives  are the personal library of Sindragosa, prime consort of the blue dragonflight, located in the Azure Span in the Dragon Isles. It is where the Azure Oathstone is located, as well as the entrance to the Azure Vault.

**Brackenhide Hollow** — Brackenhide Hollow is a dungeon in Dragonflight.

**Cobalt Assembly** — The Cobalt Assembly  is the home of the Cobalt Assembly faction of blue dragons in north-central Azure Span. The area has been taken over by the Sundered Flame, and the blues of the area are asking for adventurers to help restore their cache of arcana.

**Iskaara** — Iskaara  is the home of the Iskaara Tuskarr in southwest Azure Span. The Filled Bowl serves as the inn of the village. The Community Feast event occurs here.

**Big Tree Hills** — Big Tree Hills is an area located in the Azure Span.

**Brackenhide Waterhole** — Brackenhide Waterhole is an area located in the Azure Span.

**Camp Antonidas** — Camp Antonidas  is a Kirin Tor camp in central Azure Span. Mage's Rest serves as the inn of the camp. It is named in honor of the former Grand Magus of the Kirin Tor, Antonidas.

**Camp Nowhere** — Camp Nowhere  is a Dragonscale Expedition camp in southeastern Azure Span. Dealing with the fallout of Imbu to the southwest and the Rustpine Den to the north, the expedition is also interested in exploring the Ruins of Karnthar.

**Darktooth Pond** — Darktooth Pond is an area located in the Azure Span.

**Gorloc Shore** — The Gorloc Shore is an area located in the Azure Span. It is the home of the Brinetooth gorlocs.

**Imbu** — Imbu is a tuskarr town located in southeastern Azure Span, southwest of Camp Nowhere. The town was attacked by the Primalists and renegade furbolg, and now the tuskarr of the Imbu tribe are fighting to take it back.

**Kauriq Gleamlet** — The Kauriq Gleamlet is a large fishing camp belonging to the Kauriq tribe of tuskarr living in the Azure Span.
