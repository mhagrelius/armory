## Opening paragraph

Zaralek Cavern is an enormous cave system underneath the Dragon Isles. It is accessed via a large cave between the Ohn'ahran Plains and Valdrakken.
Deep beneath the Obsidian Citadel from which Neltharion once defended the Dragon Isles lies a vast hidden laboratory where the Earth-Warder conducted experiments... forbidden practices he wanted to conceal from the other Aspects. Within these halls lies the shadow chamber from which Neltharion first communed with the Old Gods, walking a path that would one day lead to his transformation into Deathwing.
While Raszageth has been defeated, the Primalists carry on in their bid to unravel all the titans have wrought and must forge new alliances–or rekindle old ones. As the heroes of Azeroth pursue enemies into the dangers of Zaralek Cavern, they are aided by the local inhabitants found under the Dragon Isles — the molefolk known as Niffen and the Drogbar. They may not know much of the surface wars, but their knowledge of the underground is key to players and their allies finding their footing within.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Loamm** — Loamm  is the main town of the niffen in Zaralek Cavern.

**Glimmerogg** — Glimmerogg  is the home of the friendly drogbar of Zaralek Cavern in the southwestern corner of the zone. Led by Brogg the Beast, the drogbar are all about lifting and snail racing, hosting the Glimmerogg Games.

**Nal ks'kol** — Nal ks'kol  is a titan facility in southeastern Zaralek Cavern. Players visit the facility for the duration of the second chapter of the Embers of Neltharion campaign, "Sundered Legacy", starting with  [70] Future Aspects.

**Obsidian Rest** — Obsidian Rest  is the forward post of the Valdrakken Accord and Wrathion's Blacktalon on the way to Aberrus in northern Zaralek Cavern.

**Dragonscale Camp** — Dragonscale Camp  is, as suggested by the name, a small Dragonscale Expedition camp in southeastern Zaralek Cavern.

**Loamm Niffen** — Loamm Niffen is the fifth Major Faction of World of Warcraft: Dragonflight.

**Primalists** — The Primalists are the followers of the Primal Incarnates, ancient proto-dragons that chose to side against the titans and the Dragon Aspects. They recruit people by offering them elemental powers. The Primalists have been known to reshape the land, whether by lava or by ice, like at the Flashfrost Enclave.

**Dragonscale Expedition** — The Dragonscale Expedition is the name of the joint Alliance and Horde expedition to the Dragon Isles. Explicitly not a military expedition, it set up outposts throughout the Isles as it continued to explore them. Despite their shared mission, rivalries between the Explorers' League and Reliquary endured.

**Valdrakken Accord** — The Valdrakken Accord is a draconic organization led by the Dragon Aspects to represent the reunification of the dragonkin upon their home of the Dragon Isles. **Blacktalon** — The Blacktalon (also spelled Black Talon) are Wrathion's agents. Though they may seem small in numbers, they are well trained, and prepared to die for him if necessary.

**Obsidian Brood** — The Obsidian Brood, also known as the Outland brood, refers to the group of black dragons that were shepherded and cared for by Sabellian in Outland. Having been originally placed on Draenor by Deathwing, the clutch was abandoned after the mad aspect's plans went awry and the planet later exploded.

**Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible** — Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible is the second raid in Dragonflight, added in patch 10.1.0. Hidden beneath the Obsidian Citadel, it is a massive laboratory in Zaralek Cavern where Neltharion's most horrid creations were made, including the dracthyr.
