## Opening paragraph

Eredath, called the Jewel of Argus, was one of the most illustrious cities of Argus, the pinnacle of eredar civilization, and the most sacred of all cities. Following the destruction that befell Argus, the city of Eredath now floats above the planet, in a relatively pristine state.
Eredath serves as one of three zones on Argus and hosts the Seat of the Triumvirate dungeon.

## Geography

The city core of Eredath is a floating island above the rest of the planet Argus. Eredath is dominated by the Seat of the Triumvirate to the northwest, near a huge rock wall caused by the deformation of the planet's surface. Much of the city is inaccessible to players but can be seen in the sky, floating at an angle.

## Notes and trivia

thumb|The glow of the Xenedar near the rift.
- When jumping out of the edge of the map, the player enters the "Twisting Nether" subzone shortly before dying, similar to how leaving an Outland zone does.
- Eredath can be seen in the sky from Krokuun. Likewise, the glow of the Xenedar in Krokuun can be seen on the surface from Eredath.
- This zone was originally named Mac'Aree. The name first appeared during The Burning Crusade with the character Jessera of Mac'Aree (now Maatparm), referencing Blizzard Lead Level Designer Jesse McCree. Both the zone and the character were renamed and retconned in patch 9.1.5 after McCree was fired from Blizzard.
- At the time, quest designer Jennifer Klasing, disliked the fact that the city was indirectly named after a developer and therefore asked the voice actors to pronounce the name as "Muh-KAH-Ree".

## Places and people this zone is about

**Seat of the Triumvirate** — The Seat of the Triumvirate is a long-abandoned building in the center of Eredath that was once the center of eredar ruling power. It has been overtaken by the presence of the Void and its Shadowguard, seeking the darkened naaru L'ura. It was a site of miracles and marvels.

**Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.

**Shadowguard** — The Shadowguard are a faction of ethereals who have heeded the call of the Void and become void ethereals. They serve as Nexus-King Salhadaar's military division.

**Argussian Reach** — The Argussian Reach, also known as the Krokul or the Darkfallen, is a faction associated to the Broken of Argus based in Krokul Hovel.

**Army of the Light** — The Grand Army of the Light (also called the Golden Army, the Army of Light, the Light's Army, or the Lightforged) is a faction of enlightened races led by the naaru who rose against the Burning Legion.

**Vindicaar** — The Vindicaar (pronounced VIN-di-kar) is a space-faring vessel, similar in design to the naaru dimensional ships, created by the draenei of the Exodar in order to return to their homeworld of Argus.

**Lightforged beacon** — Lightforged beacons are teleportation devices used by the Army of the Light on Argus. After the Argus Campaign, the beacons have become a part of the Alliance.

**Conservatory of the Arcane** — The Conservatory of the Arcane is a location in Eredath. It was the greatest school of magic on Argus. The Augari, Consulate, and Wakeners orders were based here and led by Archimonde, who was himself an alumnus.

**Prophet's Reflection** — Prophet's Reflection is a location in Eredath.

**Shadowguard Incursion** — Shadowguard Incursion is a location in Eredath.

**City Center** — The City Center is an area of Eredath located near Azurelight Square and the Ruins of Oronaar.  It is a travel hub, consisting of a small draenei structure hosting a Lightforged Beacon defended by Lightforged Bulwarks.

**Krokuun** — Krokuun is a region of Argus where the armies of Azeroth first landed during their quest to stop the Burning Legion. Kil'jaeden's Legion ship has crashed here near the Xenedar.
