## Opening paragraph

Siren Isle, known as Cyrandall to the Vrykul, is a storm-wracked island. During the Fourth War, it was used by Kul Tiras to mine Azerite, but since then its inhabitants have mysteriously disappeared.
The goblins, Arathi, and earthen have all ventured there, drawn by a mysterious whispering crystal. The Bloodwake vrykul, Darktide naga, and Bilge Rats pirates vie for control of the isle.

## History

Around twenty thousand years ago, wishing to avoid the Winterskorn War, the iron vrykul Cyrce guided the Sturmvyn clan to settle in the area they called the "Cradle of Hymosul".

After the Great Sundering in 10,000 BDP, the naga attacked and overwhelmed the clan, until Cyrce ordered them to retreat to their Vault.

===The War Within===

In Year 42 ADP, a goblin salvage team discovered the isle and the strange "singing crystal" at its center, leading the Hallowfall Arathi, Algari earthen, and Bilgewater's Greasemonkeys to form an expedition to investigate the mystery of the Siren Isle.

Among the Arathi, the Sacredites were amazed to discover that the crystal, Lorandel as they named it, was just like Beledar in Hallowfall.

## Notes

- Siren Isle uses an unreleased Island Expedition map, named Abandoned Mines, from Battle for Azeroth. Its backstory as a Kul Tiran Azerite mine from the Fourth War may be a reference to this fact.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Forgotten Vault (Siren Isle)** — The Forgotten Vault is accessed by interacting with the Singing Tablet at  in the Sacred Hollow of Siren Isle.

**Flotsam Shoal** — Flotsam Shoal is the makeshift base of operations for the expedition from Khaz Algar consisting of the Hallowfall Arathi, Dornogal earthen, and Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys found in the eastern part of Siren Isle.

**Dawn** — Dawn was the first earthen to awaken from the Hall of Awakening after it was restored by Magni Bronzebeard. She now serves as a guide for newly awakened earthen, alongside Foreman Uzjax, helping them navigate the process of awakening and preparing them for the outside world.

**Bilgewater Cartel** — The Bilgewater Cartel is one of the largest and most powerful goblin cartels found on Azeroth, run by the shrewd Trade Prince Monte Gazlowe. From their headquarters on Kezan at Bilgewater Port, the cartel dominated the trade all over the South Seas under the leadership of Jastor Gallywix, until it was destroyed due to …

**Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys** — Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys are a Horde team that can be encountered on Island Expeditions as well as a group that goes to Mechagon Island. They can also be found idling in the Port of Zandalar along with the other teams.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Hallowfall Arathi** — The Hallowfall Arathi are the third Major Faction of The War Within, based out of Mereldar in Hallowfall. They represent the Hallowfall Expedition of the Arathi Empire.

**Dornogal (nation)** — Dornogal is a nation of earthen located on the continent of Khaz Algar. Under the leadership of the Council of Dornogal, the Algari earthen society has been isolated for thousands of years, gradually developing their own behaviors, language, and demeanor with many similarities to other former earthen groups.

**Bilge Rats** — The Bilge Rats are an unconventional pirate crew of "mongrel" races based out of Freehold in Tiragarde Sound. They have a bar called Stranded Rats.

**Bloodwake** — The Bloodwake are a clan of vrykul raiders located on different Island Expeditions: The Dread Chain, Havenswood, Jorundall, Molten Cay, The Rotting Mire, Skittering Hollow, Un'gol Ruins, Whispering Reef and Verdant Wilds.

**Darktide** — The Darktide are naga attacking seaside settlements in Kul Tiras, in order to amass prisoners to sacrifice to their dark master.

**Kul Tiras (kingdom)** — Kul Tiras (/kuːlˈtɪrəs/ KOOL-TEER-əs), officially the Admiralty of Kul Tiras (also spelled Kul'Tiras) is a maritime human kingdom based on the archipelago of the same name.
