## Opening paragraph

A true enigma, the Timeless Isle has drifted in and out of Pandaria's mists for hundreds of years. Here, time has no meaning, and the sun neither rises nor sets. Its unique characteristics have brought the Isle under the scrutiny of the bronze dragonflight, pandaren explorers, and the black dragon Wrathion.
The Timeless Isle is a level 30-35 PvE zone located to the east of the Jade Forest. Seek the Isle alone or in a group to collect Timeless Coins (which will allow players to purchase unique gear from the locals) or to do battle with massive creatures and dangerous world bosses. Among the latter are Ordos, the lost Yaungol fire god; and the four August Celestials, who stand ready to test the mettle of adventurers taking part in Wrathion's legendary quest.
While there are quest-givers on the Timeless Isle, they are not there to give specific directions; they encourage players to simply throw themselves into the experience, collecting coins and reacting to the dynamic events, bosses, and puzzles that emerge from the depths of the isle’s unexplored interior...

## History

thumb|Celestial Court and Ordon Sanctuary

The Timeless Isle, so named because it exists in a state of perpetual sunset, was once a place of great reverence for the pandaren. It was here that trials of wisdom, hope, strength, and fortitude took place in front of the great Celestials... where warriors and aspiring leaders would show their willingness to walk in the footsteps of the great Emperor, Shaohao. As a testament to their goal of achieving balance in all things, the pandaren monks even existed in harmony with the nearby Yaungol, worshippers of the fiery demigod Ordos.

But one day, the island vanished. Some scrolls indicate that the Timeless Isle has come and gone throughout history—sometimes just a glimmer in the distance, often appearing in different locations. Some less fortunate adventurers dared step foot on the island during these moments and were just as quickly lost to the Timeways, never to be seen again.

Now—and perhaps only for a moment—the island has returned.

After the awakening of the Dragon Isles, Li Li Stormstout reported that sometimes the Isle cannot be reached. Luckily however, it was accessible when she, Chen Stormstout, and Catelyn the Blade went to visit it. Catelym immediately noticed the strange behavior of the sun.

## Geography

The Isle is lushly forested, with flora similar to that found in the Jade Forest. The waters of the Ruby Lake are colored a strange reddish hue, possibly due to the ruby crystals found deep within the mountains of the Timeless Isle. Yaungol control the mountainous regions of the island, led by their "fire god" Ordos, while the lowlands are mostly abandoned save for the Celestial Court where many residents of Pandaria have gathered to watch the August Celestial's trials. The Huojin and Tushui pandaren have made landfall and established camps at the western shore, but have been asked by the Celestials to not press further inland.

## Notes and trivia

- Li Li Stormstout describes it as the home and playground of gods.
- Despite the Timeless Isle being frozen in a perpetual sunset in lore, Inky Black Potion will still work and turn the area into night.
- On an island off the coast of Gorgrond are four chairs and a campfire. If one of the chairs is sat in and the fire clicked, it will teleport the user to a cave on the coast of the Timeless Isle. One can use the fire there to teleport back as well.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Celestial Court** — The Celestial Court  is the home of the Celestial challenges. Located in the center of the Timeless Isle, the courtyard is surrounded clockwise from the north by the Three-Breeze Terrace, the Cavern of Lost Spirits, Red Stone Run, Mossgreen Lake, Old Pi'jiu, Tushui Landing, Timeless Shore, Huojin Landing, and the Misty…

**Old Pi'jiu** — Old Pi'jiu  is a ruined pandaren fishing town on the southwestern part of Timeless Isle that used to have brewery. To the north is the Celestial Court, the Tushui Landing is to the northwest, and eastward is the Firewalkers' Path leading to the Firewalker Ruins and points northward.

**Huojin Landing** — Huojin Landing  is the Horde's beachhead on the Timeless Isle, maintained by the Huojin Pandaren. Situated at the northern end of the Timeless Shore, the Misty Strand is found immediately to the northeast, the Celestial Court is inland to the southeast, and the Tushui Landing is found at the southern end of the shore t…

**Tushui Landing** — Tushui Landing  is the Alliance beachhead of Timeless Isle. Located on the southern end of the Timeless Shore, the landing is northwest of Old Pi'jiu, southwest of the Celestial Court, and due south of the Huojin Landing, the Horde's beachhead.

**Ordon Sanctuary** — The Ordon Sanctuary  is the home of Ordos, the yaungol demigod of fire. Found on a rise high above the rest of the Timeless Isle, the sanctuary was originally accessed by the last bridge span of the Firewalkers' Path, but the bridge has been destroyed. 

**Ordon** — The Ordon are a large warlike tribe of yaungol found on the Timeless Isle. While the yaungol as a race are usually associated with fire, the Ordon base their entire culture around it. The Ordon worship and are led by Ordos, a yaungol shaman who sacrificed himself to the flame to become a demigod.

**August Celestials (faction)** — The August Celestials are four Wild Gods who defend the temples of Pandaria from the evils of the sha and mogu. This faction includes the four celestials and their respective servants.

**Emperor Shaohao (faction)** — Emperor Shaohao is a reputation earned by helping Emperor Shaohao. His spirit now oversees the war against the Ordon yaungol, chaotic barbarians who threaten Pandaria while the bulk of the Alliance and Horde participate in the Siege of Orgrimmar.

**Cavern of Lost Spirits** — The Cavern of Lost Spirits  lies beneath the Ordon Sanctuary northeast of the Celestial Court near the center of the Timeless Isle. Zarhym can frequently be found inside and starts the "Zarhym Altogether" event, a timed, phased loot-grabbing run.

**Croaking Hollow** — The Croaking Hollow  is the southeastern beach of Timeless Isle. Bordered by the Red Stone Run to the north, and the Firewalker Ruins and the Firewalkers' Path high above on the cliffs, the hollow is home to poisonous frogs! The frogs also guard one of the four Sturdy Chests on the isle.

**Firewalker Ruins** — The Firewalker Ruins  are on the first rise of the Firewalkers' Path on the southeastern side of Timeless Isle. The ruins are above the Red Stone Run to the north and the Croaking Hollow to the east.
