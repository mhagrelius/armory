## Opening paragraph

This land of cloud-ringed mountains and hidden bamboo thickets runs along Pandaria's eastern shore. Horde and Alliance forces have spread out into this extraordinary locale, hoping to gain support from the ferocious hozen and industrious jinyu races that dwell throughout the Jade Forest.
The Jade Forest is a zone located in eastern Pandaria, bordering the Valley of the Four Winds at the southwest and Kun-Lai Summit at the northwest. The heart of the Pandaren Empire, it is a lush rainforest with statuesque stone spires, prominent with the pandaren architecture. The Temple of the Jade Serpent is located in the eastern portion of the zone, and it is the very first dungeon adventurers will encounter when first arriving in Pandaria.

## Geography

The landscape of the Jade Forest is verdant and lush. High spires dot the region and its coastal areas, and the flora is diverse in several areas of the land. Pandaren buildings, such as temples, monasteries, farmlands are prominently gracing the land and fascinate its curious visitors that have managed to get past the mists shrouding the continent. In some places, ancient mogu ruins can be found - one example are the Terrace of Ten Thunders in the north, yet travelers must exercise caution, as the reawakened servants of the Thunder King have reawakened and now plot the downfall of Pandaren empire. Since the arrival of two warring factions of Azeroth, they have gained a foothold in old mogu structures in the northern and southern reaches of the forest, as well as gained support pandaren villages. Paw'don Village has sided with the Alliance, while Honeydew Village has sided with the Horde. In the east, there is a temple and a monument dedicated to the Eastern August Celestial Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent, but due to the release of negative energies the temple became overrun by the Sha of Doubt and his spawns, and the monument destroyed.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Dawn's Blossom** — Dawn's Blossom is a small town and the heart of pandaren life in the Jade Forest. It is an important stop for travelers and has not taken a side in the Alliance / Horde conflict.

**Honeydew Village** — Honeydew Village is a pandaren village in northwestern Jade Forest. The village is made up of brewers and farmers, run by the Honeydews, however, the real heart of the village is the brewery, which is run by the Honeypaws. They make the best Honeybrew anywhere.

**Paw'don Village** — Paw'don Village is a pandaren village just north of Garrosh'ar Point, previously led by Mayor Sunke Khang. The village is divided into two parts, connected by a bridge.

**Greenstone Village** — Greenstone Village is a pandaren village found in east-central Jade Forest between Dawn's Blossom, the Arboretum, and the Emperor's Omen.

**Garrosh'ar Point** — Garrosh'ar Point was a Horde landing site in the Jade Forest led by Ga'trul. The outpost came under heavy assault from Alliance military led by the mighty airship The Skyfire when on a mission to search for Admiral Taylor and Anduin Wrynn.

**Grookin Hill** — Grookin Hill is a hozen village found just west of Tigers' Wood, past the Slicky Stream in western Jade Forest. This is the main village of the Forest Hozen, where the big chief resides.

**Camp Nooka Nooka** — Camp Nooka Nooka is a hozen village in northwest Jade Forest, right next to Lake Kittitata. The small village is led by Tooki Tooki, under the great Chief Kah Kah.

**Order of the Cloud Serpent** — The Order of the Cloud Serpent is an order of serpent riders sworn to defend the Jade Forest and the Temple of the Jade Serpent, dedicated to the August Celestial Yu'lon.

**Mogu empire** — The mogu empire (or Mogu Dynasty) possessed immense size and strength. The mogu built it based on might, in which the weak — other races — were subjugated, and the strong — always mogu — ascended to power.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Forest Hozen** — The Grookin tribe, also known as the Forest Hozen, are a group of hozen living in the Jade Forest. They find themselves allied with the Horde after the Horde gains their favor by rescuing some of their members, sating their appetite for fish, and killing some of their most fearsome enemies.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.
