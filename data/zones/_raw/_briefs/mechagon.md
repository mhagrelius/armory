## Opening paragraph

Mechagon Island (displayed as just Mechagon in the overworld) is an island located northwest of Kul Tiras. Added in patch 8.2.0, it features one zone, a megadungeon (Operation: Mechagon), and an arena (the Robodrome).

## History

===Ancient times===
In ancient times, Mimiron built a vault here in which he kept technological and titan marvels.

Before King Mechagon claimed the island, other civilizations lived upon it. The history of these forgotten people was buried beneath the land. Evidence of two different civilizations appear, with one having been vrykul in origin and the other appearing to have been tortollans. In addition, the discovery of a giant clockwork hammer, too big for any mechagnome to wield, possibly indicates the existence of an earlier mecha-civilization on the island. A very old night elven ring has also been found on Mechagon, raising more questions about the island's past. It seems pirates were also active around the area.

===Foundation of Mechagon===
King Mechagon was once the king of the Gnomeregan gnomes, who 400 years ago led his followers in search of a mystical land of technology. He left no clues where he was headed and over time his tale became a legend to the gnomes. In truth, King Mechagon managed to find this land and claimed it as his own. Since then, Mechagon Island has become the lost home of the mechagnomes. Under the guidance of Walton Cogfrenzy, the gnomes erected the mighty underground marvel of Mechagon City. Under King Mechagon, the gnomes began to convert themselves into partially mechanical versions of themselves; thus removing the problems of age upon their society.

===Rediscovery===

Centuries later, King Mechagon decided that the time had come to remove the Curse of Flesh from all titan creations. He was busy creating the Mechoriginator, a machine he'd been perfecting in order to achieve his goal. Not wanting to see his father's plan come to fruition, Prince Erazmin began to secretly plan a revolt against him. His plans were unfortunately discovered when the elderly gnome adventurer, Kervo Cogpipe,  single-handedly found his way to the legendary lost island and told the king of his betrayal. With no other choice, Prince Erazmin fled to Rustbolt with all those who supported him, leading to a civil war between the Rustbolt Resistance and the Arcforged loyalists.

===Battle for Azeroth===

Ignorant of this conflict, forces of the Alliance and Horde had heard of a vault being discovered at the Abandoned Junkheap. An expedition under Tinkmaster Overspark were the first to arrive and were able to open the vault. Inside, they managed to activate a message by King Mechagon, which unfortunately was followed by the activation of a scanning device that killed all those who were not gnomes. It only stopped when Gila Crosswires was scanned.

As the Alliance forces left the vault, a group led by Gazlowe had learned of the vault as well and had just arrived as they were leaving. With Overspark's departure, the Horde invaded the vault and killed all except for one gnome. Just as the Alliance did, the Horde activated the message and suffered the same fate until Gazlowe got the idea of using the captured gnome to stop the sequence.

Arriving on the island, Overspark and Gazlowe were greeted by Prince Erazmin who explained the situation. Both sides agreed that despite being at war, they would help the rebellion. With everything settled, Erazmin gave a tour of the island. He explained that Rustbolt was recently ransacked and he will need help to launch an assault to rescue his supporters. With help from both sides, the prince attacked Junkwatt Depot to free his captured allies and also regain Pascal-K1N6.

Before leaving, the prince decided to deliver a message to his father by damaging the HK-8 Aerial Oppression Unit. Arriving back at Rustbolt, he informed both forces that there was much to be done before advancing onto the next phase. When preparations were complete, the allied forces launched Operation: Mechagon, leading to the tyrant King Mechagon's end.

====Alternate universe====
thumb|Mechagon Island's alternate universe.
In an alternate universe, just as everything seemed to go well for the rebellion, Bondo Bigblock sold them out. In exchange for full mechanization, Bondo built giant bots and attacked Rustbolt. Soon after the successful invasion, a hologram of King Mechagon appeared in the middle of the base and declared victory. Following his triumph, he activated the Mechoriginator and destroyed all life on Azeroth. In the aftermath, Christy Punchcog and Izira Gearsworn were some of the few who survived. Izira's legs were destroyed, so Christy with the help of an adventurer was able to create a new set of legs for her.

After getting back on her feet, Izira decided to go after Bondo Bigblock as revenge and killed him. On her journey, she found her old prince Erazmin, transforming into nothing more than a rusty bot. Putting him to rest gave Izira an idea. Inside a Armored Vaultbot nearby, there was a Visual Holo Sphere, which held secrets to defeat King Mechagon. She asked an adventurer to retrieve it and send it to her main timeline self.

## Description

A mystery awaits that is of particular interest to the gnomes and goblins. A previously closed vault in Kul Tiras has opened and within it a radio transmitter sends a signal for help and points to a new land to explore, Mechagon. This is an ancient lost city of the gnomes. The beginning of the adventure is within the Junker Wastes where heroes will be beset by death robots, but there are potential allies nearby. Hunted by robots themselves, the mechagnomes introduce you to a whole new society and take you to where higher cast gnomes live who have dedicated themselves to their king and have embraced robotics.

King Mechagon has a vision to return anyone of flesh into more pure robotic parts and it's up to you to put a stop to him.

## Geography

Mechagon is a small island located northwest of Kul Tiras and has a similar landscape and fauna as Stormsong Valley. Rustbolt, home to the Rustbolt Resistance, is located on the northeast of the island while the entrance to Mechagon City, the Junkwatt Depot, is found in the southwestern hills. North lies a small mountainous area where the Scrapbone troggs make their home within Scrapbone Den. Southwest lies a small cluster of islands known as the Toothy Shallows.

## Notes and trivia

- Mechagon Island shares some of its fauna with Stormsong Valley, such as the Brinebeak Albatrosses and the Coral Snapper crabs.
- Ionized Minnows scourge the waters around the island. They strip the ocean floors of any food, making it difficult for other species to survive.
- Ion Hazzikostas has described Mechagon as "what you might expect from a gnomish paradise".
- Someone or something from Mechagon is called "Mechagonian".
- Snooters were first discovered on Mechagon. They appear to be pets of the Mechagnomes, as the Golden Snorf was King Mechagon's pet.
- As a zone, Mechagon is slightly bigger than the Timeless Isle and smaller than Nazjatar.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Junkwatt Depot** — The Junkwatt Depot is located on Mechagon Island. It is the fortified workyard that surrounds the gates of the Robodrome and Mechagon City proper.

**Overspark Expedition Camp** — The Overspark Expedition Camp is located on Mechagon Island.

**Greasy Eel** — The Greasy Eel is a goblin submarine captained by Captain Krooz. Thanks to Ricket, it came to Dazar'alor and was used by Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys to get to Mechagon Island.

**Mechagon City** — Mechagon City (commonly referred to as the lost city of Mechagon) is an old gnomish city located on a formerly unexplored island in the Great Sea called Mechagon Island. The Robodrome is located in the entrance to Mechagon.

**Arcforged** — The Arcforged were the mechagnomes that followed King Mechagon and his ideas of "reversing" the Curse of Flesh.

**Gnomeregan (faction)** — Gnomeregan (formerly known as Gnomeregan Exiles) is the faction associated with the playable gnome of the Alliance. They have recently their capital of Gnomeregan in western Dun Morogh.

**Bilgewater Cartel** — The Bilgewater Cartel is one of the largest and most powerful goblin cartels found on Azeroth, run by the shrewd Trade Prince Monte Gazlowe. From their headquarters on Kezan at Bilgewater Port, the cartel dominated the trade all over the South Seas under the leadership of Jastor Gallywix, until it was destroyed due to …

**Grumbol's Hammers** — Grumbol's Hammers are an Alliance group led by Grumbol Grimhammer. They can be found on Mechagon Island.

**Explorers' League** — The Explorers' League, also known as Explorers' Guild, Explorer's League or simply the League, is an organization dedicated to researching the origin of the dwarven race. They have many archaeological dig-sites in all corners of Azeroth, but also on Outland and alternate Draenor.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.

**Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys** — Gazlowe's Greasemonkeys are a Horde team that can be encountered on Island Expeditions as well as a group that goes to Mechagon Island. They can also be found idling in the Port of Zandalar along with the other teams.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.
