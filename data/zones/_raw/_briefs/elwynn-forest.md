## Opening paragraph

Elwynn Forest, the Forest of Elwynn or simply Elwynn, is a vast woodland of stately hardwoods nestled in the continent of Azeroth, just below the foothills of the Burning Steppes to the north. The Redridge Mountains lie to the east, and Duskwood lies to the south across the Nazferiti river. It is the heartland of the human Kingdom of kingdom of Stormwind and consequently is the starting point of all human adventurers.
Marshal Dughan serves as the municipal law enforcer for Goldshire and Marshal McBride serves as the municipal law enforcer for Northshire Valley. Though much of the forest was burned down during the First War, trees and lush glades have grown anew. Stormwind City lies northwest of the forest and is heavily patrolled by the Stormwind guards.

## History

===Founding of Stormwind===
thumb|Stormwind City, capital of the realm.
Circa 1,200 BDP, Stormwind was founded by Faldir and the descendants of the Arathi bloodline, who had migrated south from the Empire of Arathor in Lordaeron. Traveling past the rocky mountains of Khaz Modan, their journey finally ended after many long seasons, and they settled in the northern region of the continent they would name Azeroth. In a fertile valley, they founded the kingdom of Stormwind, which quickly became a self-sufficient power in its own right. Over the years, it found prosperity through the farmsteads that dotted the surrounding fertile region, and as its population grew, small towns sprang up in the nearby regions, notably in Elwynn Forest, and ever since the humans settled their capital of Stormwind City in the forest, it has thrived under their stewardship.

During the Gnoll War in 75 BDP, the village of Goldshire was attacked by the Redridge gnolls, who were repelled by the Stormwind Army.

===Great Wars and aftermath===
During the First War in 3 ADP, after Brightwood, Westfall, and the Redridge Mountains fell, thousands of orcs marched through Elwynn Forest and encircled Stormwind City, cutting off all access except for the sea. When Kilrogg Deadeye and Cho'gall led their Bleeding Hollow and Twilight's Hammer clans in the assault of the human capital, Knight Champion Anduin Lothar led the Knights of Stormwind in a charge through Elwynn Forest, catching the orcs in the rearguard completely by surprise. Outflanked on both sides and suffering many casualties, the orcs were left with no choice but to flee. In Year 4 ADP, Goldshire and Moonbrook were the final two towns to fall to the Orcish Horde, as Warchief Blackhand sought to sever the lifeline between King Llane Wrynn I and his people in preparation of the sack of Stormwind Keep. The second siege of Stormwind ultimately ended with the capital reduced to ruins, and Lothar taking on the mantle of regent lord to guide the refugees to Lordaeron aboard thousands of boats. The ruins of Stormwind City were then lorded over by the orcs of the Stormreaver clan.

After the Second War ended in 6 ADP, special importance was placed on rebuilding Stormwind, with Supreme Commander Turalyon overseeing the repatriation of the citizens in Elwynn Forest. Archbishop Alonsus Faol of the Church of the Light notably aided in rebuilding Northshire Abbey. To this day, the only reminders of the Horde's occupation are a few scattered orcish catapults, broken and rotting across the region.

After the Stonemasons Guild spent years toiling to rebuild Stormwind City, they were left broke, as their fees and salaries were left unpaid and unspoken for by the city's officials. When the time of the rightful payment came, the House of Nobles couldn't because they ran up a huge debt by expanding the kingdom's military presence through Elwynn and into Stranglethorn, and the massive debt crippled the kingdom's economy. When Guildmaster Edwin VanCleef demanded restitution for their works, the nobles ordered the guild disbanded, which resulted in riots that led to the murder of Queen Tiffin Ellerian Wrynn and the creation of the Defias Brotherhood.

===World of Warcraft===

thumb|Northshire Abbey rebuilt in its eponymous valley.
By the Year 25 ADP, many wolves fled from Duskwood to Elwynn, scared by the darkness taking over the forest. Long ago, Elwynn was populated with a group of spiders that lived peacefully amid the woods, until the curse that fell over Brightwood turned the once peaceful woodland into a realm of terror. The spiders began to mutate and grow to monstrous proportions, and entered the shadowed land.

After Azeroth had been victim to massive mystic struggles, which tore magic currents from once stable channels, the mages of the Wizard's Sanctum struggled to determine the effects these changes had caused, and some of them feared the worst. When the flow of magic in Stormwind City and Elwynn Forest was somehow altered, they sent a mage adventurer to Mirror Lake, in order to test its water for magical properties before those who drank from it would be adversely affected.

At some point, a group of murlocs inhabiting Stone Cairn Lake became a local nuisance, responsible for the deaths of many people. Guard Thomas feared that if things didn't soon improved, there would be fighting in Goldshire after a few days, and Marshal Dughan eventually placed a bounty on the murlocs for the adventurers.

===Wrath of the Lich King===

Shortly before the Scourge Invasion in 27 ADP, the Scourge launched an attack on Goldshire.

===Cataclysm===

During the Shattering in 28 ADP, the earthquakes opened a break in the mountain between Elwynn Forest and the Burning Steppes, allowing a force of Blackrock orcs led by Kurtok the Slayer to invade Northshire Valley. When the Stormwind Army was deployed, the orcs took over and burned down the Northshire Vineyards, planning their final assault against the human defenders, until Alliance adventurers arrived in support to repel the Dark Horde invaders. After Kurtok was slain, peasants were sent to seal the passage, so that no more orcs could come through.

The Stormwind Army also had put a bounty on Hogger and incarcerates him to the Stockades in the aftermath of his defeat.

===Elegy===

During the War of the Thorns in 33 ADP, the flood of night elves refugees from Darnassus spread to seemingly every surface of Stormwind, and spilled out most of the way to Goldshire.

===Shadowlands===

During Death Rising in 35 ADP, Elwynn Forest came under attack from the Scourge, until the Stormwind Army and the Argent Crusade were mobilized to repel the undead.

## In the RPG

Elwynn is a gorgeous span of fertile land and thick forests. The weather is brightly sunny and cheerful during the day and silent and peaceful at night. Unlike Duskwood, it is close enough to Stormwind to enjoy considerable Alliance guardianship. Home to many farmers, loggers, and miners, Elwynn Forest is a tranquil region with several friendly places to stop. Little troubles this region; kobolds infiltrate some mines, and murlocs have moved inland to reside in some lakes and rivers, but overall it is safe. One should stick to the roads though, as the forest is home to many fearsome and territorial creatures. Those who can defend themselves are not in any danger, but others may not be so lucky. Elwynn enjoys consistently good weather, warm and humid with few stormy days. Gentle rain falls just enough to make everything grow full and lush.

This woodland is pleasant and tranquil. Loggers and fishers fuel Stormwind’s economy, and a diligent militia keeps it safe from the kobolds and murlocs that occasionally surface. The Defias Brotherhood, a group of robbers and bandits wearing red masks, has recently become a growing menace in the region. The Brotherhood reaches into Elwynn Forest from time to time, preying on lone travelers, although their main center of operations lies somewhere in Westfall. Some say they have a case against Stormwind, as they weren’t paid for their work rebuilding the city; others say they are whiners who charged too much in the first place. Others (such as Brann Bronzebeard) did their work and were paid, and don’t understand the problems.

Elwynn Forest is under guardianship of the kingdom of Stormwind but has its own democratic government.

True criminals in the area are usually sent to Stormwind for trial and punishment in the Stockade, as most people care more for their jobs than housing criminals. Goldshire has a small holding area for drunks and pickpockets, but few stay in the clink for very long. Elwynn’s towns will welcome most any travelers, especially those who want to shop or trade to help the local economies. They are especially kind to those willing to lend a hand in a tough spot.

## People and culture

thumb|Goldshire, the main town of the region.
The humans have lived in Elwynn for centuries, but members of all Alliance races can be frequently seen traveling and residing in Elwynn due to its proximity to the capital, Stormwind City. Most residents of Elwynn are involved in one of its major industries: logging, farming, fishing, or mining. They are also renowned for the breeding of their Evendales: steeds especially prized by adventurers for their loyalty and steadiness in the face of danger. The people are hardy and hard-working and for all its pomp and grandeur, Stormwind wouldn’t last long without a constant stream of food and supplies to support the city’s population.

The region hosts the burning of the Wickerman and is also visited periodically by the famous Darkmoon Faire.

## Geography

thumb|left|upright=0.9|Eastvale Logging Camp
Elwynn lies below the southern foothills of Blackrock Mountain. The Forest is a labyrinthine mixture of rushing rivers, various lakes, and forests of magnificent trees and lush glades. Its rich farmland is a result of frequent rains. Recently, several of the lakes have been found to be inhabited by hostile murlocs. Stormwind City itself sits proudly in the northwestern hills of the region, its gates opening down onto the Forest. East of Stormwind and behind a thick wall lies the peaceful Northshire Valley and its Abbey. Northshire was the pride of Stormwind's vineyards until Defias bandits drove off the farmers and occupied the farm and fields. Though the Defias were eventually taken down, Northshire couldn't catch a break: after the Cataclysm it was invaded by goblin assassins and Blackrock orcs, who ate the grapes and burned the vineyards. The formerly kobold-infested Echo Ridge Mine has been closed up.

Bears, wolves, spiders, and boars constitute the bulk of the region's large wildlife. The creatures are hostile everywhere except for Northshire. The roads leading from Stormwind and Northshire converge to the southeast just north of Goldshire. Goldshire is Elwynn's largest settlement besides Stormwind itself. Second to Goldshire is the Eastvale Logging Camp in the east near the border with Redridge Mountains. Eastvale is the center of Elwynn's lumber industry. On the road from Goldshire to Eastvale is the Tower of Azora where mages conduct arcane experiments in seclusion. On the road to Westfall from Goldshire is the imposing Westbrook Garrison. This barracks and surrounding grounds house a number of the Stormwind Guard and protects Elwynn's western border. Dotted throughout the forest are small farmsteads growing crops such as wheat, pumpkins, melons, and grapes. 

Kobolds infested the Jasperlode Mine and Fargodeep Mine. Gnolls have erected camps in the Forest's Edge region and the northern rim of Stone Cairn Lake. It is rumored that both the kobolds and gnolls are somehow associated with the Defias Brotherhood. Murlocs have taken up residence in and around Stone Cairn Lake and Crystal Lake. Defias presence in the region has been growing and their people have holdings at Mirror Lake Orchard, Northshire Vineyards, the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch, Jerod's Landing, and Stone Cairn Lake, until they were taken down.

## Notes and trivia

- Bimble Longberry fruits come from the forests of Elwynn.
- The farms of the Blackridge, Woodring, and Liam's families are located near Goldshire. The Defias Brotherhood kidnapped the Blackridges and moved them to the Deadmines at some point.
- There was an Alliance ship named the Elwynn.
- The two rival farmsteads, Stonefield Farm and Maclure Vineyards, may be a reference to the real-world Hatfield/McCoy feud.
- Elwynn Forest is prominently featured in South Park's "Make Love, Not Warcraft" episode.
- This starting zone is usually the highest populated due to Stormwind and the Goldshire dueling phenomenon.
- Since cata, a small sheep farm in the mountains around Northshire Valley is inhabited by a gnome named Agee Tyler, a reference to the Blizzard employee Tyler Agee.
- On most servers, Goldshire is an Alliance player hangout with people everywhere dueling and talking.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Goldshire** — Goldshire is a human town centrally located in Elwynn Forest, just southwest of Northshire Valley. It has roads that lead to Stormwind City to the northwest, Northshire Abbey to the northeast, Westfall to the west, and Redridge Mountains to the east.

**Northshire Abbey** — Northshire Abbey is the seat of the Brotherhood of Northshire, a religious order of clerics affiliated to the kingdom of Stormwind. It is located in Northshire Valley, in the foothills of Elwynn Forest, with the town of Goldshire lying to the southwest.

**Eastvale Logging Camp** — Eastvale Logging Camp (also known as the Eastvale Lumber Camp or just Eastvale Camp) is a series of Stormwindian lumber mill camps with living areas in between, located in eastern of Elwynn Forest. Orders for lumber are constantly streaming in from various buyers, and the lumberjacks work against strict deadlines.

**Maclure Vineyards** — The Maclure Vineyards, located in southern Elwynn Forest, belong to the Maclure Family. The Maclures are in a bitter rivalry with the nearby Stonefield Family, but despite their intense dislike of each other, a child from both families has fallen in love with each other in true Romeo and Juliet fashion.

**Mirror Lake Orchard** — Mirror Lake Orchard is situated in western Elwynn Forest, on the southern banks of Mirror Lake. At some point, Defias bandits had taken possession of the orchard as well as the nearby cottage.

**Northshire Vineyards** — The Northshire Vineyards are located just east of the abbey in Northshire Valley.

**Brackwell Pumpkin Patch** — Brackwell Pumpkin Patch is a Stormwindian farmstead located in southeast Elwynn Forest. It served as the Brackwell family's home before the bandits moved in.

**Jerod's Landing** — Jerod's Landing is a boat landing on the Elwynn Forest bank of the Nazferiti river. Upon being taken over by the Defias Brotherhood, the Dockmaster and his men used it to smuggle stolen goods from Redridge Mountains to Klaven's Tower in Westfall.

**Stormwind City** — Stormwind City, also known as New Stormwind or simply Stormwind, is the capital city of the eponymous kingdom of Stormwind and the largest human city on Azeroth. **Death Rising** — Death Rising was a world event in patch 9.0.1 available from 10 November until the launch of Shadowlands on 23/24 November 2020 that heralded the launch of World of Warcraft: Shadowlands. It was similar in scope to the Scourge Invasion events.

**Genn Greymane** — Genn Greymane (sometimes spelled as "Graymane") is the former reigning King of Gilneas, and one of the original founders of the Alliance of Lordaeron. He is a large bearish man, a brawny warrior with thick features, a heavy beard, and black and gray armor.

**Stormwind (kingdom)** — The Kingdom of Stormwind (also called Kingdom of Azeroth) is one of the seven human nations in the Eastern Kingdoms, which controls much of the southern continent of Azeroth, from Elwynn Forest and Westfall to Duskwood and the Redridge Mountains.
