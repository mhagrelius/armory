## Opening paragraph

Vashj'ir (pronounced /vɒʒɪər/) is an underwater region located near the Eastern Kingdoms, off the western coast of Khaz Modan. Player characters can swim throughout the zone by jumping, but when on the ground it acts similarly to the terrestrial zones, and players can use special underwater mounts to move around it. In the Abyssal Depths is located the Abyssal Breach that allows you to reach the Abyssal Maw, which contains two 5-man dungeons. Unlike surface zones, the zones throughout Vashj'ir have no official night cycle. At night, Kelp'thar Forest, Shimmering Expanse, and Abyssal Depths appear fully lit by some sort of light source.

## History

Once a great Highborne city housing some of the most revered night elves of ancient Kalimdor, Vashj'ir was one of the royal cities in the days when Azshara was queen. The ruins of the city located in the Shimmering Expanse were the home of Lady Vashj, who was the daughter of Matron Lestharia Vashj, the city's beloved ruler. Following the War of the Ancients in 10,000 BDP, Vashj'ir was swallowed by the Great Sea in the Great Sundering.

At some point, the kvaldir led by Varkul the Unrelenting were seen fighting the naga in the Shimmering Expanse, as the latter wanted the city of Vashj'ir but mostly the Crucible of Nazsharin from them. During the battle, the kvaldir managed to steal the artifact that was to be used for the summoning ritual of Ozumat, until the kraken was eventually summoned to wipe them out.

Following the Cataclysm in 28 ADP, Queen Azshara sent Lady Naz'jar and her loyal naga to Vashj'ir, where there was a rift to the Abyssal Maw, the elemental realm of Neptulon the Tidehunter. The elemental lord had defied N'Zoth's command, and rather than just destroying him, the Old God coveted his power to control the world's seas. With it, the naga could cut off all sea travel between the continents, splintering the world's nations into isolated enclaves. The Earthen Ring shaman eventually called for the help of the Horde and the Alliance, who ventured beneath the waves and broke the strength of the naga and their allies.

===Legion===

By the time of the Burning Legion's third invasion in 32 ADP, the naga in Vashj'ir were still active, and the champions of the Conclave were tasked to recover the lumenstone they kept.

## Notes

- The Vashj'ir Honor Guards are likely from here.
- Vashj'ir is one of the zones in Cataclysm that heavily features underwater walking as a constant feature.
- If a character dies a certain distance away from Vashj'ir, they'll reappear in the graveyard in Ruins of Lordaeron (if they're Horde) or Faol's Rest (if they're Alliance) in Tirisfal Glades, despite this zone being far away from Vashj'ir.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Nazjatar Empire** — The Nazjatar Empire is the naga civilization beneath the Great Sea, formed by Queen Azshara and her followers in the wake of the Great Sundering and the birth of the naga race.

**Neptulon** — Neptulon the Tidehunter is the Elemental Lord of water and the master of the Abyssal Maw. In past ages, Neptulon was one of the Old Gods' lieutenants and was defeated by the Pantheon of Order and their titan-forged, who imprisoned him and his water elementals within the Elemental Plane.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.

**Independent** — The terms independent or neutral are used for individuals, organizations and factions that are aligned with neither the Alliance nor the Horde.

**Kaldorei Empire** — The Kaldorei Empire (or kaldorei empire and Night Elf Empire) was an ancient night elf civilization spanning most of the continent of ancient Kalimdor, from Lathar'Lazal in the far west to the halls of Uldaman in the east.

**Shimmering Expanse** — Shimmering Expanse is the second zone in the undersea region known as Vashj'ir. It is the stronghold of the naga empire within the area. Besides the wide variety of plant and marine life there are also many mysterious wonders such as Nespirah. This zone is home to the ancient Ruins of Vashj'ir.

**Highborne** — The Highborne (or quel'dorei, meaning "children of noble birth" in Darnassian), were the upper class and a sect of the ancient night elf civilization composed of the favored servitors of Queen Azshara. They had an aptitude for magic.

**Ozumat** — Ozumat is the patriarch of all kraken. It appears to be in the service of the Old Gods' forces, fighting alongside the naga and faceless ones during  [30-35] Final Judgment and  [30-35] Defending the Rift.

**Earthen Ring** — The Earthen Ring is a neutral faction of shaman dedicated to preserving the balance between the elements. This gathering of the most powerful shaman of Azeroth seeks to maintain the balance between the world's elemental forces, unlike its sinister adversary, the Twilight's Hammer cult.

**Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.

**Kelp'thar Forest** — Kelp'thar Forest is the first zone in the undersea region known as Vashj'ir. It is one of the two starting zone for all players level 80 and higher (the other is Mount Hyjal). The forest features a vast array of aquatic life and plants.
