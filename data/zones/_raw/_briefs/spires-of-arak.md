## Opening paragraph

Spires of Arak is a zone in southern Draenor. Previously known only as Arak, it once contained a single massive spire that dominated the forested landscape, before it was sundered by the destruction of the Apexis' Breath of Rukhmar. It is the homeland of arakkoa, who rule over the land from the Skyreach, a majestic palace situated atop the largest spire in the area. Their society, however underwent a great schism between the winged arakkoa and the wingless, who are considered lesser beings by their winged brethren. Many of the wingless arakkoa flee the Spires and pour into Talador, while the Iron Horde's Shattered Hand clan strengthens their presence. Many Azerothians also have built their bases along the coast, such as the Steamwheedle Cartel or the Alliance expedition led by Admiral Taylor.
All that remains of the Spires of Arak in Outland is Skettis, which is now considered to be a part of Terokkar Forest (formerly known as Talador).

## Places and people this zone is about

**Admiral Taylor's Garrison** — Admiral Taylor's Garrison was a forward camp established by Admiral Taylor in the Spires of Arak, but fell to mutiny when the necromancer Ephial led an uprising. The ghosts of its deceased inhabitants remain, some of them seeking to bring the traitors to justice.

**Bladefist Hold** — Bladefist Hold is the capital of the Shattered Hand clan on Draenor. Kargath Bladefist rules the clan from here.

**Pinchwhistle Gearworks** — Pinchwhistle Gearworks is a neutral goblin settlement on the eastern coast of the Spires of Arak on Draenor, constructed after the inhabitants' previous town Pinchwhistle Point to the south was infested by spores from the local fungi.

**Axefall** — Axefall is a Horde settlement in the Spires of Arak. The only major building in this outpost is decided by the commander, which is either the Smuggler's Den or the Hearthfire Tavern. Before a choice is made, the space is occupied by Axefall Worksite.

**Pinchwhistle Point** — Pinchwhistle Point was a Steamwheedle goblin settlement located at the cape of Arak. The town and the inhabitants were, however, infested by spores from the local fungi and were abandoned.

**Camp Thornscar** — Camp Thornscar is a base of the Shattered Hand clan in Shadowglade in the Spires of Arak. From there, the orcs hunt and capture arakkoa to kill and torture them. They also raise ravagers as mounts.

**Shattered Rise** — Shattered Rise is a subzone in the Spires of Arak on Draenor. It is a Shattered Hand clan area.

**Harvesting Pits** — The Harvesting Pits are located in the Spires of Arak.

**Arakkoa** — The arakkoa ("heirs of Arak" in Ravenspeech)  are a bipedal, keenly intelligent avian race, native to Draenor (now Outland), which they call Rakshar ("the Sunstone"). They have an affinity for arcane magic and secrecy.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.
