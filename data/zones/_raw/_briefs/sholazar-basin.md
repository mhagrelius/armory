## Opening paragraph

Sholazar Basin (/ˈʃoʊləzɑːr/ SHOL-ə-zarh) is a tropical jungle basin in the midst of Northrend. It is found between Borean Tundra and Icecrown. The region's climate and safety from the Scourge is maintained by pylons, erected by the titans. The whole history of Sholazar is that of a cradle of life, with at least some of the titan keepers taking an interest in the development of creatures great and small.
During the war against the Lich King, one pylon in the east has fallen, and the Scourge took this opportunity to invade from Icecrown to the north.
In Sholazar Basin, adventurers can take part in a faction war between the wolvar Frenzyheart Tribe and the gorlocs, arctic cousins of murlocs. The various gorloc tribes have gathered together to form the group called the Oracles. Adventurers are only able to choose one side at a time, but are allowed to easily change to whichever faction they wish to be allied with.

## History

thumb|Sholazar Basin landscape
When the titans shaped Azeroth, Freya designed Sholazar Basin to be an area for experimentation along with Un'Goro Crater and the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. All three locations were places where the power of the Well of Eternity had coalesced. The Wild Gods emerged from these cradles of life. The titans' experiments were originally housed in the nearby Wintergrasp Fortress, with their most promising ones being relocated to Sholazar later.

===Wrath of the Lich King===

During the war against the Lich King in 27 ADP, the Lifeblood Pillar was sabotaged by the Cult of the Damned and fell, which allowed snows from Icecrown to collapse into Sholazar Basin, creating the Avalanche and giving the Scourge a pathway in the region. The Lost Lands spread out as an area blighted and dying, with the air fouled with a yellow haze, and the ground thick with undead. As a failsafe created by Keeper Freya, the Avatar of Freya fought ceaselessly to contain the spread of the blight and even sought the help of adventurers in her duties. Together, they used The Etymidian, one of the most destructive forces ever created by the titans, to destroy thousands of undead and the overlords leading the Scourge invasion.

===Exploring Azeroth===

Following the war against the Jailer in 36 ADP, the brothers Bronzebeard met the Avatar of Freya who told them that the situation about the blighted Lost Lands was better than they were during the war against the Lich King, and healing slowly. When Muradin Bronzebeard wondered why Sholazar Basin and Un'Goro Crater were shaped so similarly, his brother Brann theorized that it could be to see how two similar setups diverge over time, like a science experiment. Or that there was one thing the titans and their keepers were trying to accomplish, and making two different places to work on it was better than one. However, these theories led Brann to wonder what they were trying to learn.

## In the RPG

thumb|Map of Sholazar Basin in Lands of Mystery.

Sholazar Basin fills the northwest corner of the Borean Tundra and is difficult to believe it is part of Northrend. Like Kalimdor's Un'Goro Crater, the Sholazar Basin is an anomaly. To get there, you will have to climb a nasty ridge to reach it. Once you top that ridge, however, you will be looking down into a lush tropical jungle. Hot springs dot the basin, and you will have to watch out for steam vents and geysers — they will cook the flesh from your bones in an instant if you get caught in one. Thick vegetation fills the basin, and birds and small animals are everywhere. So are insects. In the past, the Dragonblight was the most fertile region in Northrend, followed by Sholazar Basin. After it became a wasteland, the Basin took its place.

A high elf scholar that once visited Kaskala believed the Titans used the Sholazar Basin as a testing ground. Some of their experiments, or at least the remains of those experiments, may still exist. Thus, the basin is a potential treasure trove of knowledge and power.

## Geography

After the bitter cold of the rest of Northrend, Sholazar Basin is like stepping onto an entirely different plane. It's a beautiful place, tropical, hot, and beard-curlingly humid. This is all the doing of the keepers and the magical artifacts they left to protect the basin from both ill weather and foul creatures like the Scourge. All of this for the benign love of the life they've created.

A dense jungle, named the Wildgrowth Mangal, shot through with small streams spreading across the basin's central area. Around the basin, spaced out to make a rough ring if you were to connect them like dots, are the so-called pillars, though they look far more natural to the topography than the name conjures. They're tall, narrow buttes that rise up from the relatively flat basin floor, each with a thin, elongated device hovering over it, swirling with magic. The pillars emanate pure Life magic into the basin.

## Notable characters

Sholazar Basin NPCs
thumb|upright|The Avatar of Freya protecting Sholazar Basin from the [[Scourge.]]
Sholazar Basin is home to several characters of note. At Frenzyheart Hill, High-Shaman Rakjak leads the Frenzyheart Tribe in the fight against local fauna and their hated enemies, the Oracles, led by High-Oracle Soo-say. The wolvar are not the only new arrivals to the basin, as Hemet Nesingwary leads his band of hunters in search of new game, targeting Broodmother Slivina as the ultimate challenge to a hunter. Artruis the Heartless leads the Scourge attack on the region, but is continually held at bay by the mysterious Avatar of Freya.

## Notes and trivia

- In Lands of Mystery, this area was treated as a subzone of the Borean Tundra instead of its own zone. The map, however, had it as its own zone.
- To open up the flight path at Nesingwary Base Camp, there is a 2-part quest chain from Weslex Quickwrench that you have to complete first.
- Given Blizzard's fondness for pop-culture references, Sholazar may be a nod to the Savage Land of Marvel Comics. Both are tropical paradises formed in arctic regions by a powerful race of creator-beings.
- At the coordinates Sholazar Basin, a hatch with the numbers "5 9 16 17 24 43" can be found as a reference to the series Lost.
- The naming of subzone The Lost Lands may be a reference to the Land of the Lost, which both Sholazar Basin and Un'Goro Crater seem to be based on.
- Sholazar Basin, like Un'Goro Crater, is littered with mysterious Titanic crystals that can be activated through structures or watchers when the need arises.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Nesingwary Base Camp** — The Nesingwary Base Camp is Hemet Nesingwary's new location in Northrend. The base camp is located in western Sholazar Basin, just north of the Seabreach Flow, inside the Path of the Lifewarden, southwest of the Suntouched Pillar and west of the Wildgrowth Mangal.

**Frenzyheart Hill** — Frenzyheart Hill is the village of the Frenzyheart Tribe of wolvar in Sholazar Basin. It is a village of their typical low, round dwellings atop a hill that's been named after them. They founded it in the region after being driven from their lands by the Scourge, prior to the war against the Lich King.

**Rainspeaker Canopy** — Rainspeaker Canopy is a village wich sits northeast of River's Heart, within the Wildgrowth Mangal of the Sholazar Basin. It is home to the Oracles, a gorloc tribe. They live in little elevated houses, open-sided to encourage cool breezes and thatched with leaves to keep off the rain.

**Kartak's Hold** — Kartak's Hold is an outpost of the Frenzyheart Tribe in the far southwestern corner of Sholazar Basin. They are at constant war with the gorlocs of Sparktouched Haven. It is here they worship Kartak the Abominable.

**Mistwhisper Refuge** — Mistwhisper Refuge is an outpost of the Oracles clan of gorlocs in northwest Sholazar Basin. It is bordered by the Bittertide Lake to the south, Dorian's Outpost to the northwest, the Savage Thicket to the north, and the Glimmering Pillar to the east.

**Mosswalker Village** — Mosswalker Village is the ravaged village of the gorloc Mosswalker tribe, located in the Lost Lands and south of The Avalanche in Sholazar Basin.

**Sparktouched Haven** — Sparktouched Haven is an outpost of the Oracles, located just west of The Mosslight Pillar in south-west Sholazar Basin. It is the home of the Sparktouched tribe who are at constant war with the wolvar of the Frenzyheart Tribe at Kartak's Hold.

**Spearborn Encampment** — Spearborn Encampment is a Frenzyheart outpost in northwest Sholazar Basin. It is bordered by the Savage Thicket to the east, the Glimmering Pillar to the southeast, and Dorian's Outpost to the south. The nearest Oracle camp of gorlocs is to the southeast at Mistwhisper Refuge.

**Frenzyheart Tribe** — The Frenzyheart Tribe faction is a primitive tribe of wolvars located in Sholazar Basin.

**Oracles** — The Oracles are a faction of several friendly gorloc tribes that inhabit Sholazar Basin. They see themselves as guardians of the titan technology that remains in the area, though they don't really have an understanding of the artifacts they are guarding.

**Nesingwary Safari (group)** — The Nesingwary Safari, also known as the Nesingwary Expedition or Hemet's Hunting Party, are the various adventurers and big game hunters who accompany the legendary dwarven hunter and Alliance war hero Hemet Nesingwary in his travels around Azeroth and beyond.

**Titan** — The titans (sometimes capitalized) are a race of colossal, planet-sized cosmic beings, composed of arcane magic and the primordial matter from which the universe was born. As the main manifestation of Order, they roamed across the cosmos like walking worlds, imbued with the raw power of creation itself.
