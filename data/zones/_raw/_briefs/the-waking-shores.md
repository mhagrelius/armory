## Opening paragraph

The Waking Shores are a zone on the Dragon Isles and the ancestral home of the red and black dragonflights. A wild, untamed land rife with elemental magic, the Waking Shores are the first zone that players see when they arrive to the Isles with a joint Explorers' League-Reliquary expedition. In the zone there are gorlocs.
The zone storyline sees Alexstrasza summoning the Alliance and Horde to aid the red flight against the native djaradin, while Wrathion seeks to reclaim the Obsidian Citadel of the black flight from enemy hands and come to terms with the state of his ruined flight.

## Description

The Waking Shores fell dormant after the dragonflights left their homeland ten thousand years ago. Now, with Azeroth's world-soul revitalized, the awesome primal might of the Shores stirs anew, rousing the dragons' ancient allies and age-old enemies from their long millennia of slumber. This land is stirring with the reawakening of elemental energies. Long ago, the elements were present in their most primal, unfettered form; however, the resurgence of magic in the Dragon Isles has caused that energy to reawaken.

## Geography

Geysers and hot springs are commonplace, and powerful streams and rivers carve grottoes into the colorful rocks and sediment. Waterways feed into the land's heart and the Life Pools of the red dragonflight. While millennia ago this area was populated with dragon architecture like the rest of the Broodlands, most of those structures have long since fallen into decay and become overgrown.

Volcanic areas are alive with molten earth and fire. As the elevation rises, the land becomes harsher. In these imposing mountains, Neltharus, the Obsidian Citadel was built by the black dragonflight so that the Earth-Warder could defend the Broodlands. Abandoned long ago, the imposing fortress has now been claimed by the djaradin.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Scalecracker Keep** — Scalecracker Keep is a subzone of the Waking Shores on the Dragon Isles, located along the northern coast. It is a large djaradin settlement and the home of the Qalashi.

**Dragonscale Basecamp** — Dragonscale Basecamp  is the headquarters of the Dragonscale Expedition.

**Rubyscale Outpost** — Rubyscale Outpost is a small outpost of the red dragonflight located on the eastern part of the border between the Waking Shores and the Ohn'ahran Plains.

**Dragonbane Keep** — Dragonbane Keep is a keep located just directly south of the Obsidian Citadel. The area was originally a djaradin holding; though appears to have been abandoned when the djaradin entered their hibernation with the departure of the dragons.

**Hatchery Observatory** — The Hatchery Observatory is a tower located in the south of the Waking Shores. It overlooks the Ruby Lifeshrine to the north.

**Obsidian Bulwark** — The Obsidian Bulwark  is a ruined outpost inhabited by Wrathion's Blacktalon forces. It is located near the center of the Waking Shores, on a hill overlooking the Emberflow and providing a view of the Obsidian Citadel.

**Skytop Observatory** — Skytop Observatory is a ruined dragon settlement located atop a mountain in the southwestern Waking Shores, east of the Ruby Lifeshrine. Recently, several dragons and dragon-riders from the Valdrakken Accord made the area their home and now teach others about dragonriding.

**Ruby Lifeshrine** — The Ruby Lifeshrine  is the seat of the red dragonflight and location of the Ruby Oathstone in southeastern Waking Shores. It is surrounded by the Ruby Life Pools from west to northeast, Valdrakken to the southeast across the chasm, and the Flashfrost Assault (and entrance to the Ruby Life Pools instance proper) to the…

**Obsidian Citadel** — The Obsidian Citadel is the ancient home of the black dragonflight, located in western Waking Shores. It is here that the Obsidian Oathstone and Obsidian Throne can be found. The citadel was constructed so that the black flight might defend the entire Dragon Isles.

**Red dragonflight** — The red dragonflight is a draconic organization primarily led by the eponymous red dragons to uphold their creed to the world. They are led by Alexstrasza the Life-Binder, whom the titan Eonar charged with protecting life.

**Black dragonflight** — The black dragonflight (sometimes referred to as the obsidian flight) is a draconic organization primarily led by the eponymous black dragons to uphold their creed to serve as warders of the earth.

**Qalashi tribe** — The Qalashi tribe is a djaradin tribe found in the Waking Shores on the Dragon Isles. Formerly led by Warlord Sargha, they are the fiercest of dragon hunters. According to legend, a legendary Qalashi warlord conquered the weaker djaradin tribes and united their strongest members under the Qalashi banner.
