## Opening paragraph

Drustvar (/ˈdrʌst.vɑɹ/ DRUHST-vahr) is the westernmost region of Kul Tiras. Heavily forested and mountainous, Drustvar is more inhospitable than neighboring lands, and produces most of the kingdom's ore as well as some of its toughest warriors. It is the seat of power for House Waycrest, one of four noble houses that rule Kul Tiras. As the original home of the Drust vrykul, the region witnessed intense fighting as the first humans of the Arathorian Empire battled to colonize the islands. Its legacy remains both dark and sinister, and has resurfaced in modern times in the form of perverse witchcraft and twisted cults.
The people of Drustvar have a deep hunting tradition, which includes a group of local rangers, while the Waycrest Guard employs falconers. Unlike the rest of Kul Tiras, which worships the Tidemother and the sea, the primary religion in Drustvar seems to be the worship of the Holy Light. It was described as a rugged land with a dark history and superstitious people.

## History

===Early history===
Drust-human war
thumb|The Drust ruins of [[Gol Osigr near Mount Cortis.]]
After the Great Sundering in 10,000 BDP, various vrykul tribes set out for the seas, and one such population, known as the Drust, settled across the archipelago of Kul Tiras. Across Drustvar, Stormsong Valley, and Tiragarde Sound, they beat back the forces that threatened the land, including the great sea beasts and naga, then built roads and structures in the areas they inhabited.

Around 2,700 BDP, humans from the Arathorian Empire expanded their territory to the island, which they would later call Kul Tiras. When the settlers tried for peace with the Drust, the latter went to war immediately, and aggressively launched raids against the humans' fledgling hamlets from Drustvar. The assaults carried on for many years, until the ancestors of House Waycrest decided something had to be done, and eventually began a war against the Drust. Seeking a way to counter the Drust's death magic, the Waycrests founded the Order of Embers to fight them, while the Thornspeakers druids broke off from their barbarous people, and eventually integrated into Kul Tiran society. At the end of the war, Colonel Arom Waycrest organized a final assault against the Drust and their city of Gol Osigr, assisted by both Ulfar's Thornspeakers and Athair. After he defeated King Gorak Tul, Arom was given the title of Lord and from then on, his family would rule Drustvar as House Waycrest.

After the battle, Athair told Lord Arom that to repay the kindness he had shown to him, and that as long as he exists, he would protect the people of Drustvar should they come for his help. Ever since then, the valiant and true have found shelter with the White Stag of the forest. The Thornspeakers once tended to Drustvar as kin of the trees, but that age has long since passed.

===Battle for Azeroth===

Drust incursion
thumb|left|Fallhaven cursed by the [[Heartsbane Coven.]]
By the time of the Fourth War in 33 ADP, Drustvar was controlled by Lord Arthur and Lady Meredith Waycrest, who had gone suspiciously absent. In their absence, Drustvar's contributions of arms (and sausages) began to dry up until the Alliance adventurers worked with their Kul Tiran allies to get to the bottom of the mystery, as well as the curse over the land. Unbeknownst to the Admiralty, Lady Meredith, stricken with grief due to her ailing husband, had called out to any power that could save him. It was Gorak Tul himself who answered her cry and promised that death would never part the couple, only if she began to recruit new members for the Heartsbane Coven, which would bring the Drust back to the realm of the living. After adapting to their newfound powers, the witches started to kidnap the local populace and take them to Waycrest Manor, until the entirety of Corlain was under their control.

After he was informed by Priscilla Ashvane that the Waycrests were falling into a state of disrepair, Harlan Sweete sent Captain Leadfist to establish a base at Chandlery Wharf. Their objectives were to deplete the Waycrest's supplies, keep them destabilized, and prevent them from being able to support the Proudmoores, while filling the Irontide's coffers at the same time. Anyport also fell under the control of Richard Tornsail and the Irontides, until the heroes helped Cesi Loosecannon defeat the pirates and become the new boss of the smugglers' cove.

When wicker constructs attacked the small town of Fletcher's Hollow, the populace was desperate to restore order and turned against Lucille, the daughter of Lord and Lady Waycrest. Accused of being a witch, the townsfolk nearly hanged her at Hangman's Point until the Alliance ambassador, who had just saved Fallhaven from the Coven's curse, arrived to clear her name. After the heroes rallied Fletcher's Hollow's woodsmen, fended off the wicker constructs, and evacuated the town, Constable Henry Framer received a letter from Mayor Evelyn Pare exonerating Lucille.

thumb|Arom's Stand, headquarters of the [[Order of Embers.]]
Upon learning from Marshal Everit Reade that the witches had spread unchecked through Drustvar, recruiting women from the villages to join their ranks and binding spirits to wicker thralls, Lucille and the Alliance ambassador investigated the ancient death magic of the Drust. Within the ruins of Gol Var, they uncovered the secrets of the Order of Embers, and eventually created a new generation of inquisitors. During their assault on Waycrest Manor, Lucille and her allies fought against the Heartsbane Coven, as well as Lord and Lady Waycrest, who had been turned undead. When their deaths opened a portal to the realm of Thros, allowing Gorak Tul to return to the living world, the Alliance heroes rallied for one final push to thwart the Drust King's efforts. In the aftermath, Lucille Waycrast assumed her role as Lady of House Waycrest, appointed Joan Cleardawn as the new Marshal of the Waycrest Guard, and later pledged the support of her house to Lord Admiral Jaina Proudmoore. 

Aboard The Banshee's Wail, Trade-prince Jastor Gallywix led the Horde's expedition into Drustvar, seeking to exploit the large amount of Azerite beneath the region. The Bilgewater Cartel eventually settled Krazzlefrazz Outpost and began their mining operation, collecting silver within the Bleak Hills Mine and Azerite from Arom's Crossing. From the Strider airship, High Tinker Gelbin Mekkatorque eventually led an attack on the mining operations in Drustvar, using an army of remote-controlled robots.

During the Faction Assaults on Drustvar, the Order of Embers fought alongside the Purified against the Horde, protecting the region and its inhabitants.

Both Fletcher's Hollow and Carver's Harbor were occasionally attacked by Darktide naga, who amassed prisoners to sacrifice to their dark master, before being defeated by the adventurers.

## Notes and trivia

- The citizens of Drustvar are represented in the capital Boralus by the attendants of House Waycrest. There are also traders from the area.
- One of the ships of the Third Fleet was the Drustvaria.
- There is a bar in Anyport called The Drust Bar.
- In life the Defenders of Drustvar were valiant champions who had sworn to protect the land and its people.
- Bloodflies are a type of flies found in Drustvar known to bite people. According to a story, they don't bite a witch because they wouldn't touch her foul blood.
- There are many sausage vendors in Drustvar or hailing from the area, such as Charisse Payton and Elijah Eggleton, a cultural note acknowledged by the Sausage Sampler achievement. Even Brennadam Citizens reminisce about them.
- The cut quest Thesis Statement had someone (likely from the Adventurer's Society) hypothesizing that there were titanic ruins under Drustvar because of the presence of troggs.
- In its earliest stages of development, Drustvar was planned to be a cold, frozen zone in northern Kul Tiras with the witches as only a small part of the story.
- During alpha and beta, the adjective for something related to Drustvar was "Drustvari".

## Places and people this zone is about

**Arom's Stand** — Arom's Stand is a Kul Tiran town located in central Drustvar, named after Arom Waycrest, the first lord of Drustvar. It serves as the headquarters of the Order of Embers.

**Fallhaven** — Fallhaven is a Kul Tiran farming village in eastern Drustvar. Its mayor, Cyril White, is a longtime friend of the Proudmoores. During the Fourth War, the villagers were cursed by the witch Helena Gentle, who used the Heartsbane Coven's dark magic to make them unable to move or respond.

**Fletcher's Hollow** — Fletcher's Hollow is a village in eastern Drustvar. It features several buildings, including the Rusty Bucket Tavern, Ladd's Boat Rental, and Deals for Wheels.

**Falconhurst** — Falconhurst is a Kul Tiran town located in southwestern Drustvar, cut off from other settlements by the Crimson Forest.

**Carver's Harbor** — Carver's Harbor is a Kul Tiran slaughterhouse located on the eastern coast of Drustvar. It is a meat market, with a dock by the sea, and employed some of the greatest butchers of the region. The two buildings here are named the Hog's End Butchery and Gibb's Boat Restoration.

**Anyport** — Anyport (also referred to as the Cove) is a smugglers cove located in western Drustvar. The Lowtide Tavern is located below the broken night elf ship, and the Drunk Tank is in the water, north of Anyport.

**Corlain** — Corlain is the regional capital and largest city of Drustvar, serving as the seat of power for House Waycrest. The settlement itself is divided into an upper and lower section, with Waycrest Manor dominating the northern portion of town. The Waycrest Guard are responsible for keeping order within Corlain.

**House Waycrest** — House Waycrest is one of four noble houses that rule Kul Tiras. Their seat is the Waycrest Manor in the region of Drustvar. Before rising as a noble house, the Waycrest family led the war against the Drust, when humans first settled Kul Tiras thousands of years ago.

**Kul Tiras (kingdom)** — Kul Tiras (/kuːlˈtɪrəs/ KOOL-TEER-əs), officially the Admiralty of Kul Tiras (also spelled Kul'Tiras) is a maritime human kingdom based on the archipelago of the same name.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Irontide Raiders** — The Irontide Raiders (or simply the Irontide, and apparently under the business moniker of Sweete Enterprises of Freehold) are a ruthless pirate band based out of Freehold in Tiragarde Sound.

**Heartsbane Coven** — The Heartsbane Coven is a coven of witches who appeared in Kul Tiras and terrorized the people of Drustvar during the Fourth War. They are humans whose bodies have been contorted by the ancient Drust magic that they wield. When a witch grows powerful enough, they slit their own throats to ascend.
