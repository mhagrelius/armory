## Opening paragraph

Hallowfall is a subterranean zone in Khaz Algar, located below and to the west of the Ringing Deeps and north and above Azj-Kahet. Hallowfall is a landscape of verdant plateaus and river canyons bordered to the west by the endless Undersea, all within an enormous cavern. Beledar, an immense crystal lodged into the cavern ceiling, provides light, warmth, and growth for vegetation, creating a bright and lush environment reminiscent of the surface world. It is inhabited by the Hallowfall Arathi, an expedition of the Arathi Empire. From their capital of Mereldar, the Arathi use their airships to travel throughout the cavern and establish docks, farms, and villages.
The Arathi settled in the area more than a decade before the events of The War Within, after the Arathi emperor received a vision from the Sacred Flame of Hallowfall and a source of radiant light, the "Emperor's Vision". The Hallowfall Expedition traveled across the Storming Sea to seek out the vision, until their fleet of airships was enveloped in Light from the Emperor's Vision and magically transported into Hallowfall. The resulting crash left them stranded and cut off from the Empire. The land in Hallowfall proved fertile, and the survivors settled in the cavern under the light of the Emperor's Vision, the "star" they named Beledar. Years later, Hallowfall was shook by the Day of Darkness—the day Sargeras plunged his sword into Azeroth—and Beledar began periodically shifting into a Void state. The Arathi now fight a near-constant battle against kobyss raiders emerging from the Undersea as well as from swarms of nerubians from neighboring Azj-Kahet. The Arathi consider Hallowfall to be a holy land, a revelation of the Sacred Flame that it is their duty to protect and cleanse from darkness.

## Notes and trivia

- All of the flora in Hallowfall is turned toward Beledar, much like plants on the surface following the sun.
- The original version of the official site for The War Within erroneously described the Hallowfall Arathi as "a human tribe who have battled against nerubians for centuries", rather than a recently-arrived branch of the Arathi Empire. The description was corrected on November 17, 2023.
- During The War Within beta, Wenren Althal's dialogue stated that the Hallowfall Arathi had set out from the Empire fifteen years prior to The War Within, while the quest Mereldar's Light stated that they'd had arrived to Hallowfall ten years prior to The War Within. Prior to release, the former reference was changed to a vaguer "over a decade ago" and the latter was removed entirely.
- Hallowfall appears to be inspired by the Hollow Earth trope commonly seen in subterranean fiction. Many Hollow Earth stories portray a lush underground world sustained by an inner sun, often also featuring a subterranean ocean and lost civilizations of ancient humans.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Dunelle's Kindness** — Dunelle's Kindness  is a town with a dawntower, located in the eastern extent of the zone. Players are directed here in the first quest of the Hallowfall storyline,  [75-78] The Hallowed Path.

**Lorel's Crossing** — Lorel's Crossing  is a town in north-central Hallowfall. Players are directed here in  [75-78] A Lamplighter's Duty, the start of the second chapter of the Hallowfall storyline.

**Mereldar** — Mereldar  is the capital of the Hallowfall Arathi, located in the western half of Hallowfall, near Beledar. It is named in honor of the priestess Mereldar, one of the first human priests to come into contact with the Holy Light.

**Priory of the Sacred Flame** — The Priory of the Sacred Flame is a dungeon in The War Within, located in western Hallowfall high on a rise, close to Beledar.

**Aegis Wall** — The Aegis Wall  is a dawntower location and fortified front against the nerubians coming up from Azj-Kahet in southeastern Hallowfall. When players first get there in  [75-78] Where the Light Touches, early in the Hallowfall storyline, the fortification is being overrun by the Dreadstrike nerubians.

**Growling Hollow** — Growling Hollow  is an area in northeast Hallowfall.

**Light's Redoubt** — Light's Redoubt  is a dawntower on the southern edge of Hallowfall. It serves as an airship dock for the Reckoning and fortification against Azj-Kahet in southwestern Hallowfall. Adventurers are directed here during  [75-78] The Final Front, in the final chapter of the Hallowfall storyline.

**Tenir's Ascent** — Tenir's Ascent  is the main airship dock of the Hallowfall Arathi, located in south-central Hallowfall. A dawntower stands at the northwestern end, and the Dawnbreaker is permanently docked here.
