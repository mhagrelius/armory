## Opening paragraph

Dornogal  (/ˈdɔːrnəɡɔːl/ DORN-ə-gawl) is the capital city of the earthen dwarves of Khaz Algar and serves as the player capital of The War Within. It is located in the center of the Isle of Dorn.
The city was named in honor of the titanic watchers Dornic and Galan. It was originally built to be the fortress for the Coreway. After Adelgonn and the Stonebound abandoned the Edicts, the city fell into disrepair. Dornogal has been isolated within Khaz Algar for a long while, which resulted in the city's earthen developing their own manners and customs. The city is surprisingly verdant and was apparently carved from the land.
In the year 42 ADP, Xal'atath unleashed the nerubians of Azj-Kahet upon Dalaran and destroyed the magical city. The resulting crash lured Stormward Baelgrim and his Stormriders away from Dornogal, allowing the nerubians to damage the Coreway. The survivors of Dalaran were welcomed into the city by Councilward Merrix for their part in defending the earthen.
To help repair the Coreway, Merrix ordered Baelgrim to enlist the aid of Adelgonn and the Unbound. After a tense reunion, Baelgrim and Adelgonn reconciled after he apologized for failing to protect the Unbound. They were officially allowed to return to the city and began repairs to the Coreway.
After the Horde and Alliance armies sailed to the Isle of Dorn to aid in the war against Xal'atath, they established embassies within Dornogal.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Council of Dornogal** — The Council of Dornogal is the ruling council of the earthen of Khaz Algar.

**Dornogal (nation)** — Dornogal is a nation of earthen located on the continent of Khaz Algar. Under the leadership of the Council of Dornogal, the Algari earthen society has been isolated for thousands of years, gradually developing their own behaviors, language, and demeanor with many similarities to other former earthen groups.

**Coreway** — The Coreway is an immense tunnel extending from Dornogal on the surface deep into Azeroth. It was built by the titan-forged so that the titans could study Azeroth's worldsoul without harming it. At one point, the Coreway extended all the way to the core of Azeroth; at present it terminates in the Ringing Deeps.

**Fissure** — The Fissure is the seedy underbelly of Dornogal, found in the eastern corner of the city. Various types of trade appear to take place here, from barbershop services to the illicit dealings of Steelvein.

**Forgegrounds** — The Forgegrounds  serves as the profession quarter of Dornogal, found in the southeastern corner of the city. The Coreway lies to the west, the Fissure to the northeast, and Foundation Hall is found to the north.

**Foundation Hall** — Foundation Hall  is the capitol building of Dornogal, located in the northwestern corner of the city. Thraegar Rest is to the southwest, Keepers Terrace is to the northwest, Stoneshaper's Atrium is to the east, and Stoneward's Rise is to the northeast.

**Alliance Embassy** — The Alliance Embassy is located at the Coreway in Dornogal.

**Archive** — The Archive  is the nexus of earthen civilization in Khaz Algar. There, memories are cycled, catalogued, and stored at the end of an earthen's scheduled rotation. A task that is done by the Archivists. It is located on the western edge of the Coreway in southwestern Dornogal.

**Cinderside Hall** — The Cinderside Hall is located in the Corridors in Dornogal.

**Thraegar Rest** — Thraegar Rest  is located between Foundation Hall and Keepers Terrace in northwestern Dornogal.

**Chip Off the Block** — Chip Off the Block is located in the Forgegrounds in Dornogal.

**Azj-Kahet** — Azj-Kahet (/ˈɑːʒ.kəˌhɛt/ AHZH kə-HET) is a subterranean zone located in the deepest part of Khaz Algar, south and below Hallowfall and the Ringing Deeps. The high capital of nerubian society, Azj-Kahet is home to the last kingdom of living nerubians.
