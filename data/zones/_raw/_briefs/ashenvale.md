## Opening paragraph

Ashenvale, also known as Ashenvale Forest, is a wilderness region south of Darkshore and Felwood, west of Azshara, and north of the Stonetalon Mountains and the Barrens. It is the ancestral homeland of the night elves, under the protection of their Sentinels and the children of the demigod Cenarius,  with Astranaar as their main settlement. Since the Third War, the Horde has expanded their influence in the eastern part of the woods, with Splintertree Post as their main base of operations. The region is heavily populated with furbolgs, satyr, and wild creatures such as spiders, wolves, and bears.

## History

===After the Sundering===
Not much is known about Ashenvale prior to the War of the Ancients, but it was said that Xavius had a tower within the forest.

After the Great Sundering in 10,000 BDP, the burgeoning night elf society expanded into the dense forests of Ashenvale, slowly rebuilding their society over a few centuries. During the War of the Satyr in 9,300 BDP, Ashenvale was ablaze by the satyrs' destructive fel magics as they struck with but one goal; the utter destruction of kaldorei civilization. After the Lords of the Emerald Flame were defeated, the Cenarion Circle fought against the Druids of the Scythe, leading to the death of Mel'Thandris Starsong, who sacrificed herself to save her fellow night elves from the worgen. After the battle, she and the Scythe of Elune were subsequently buried at the Shrine of Mel'Thandris in Ashenvale.

Around 7,300 BDP, the Highborne declared the druids cowards for refusing to wield the arcane, and while Archdruid Malfurion warned them that any use of magic would be punished, Dath'Remar Sunstrider's followers unleashed a terrible magical storm upon Ashenvale in an attempt to protest against these restrictions. As the kaldorei could not bring themselves to put so many of their kin to death, they instead decided to exile them from their lands, cutting them off from the Well of Eternity's energies.

When the Long Vigil began, Archdruid Malfurion and his druids entered the Emerald Dream, while High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind and her Sentinels continued to patrol Ashenvale. For centuries, the keepers of the grove kept close watch on the night elves and regularly helped the Sentinels maintain peace across the land, while the dryads often appeared in the open with increasing frequency. Their presence in the wilds of Ashenvale was seen as an omen of better times to come.

Around 4,500 BDP, the Cenarion Circle discovered the existence of saronite, and a group led by Fandral Staghelm decided to eradicate it. They secretly cut six enchanted branches from the boughs of Nordrassil, then traveled the world to locations where saronite blooms had sprung to life, where they planted the branches to thwart the corruption. These areas included Ashenvale, Crystalsong Forest, Feralas, Duskwood and the Hinterlands. The branches quickly took root and became new trees, which acted like conduits, channeling the powers of the Emerald Dream into the waking world, strengthening the nearby wildlife, and scouring the saronite deposits across Ashenvale.

===The Third War===

thumb|Grommash Hellscream.]]
Tyrande's missions here.
During the Third War in 21 ADP, a group of goblins under Neeloc Greedyfingers started deforesting the area before the Warsong clan arrived, but were prevented from doing much damage by the local furbolg tribe. Grommash Hellscream and his orcs eventually acquired the goblins' aid after they repelled the furbolgs and Sentinels. At the time, the elusive dark trolls had a settlement in the region, while powerful artifacts, enclosed in circles of mushrooms, were protected by forest guardians. Cenarius restored the cut trees but was killed by Grommash Hellscream tricked by the Burning Legion masters.

When the Burning Legion began its second invasion, Archimonde launched his forces into Ashenvale, with infernals raining from the sky while thousands of undead and demons appeared at the eastern edges of the region. The demon lord and his followers met resistance from the night elves, forest spirits, and the combined Horde and Alliance armies. Since they were fighting on separate fronts, however, the Legion easily overwhelmed them and marched steadily inland toward Mount Hyjal, trampling the woodlands and all who stood in its path. When Tyrande Whisperwind finally managed to wake Malfurion Stormrage, the great archdruid was stunned to see what had become of Ashenvale. As fel fire consumed the forests, a toxic undead blight choked all life from the land, forcing Malfurion to quickly awake the other druids and save the wilds. 

During their march, Archimonde commanded the corruption of Ashenvale to weaken the defenses of the kaldorei, leaving the task to the dreadlord Tichondrius, who used the Skull of Gul'dan to mutate the wildlife, corrupt the ancients, and pollute the environment in the form of Felwood. The demon hunter Illidan Stormrage eventually hunted down Tichondrius to consume the power of the skull, and while Felwood would remain tainted, its corruptive energies didn't spread further into the rest of Ashenvale and Hyjal.

At some point during the invasion of Ashenvale, an unnamed village was destroyed, along with the family of Vandel, who survived as he was hunting out in the forests.

When the Watchers were fighting against Illidan's Naga on the Broken Isles, Malfurion Stormrage brought mountain giants from Ashenvale to the isles to aid Maiev Shadowsong.

===World of Warcraft===

In Year 25 ADP, the night elves began a massive push to retake the forests of Ashenvale, focusing their attention on ridding their land of the Warsong Outriders once and for all. Among them, the Silverwing Sentinels answered the call and swore that they would not rest until every last orc was defeated and cast out of Warsong Gulch. Through the Silverwing battlemasters sent across the Alliance capitals, the faction eventually recruited heroes from far and wide in order to shatter the aggressors and stop the disappearance of their precious land.

## In the RPG

Ashenvale Forest is also the term used for the larger forested region which includes most of western Hyjal including Azshara.

===Description===
In addition to bears and great cats prowling the dense woodlands, hulking beak-faced wildkin forage throughout Ashenvale. Vicious harpies are known to make massive nests in the tops of the tallest trees. They hunt at dawn and dusk when the light filtering through the canopy throws the forest into a confusion of shifting shadows. Ancients - sentient trees who act as wardens of the forest - roam Ashenvale as well. Ashenvale is under the protection of ancients and the night elf sentinels. Neither had established a community as such within the forest, though, preferring to leave the vast woodlands in its natural state. Ashenvale boasted no cities; the old forest's inhabitants are primarily beasts, and there are few humanoid settlements. It is devoid of any notable ruins. Travelers through Ashenvale should treat these forces with respect, especially if they hope to reach their destination. The ancients a Cnd night elf sentinels guard Ashenvale zealously with constant patrols. Any who threaten the forest - cutting down live trees, poaching beasts of the forest, and the like - are met with the ancients' powerful attacks and the sentinels' deadly arrows. The only unified night elf presence dwelling in Ashenvale is a reclusive group of rangers, the Farstriders. They use the terrain and train other inhabitants in their specialized skills. They hold a difference of opinion amongst themselves regarding strangers in Ashenvale. Some believe that their duty is to welcome and protect travelers, as long as they show no threat to the forest. Others see any strangers as unwelcome interlopers who must be “escorted” to the southern border. Still, whatever their opinion on Ashenvale, the Farstriders bow to the wishes of the ancients and the sentinels. There is little reason to try to take the forest, since it has no cities to invade and no treasures to plunder (that anyone knows of), but the rangers and the ancients remain vigilant nonetheless.

After the Third War, the Wildhammer dwarves who traveled to Kalimdor with the Alliance founded a settlement in the southern mountains of Ashenvale, alerting the night elves of any incursions.

## Geography

Ashenvale is a forest full of ancient trees, teeming with life. Though pockets of the forest have become tainted, overall the area is a place of deep magic and spiritual connection with nature. Several lakes are sheltered beneath the forest's boughs. A stretch of beach lines the west edge of the forest, known as the Zoram Strand, where hostile naga dwell. The forest is bordered by mountains to the south, and more forest to the north. The eastern border is the Southfury River.

Ashenvale contains Blackfathom Deeps, an instanced dungeon located in the northwestern corner of the zone (Ashenvale, The Zoram Strand: 14.0, 15.0). In addition, the micro dungeon Bough Shadow, the site of one of the Great Trees, can be found in this zone. The Alliance entrance to the battleground Warsong Gulch is located in the southern part of the forest. Travel hubs include Astranaar and Forest Song for Alliance, and Splintertree Post and Zoram'gar Outpost for the Horde.

## Notable characters

Ashenvale NPCs

Ashenvale is home to several characters of note. At Maestra's Post, Sentinel Onaeya fights tooth and nail against the Horde assault. In Astranaar, Raene Wolfrunner attempts to cleanse the taint that has affected the local furbolgs. At the Zoram'gar Outpost, Je'neu Sancrea sends bold adventurers into the dark Blackfathom Deeps in search of mystical items. At Forest Song, the draenei Vindicator Vedaar oversees the construction of a Draenei outpost.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Forest Song** — Forest Song is an ancient night elf ruin located in northeastern Ashenvale, just northeast of Satyrnaar, north of the Warsong Lumber Camp, and southwest of Bough Shadow.

**Blackfathom Camp** — Blackfathom Camp is a small Alliance camp located northeast of Zoram'gar Outpost and southeast of Blackfathom Deeps. The camp is led by Talen.

**Mor'shan Rampart** — The Mor'shan Rampart lies at the northern terminus of the Gold Road in Northern Barrens. This makeshift defensive post was built to withstand the push of Alliance forces into the region from Ashenvale to the north.

**Hellscream's Watch** — Hellscream's Watch is a Horde encampment in Ashenvale, located just across the waters from the embattled Astranaar. It is one of the Horde's most contentious fronts in Ashenvale.

**Greenpaw Village** — Greenpaw Village is located in central Ashenvale, south of Raynewood Retreat, northeast of Silverwind Refuge, and northwest of the southern moonwell. It is the home of the Foulweald tribe, led by Chief Murgut and the shaman Oakpaw.

**Dor'Danil Barrow Den** — The Dor'Danil Barrow Den is located in Ashenvale, south of Splintertree Post, and southwest of Felfire Hill. Although it has traditionally been the site of rest for night elven druids, the incursion of the Horde put the barrow den behind enemy lines.

**Astranaar** — Astranaar is a night elven town located in central Ashenvale. It is the largest settlement in the region. It lies on an island within a lake, straddling the main road through Ashenvale, connected to the mainland with only a pair of bridges.

**Darnassus (nation)** — Darnassus is the nation of the night elves and a member state of the Grand Alliance. Named after their former capital city, Darnassus, it is the cultural descendant of the ancient Kaldorei Empire.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.

**Durotar (nation)** — The nation of Durotar (or kingdom) is an orcish nation and a member state of the Horde. Following the Third War, the clans under Warchief Thrall's leadership founded the nation of Durotar and the great city of Orgrimmar.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Furbolg** — Furbolgs are a hulking, simple-minded race of bear-men who live in the northern regions of Kalimdor, in the continent of Northrend, in the Broken Isles, in the Dragon Isles, and in the Verdant Wilds island.
