## Opening paragraph

Tanaan Jungle (pronounced "tuh-NAHN") is a region in eastern Draenor. It is the seat of the Iron Horde's dominion over Draenor and home to the Bleeding Hollow clan, as well as the recently constructed Dark Portal that serves as a time-space gateway between Draenor and Azeroth. The Azerothian expedition mounts a counter-offensive against Iron Horde's operations in their homeworld in a desperate bid to shut down the gateway, with heavy losses that force the remnants to escape elsewhere in order to find allies.
The peninsula serves as a primary base of operations for the Iron Horde, now part of the Burning Legion following Gul'dan's usurpation of Grommash Hellscream, whose influence permeates the region. Most of the Iron Horde resources and troops can be found there, supported by demonic forces. Mountains and orcish fortifications make the heart of this land nearly unreachable from the ground level, making it a perfect place for the Iron Horde's seat of power: the stronghold now known as Hellfire Citadel.

## History

The jungle was corrupted when Gul'dan unleashed the Felblight, a river of fel sludge flowing down from the pinnacle of the Throne of Kil'jaeden down to Hellfire Citadel, where it forms a deadly moat around the orcish fortress.

## Notes

- Earlier concept arts indicated that the original gateway to Draenor was supposed to be a "Chronal Spire", which was to be located in this zone.
- This zone is an alternate version of Hellfire Peninsula, where it never turned into the wasteland it is on Outland. Just like in the main timeline, it serves as a seat of power for the orcs in the alternate one, however, the Dark Portal ritual has stained the area around the structure with fel taint. When the Iron Horde served the Burning Legion, the demonic influence strengthened.
- Tanaan Jungle was closed off until patch 6.2. Players were prevented from entering by insurmountable cliffs around the zone's coasts and invisible walls in places where it would otherwise have been possible to walk in, such as the docks the player disembarks from at the end of The Home Stretch. There were no NPCs except for a large number of level 100 Iron Horde orcs in the northern inlet (what became Ironhold Harbor).

## Places and people this zone is about

**Fang'rila** — Fang'rila is the capital of the Blackfang tribe of saberon, located in southeastern Tanaan Jungle.

**Ironhold Harbor** — Ironhold Harbor is located in northern Tanaan Jungle.

**Lion's Watch** — Lion's Watch (or Lion's Stand) is the main Alliance base in Tanaan Jungle. It was established by the Alliance player and Exarch Yrel soon after landing in Tanaan Jungle to begin the final offensive against Gul'dan and the Iron Horde. The base is well fortified, with numerous cannon positions located around it.

**Ruins of Kra'nak** — The Ruins of Kra'nak is an area in north-western Tanaan Jungle. Once housing Sethekk Arakkoa, its denizens have since joined the Shadow Council with the help of Shadow-Lord Iskar.

**Rangari Refuge** — Rangari Refuge is a Rangari outpost located in southwestern Tanaan Jungle, near the Iron Front. Besides the Rangari, it also houses several Laughing Skull refugees.

**Ironmonger's Hold** — Ironmonger's Hold is located in Tanaan Jungle.

**Iron Horde** — The Iron Horde (later referred to by some of its members as the Fel Horde and Gul'dan's Horde) was formed initially as a union of the uncorrupted orcish clans of a parallel Draenor and created by Garrosh Hellscream and his father, Grommash Hellscream, who was convinced by his unknown son's (who was never born in the al…

**Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.

**Frostwolf Orcs** — The Frostwolf Orcs, or Frostwolf clan, are renowned for their prowess as one-on-one combatants. Once led by Chieftain Durotan in alternate Draenor, they are a strong, close-knit clan whose bond with wolves only adds to their strength—though their numbers remain smaller than most clans.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Hand of the Prophet** — The Hand of the Prophet is the main Alliance force in Tanaan Jungle on alternate Draenor.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.
