## Opening paragraph

Hrothgar's Landing is a small island located off the northern coast of Icecrown, in Northrend. The Sea Reaver's Run is the strait between Hrothgar's Landing and the continent.
The adventurers who have risen in the ranks of the Argent Tournament, and were awarded with the title of <Crusader> by the Argent Crusade, were directed to the island with a new series of quests surrounding the kvaldir's taking of the island from the tuskarr.

## History

There was once a tuskarr village here, until it was conquered by Drottinn Hrothgar and his kvaldir who renamed it "Hrothgar's Landing", while its inhabitants have all been slain or driven away by the kvaldir.

During the war against the Lich King in 27 ADP, the island became a mist-shrouded staging point from which the kvaldir, or "sea vrykul" as they are often known as, staged attacks against the Silver Covenant and the Sunreavers' ships on the Sea Reaver's Run. Their ships fought the kvaldir back from Icecrown's main shore and had to slay the kraken they kept summoning, with the help of adventurers.

After the war against the Jailer in 36 ADP, the Bronzebeard brothers reported that the island is still under the control of the kvaldir, and their deepcallers are still bringing up krakens to attack any ship that tries to pass through the strait.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Drottinn Hrothgar** — Drottinn Hrothgar is a kvaldir raider who has claimed Hrothgar's Landing from the local tuskarr. Adventurers are tasked to kill him in the <Crusader>-level daily quest  [25-30 Daily] Drottinn Hrothgar.

**Kvaldir** — The kvaldir (sometimes capitalized, and pronounced KUH-vall-deer), also known as sea vrykul, are the spirits of fearsome vrykul warriors or seafaring raiders who roam the seas raiding ships and coastal settlements.

**Kalu'ak** — The Kalu'ak (meaning "tuskarr" in their language) is a neutral tribe of tuskarr.

**Hrothgar's Landing** — Hrothgar's Landing is a small island located off the northern coast of Icecrown, in Northrend. The Sea Reaver's Run is the strait between Hrothgar's Landing and the continent.

**Silver Covenant** — The Silver Covenant was a militant faction of high elves founded and led by Ranger-General Vereesa Windrunner. They rejected the admission of the blood elves into the Kirin Tor, and took it upon themselves to serve as a military deterrent for any potential Horde uprising in Dalaran City.

**Sunreavers** — The Sunreavers were a faction of blood elves who represented the Horde in Dalaran. Led by Archmage Aethas Sunreaver, they joined the Horde Expedition in Northrend and sought to have their people readmitted into the Kirin Tor, a plight they were successful in.

**Mistcaller's Cave** — Mistcaller's Cave is a cave on the western side of Hrothgar's Landing.  Mistcaller Yngvar has taken up residence here and from this cave he manipulates the mists to conceal the Kvaldir and the sea beasts they command.

**Bor's Fury** — Bor's Fury is a kvaldir raiding longship found anchored in the mist off the southeast shore of Hrothgar's Landing, manned by a Kvaldir Mist Binder. Ornolf the Scarred can also be summoned to the ship by Crusaders on the quest  [25-30 Daily] Ornolf The Scarred.
