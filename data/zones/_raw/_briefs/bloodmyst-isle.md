## Opening paragraph

Bloodmyst Isle, once named Silvergale by the draenei prior the sickness spread across the land, and before they saw the full damage their crash had caused, is one of the two main islands that make up the Azuremyst Isles. Located off the coast of northern Kalimdor and north of Azuremyst Isle, the island has been ravaged by the radiation from the crashed draenei dimensional ship, the Exodar.
After the vessel's fall on Azeroth, smaller crystals rained down and buried themselves deep in the earth, while the toxic blight stained everything blood red, shaping the Bloodmyst Isle of today. Red crystals now dot the land, and even the great pine trees covering it have taken on a crimson tinge, while the wildlife has been mutated into hostile creatures.

## History

===Early history===
thumb|The Ruins of Loreth'Aran on [[Wyrmscar Island.]]
As part of the Azuremyst Isles, the island was long ago a mountainous forest, located on the western edge of ancient Kalimdor. According to draenei historians, the land was mostly untamed by the Kaldorei Empire who left it unsettled on purpose, so that hungry beasts could feed off the land and hunters could trap the choicest prey. Despite being untamed, it was populated by night elves, centered in the large city of Loreth'Aran. Blessed by the green dragonflight and its Aspect Ysera, the Dragon Riders of Loreth'Aran rode powerful green dragons into battle and lived alongside them.

During the Dragon Wars, however, the elven city was destroyed when the black dragonflight descended upon the unwary night elves and green dragons. Led by the fearsome Razormaw, a lieutenant of Deathwing, the black dragons wiped out Loreth'Aran and its defenders in a savage maelstrom of flame and death. The city's destruction was made complete by the Great Sundering, as the ocean swept in and the earth erupted, leaving only a few remaining ruins scattered across separate islands. Wyrmscar Island became home to the restless spirits of slain green whelplings and the ghost of Prince Toreth.

In 6 ADP, the Lost Hope ship commanded by Captain Edward Hanes was sunk on the Bloodcursed Reef by the bloodcurse, trapping his spirit on Wyrmscar Island.

===The Burning Crusade===

left|thumb|The Vector Coil, the power core from the [[Exodar.]]
In Year 26 ADP, when the Exodar crashed on Azeroth and ended its course on the Azuremyst Isles, the damaged vessel scattered power crystals across the island, an event the Stillpine furbolgs called the "crystal storm". Blood-like liquid soon seeped from the crystals into the ground and water, while the radiations mutated both the island's fauna and flore. The ship's crash also released Outland species into the wild, including the insectoid ravager, forcing the draenei to cull them to maintain the islands' balance. The draenei shaman later reported that the water elementals were greatly agitated by the corrupted water sources, which were polluted by the Vector Coil's leaking fluids.

The outpost of Blood Watch was set up by the Hand of Argus to find a way to cleanse Bloodmyst's corruption, as well as fight the dangerous creatures loose on the island.

While they showed few signs of mutation at first, the Royal Blue Flutterers' corruption got progressively worse, to the point where they damaged nearly everything they came in contact with. After some time, they even began to amplify the damage being done by the crystal shards, leaving the draenei with no other choice but to cull their population.

In time, Amberweb Pass became the lair of a huge spider called Zarakh, whose spawn grew to unnatural sizes due to contamination by the spaceship debris.

The water elemental Aqueous eventually discovered that the aquatic corruption was the by-product of Tel'athion the Impure, a demented researcher, who produced the filth that had been seeping into the island's water. After the shaman heroes destroyed the cache of contaminated barrels, they decapitated the mad blood elf, putting in motion the restoration of the water on Bloodmyst Isle over time.

During their offensive against the draenei, the Sunhawk blood elves were led by the man'ari eredar Sironas, who planned to use the ship's Vector Coil to create a Sun Gate and receive Sunfury reinforcements from Outland. Seeking to reclaim the Exodar and corrupt draenei into man'ari, the group was ultimately disbanded by Alliance adventurers sent by the Hand of Argus.

===Exploring Azeroth===

After the Fourth War in 34 ADP, when the Horde Council sent Zekhan and Rexxar to look over their holdings across Kalimdor, Velen extended them an invitation to the Azuremyst Isles, where they were assigned an escort consisting of Ya'rika and Kea'ton while visiting the Exodar and the region. Despite their efforts, the draenei couldn't clear away the toxic blight that had been spoiled over both islands. Upon visiting Bloodmyst Isle, Zekhan reported that the beasts of Bloodmyst were badly weakened from the toxins, and that not much wildlife has been spared from the illness over the years.

===Dragonflight===

thumb|Velens across Bloodmyst Isle with their magics.]]
In Year 40 ADP, while the Exodar was prepared for the Tishamaat, the Nazzivus satyrs attacked draenei pilgrims traveling for the celebrations. Rather than mobilizing the vindicators and alarm his people in the process, Prophet Velen gathered Hatuun, Arzaal, and the draenei adventurer at his side to strop the threat. Upon arriving, Arzaal expressed that the island's corruption felt strange, making it difficult to cleanse. When Hatuun questioned why the corruption was running rampant, Velen admitted that he hadn't foreseen it and had pulled the Rangari out of the region in order to protect them. The group eventually discovered that demons had been spreading fel, keeping the island corrupted and preventing it from healing even years after the Exodars crash. After the group defeated Sironas and the satyrs, they Light-empowered an Arkonite Generator to cleanse hundreds of fel spreaders across the island. With the corruption removed, Velen claimed that Bloodmyst could now finally begin to heal.

## Geography

Bloodmyst Isle lies off the west coast of northern Kalimdor. The island is divided into two sections by a narrow strip of water. The smaller section, which is connected by a bridge to Azuremyst Isle, is yet untainted by the red mist that covers the main part of the island. There are no instanced dungeons or battlegrounds on Bloodmyst Isle. The micro dungeon, the Vector Coil, can be found here. Blood Watch serves as the zone's travel hub, connecting it to Azuremyst Isle.

## Notes and trivia

- On the uncorrupted part of the island grows a bright, juicy fruit called the sand pear, which is favored by the elekk.
- Bloodmyst Isle has at least a couple of references to Star Wars. The flight master at Blood Watch is named Laando and there is a quest called The Kessel Run.
- The crystal contamination could be a reference to Tiberium from the Command and Conquer series, with both infesting and causing mutations to the flora and fauna.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Blood Watch** — Blood Watch is a Hand of Argus forward outpost and the main draenei settlement on Bloodmyst Isle. It is made from large repurposed pods and crystal fragments from the Exodar.

**Kessel's Crossing** — Kessel's Crossing is a draenei encampment located in Bloodmyst Isle. This nearby area has not yet been corrupted by the blood crystals from the Exodar's crashing.

**Axxarien** — Axxarien is a satyr outpost located in northern Bloodmyst Isle, home of the sect with the same name.

**Nazzivian** — Nazzivian is a satyr encampment located in southwestern Bloodmyst Isle. It serves as the base of operation for the Nazzivus sect.

**Exodar (nation)** — Exodar is a nation of draenei and a member state of the Alliance. Following the ill-fated landing of the Exodar, the dimensional ship they used to escape Outland, the draenei have worked feverishly to use its remains as their capital city on the Azuremyst Isles.

**Bristlelimb tribe** — The Bristlelimb tribe is a tribe of furbolgs found in both Azuremyst Isle and Bloodmyst Isle. They are led by High Chief Bristlelimb.

**Bloodcursed** — The Bloodcursed are a group of naga found on the Bloodcursed Reef of Wyrmscar Island and Bloodcurse Isle, both part of Bloodmyst Isle, where they are led by Atoph the Bloodcursed.

**Nazzivus** — Nazzivus (or the Nazzivian) is a sect of satyrs based in the camp of Nazzivian in Bloodmyst Isle.

**Ragefeather wildkin** — The Ragefeather wildkin are a group of wildkin that inhabits Ragefeather Ridge on Bloodmyst Isle. They have been corrupted by the Exodar's crystals, while the Royal Blue Flutterers helped spread it even further.

**Wrathscale** — The Wrathscale are a group of naga found in night elven ruins across Azuremyst Isle and Bloodmyst Isle.

**Sunhawk** — The Sunhawks were a group of blood elves loyal to Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider who commandeered the Exodar before the draenei, led by Prophet Velen, reclaimed it. **Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.
