## Opening paragraph

Krokuun is a region of Argus where the armies of Azeroth first landed during their quest to stop the Burning Legion. Kil'jaeden's Legion ship has crashed here near the Xenedar.

## Geography

The once prosperous eredar civilizations that dotted these hills have crumbled away and vanished under the Legion's defilement. Jagged, dark rocks protrude from the fel tainted groves. The Legion's own creations stick out of the land almost like the rocks themselves. Trees, vitiated of any living essence are strewn throughout the land. Almost no flora has endured the planet's harsh conditions. Only black soil, devoid of life, covers the planet.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Xenedar** — The Xenedar is a dimensional ship belonging to the Army of the Light and their former headquarters. It split off from the Genedar some time after the draenei fled Argus.

**Krokul Hovel** — Krokul Hovel is a small enclave in Krokuun where the Broken resistance led by Chieftain Hatuun took refuge. This is where many of the broken slaves from Darkfall Ridge have hidden after being freed during the initial invasion of Argus.

**Destiny Point** — Destiny Point is a small camp that serves as a base for the Army of the Light assault on Nath'raxas Hold.

**Darkfall Ridge** — Darkfall Ridge is a large subzone in Krokuun. The area was once inhabited by the peaceful eredar, but had been taken over by the foul creatures of the Legion. Siegemaster Voraan is the leader of the army here. This is where heroes of Azeroth first land during their quest to invade Argus.

**Nath'raxas Hold** — Nath'raxas Hold is a fortified city in Krokuun. Nath'raxas Spire and the Court of the Avenger make up the inner keep.

**Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.

**Army of the Light** — The Grand Army of the Light (also called the Golden Army, the Army of Light, the Light's Army, or the Lightforged) is a faction of enlightened races led by the naaru who rose against the Burning Legion.

**Argussian Reach** — The Argussian Reach, also known as the Krokul or the Darkfallen, is a faction associated to the Broken of Argus based in Krokul Hovel.

**Vindicaar** — The Vindicaar (pronounced VIN-di-kar) is a space-faring vessel, similar in design to the naaru dimensional ships, created by the draenei of the Exodar in order to return to their homeworld of Argus.

**Lightforged beacon** — Lightforged beacons are teleportation devices used by the Army of the Light on Argus. After the Argus Campaign, the beacons have become a part of the Alliance.

**Alleria Windrunner** — Alleria Windrunner (/əˈlɪɹiə/ ə-LEER-ee-ə or /əˈlɛɹ.i.ə/ ə-LAIR-ee-ə) is the leader of the void elves, a hero of the Alliance, and the oldest of the Windrunner sisters.

**Argus Wastes** — The Argus Wastes are the edges of Krokuun. They contain areas that have little Legion presence, although the wyrmtongues know how to find the wealth the area contains.
