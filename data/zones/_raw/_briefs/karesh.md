## Opening paragraph

K'aresh (/kɑːˈɹɛʃ/ kah-RESH) is the original homeworld of the ethereals and brokers. It was an arid planet, home to a thriving ecosystem and several sentient species until it was destroyed in the Devouring War, forcing the K'areshi into exodus across the Twisting Nether, the Shadowlands, and on shattered remnants surrounding the planet's husk. Most K'areshi believed Dimensius the All-Devouring was responsible for their planet's destruction. In truth, the Ravel shattered the planet in an attempt to kill the void lord before he could consume K'aresh, knowing the planet was doomed either way. Like Azeroth, K'aresh had a worldsoul, which was referred to in the masculine sense. It had twin suns named Meter and Ti'meter that gave the sky a saffron blood-orange sky, which was consumed by Dimensius along with both suns.

## History

K'areshi
Prior to Dimensius's arrival, the world of K'aresh was an arid planet, home to a thriving ecosystem and several sentient species, including the K'areshi. Led by High Priest Salhadaar and the Council of Oracles, their society was composed of a variety of city-states throughout the desert world, such as Tazavesh which was ruled by Bilaal, Ma'nussa, ruled by Ky'veza, along with Gastalt, Dervashna, Tingarla, and Sufaad. There were also tribes of nomads that wandered the desert, stopping in cities to trade and put on shows. The Oracles communed with K'aresh's worldsoul through a ritual called the Tabiqa that allowed them to reaffirm their oaths with him and hear his song.

===The Doom of K'aresh===

thumb|upright|A mural depicting the ancient K'areshi and a city under one of their shields.
Many thousands of years ago (perhaps a hundred thousand or more), Dimensius invaded K'aresh, beginning an apocalyptic conflict that the K'areshi would call the Devouring War. According to Locus-Walker, shortly before the invasion, his people began receiving a vision similar to the Radiant Song. By the time the people realized that their world was warning them of the coming danger, it was already too late. How Dimensius found K'aresh is still hotly debated among the surviving ethereals, but the effects of his coming were unmistakable: he opened countless gateways into the Void around the planet, bathing K'aresh in dark energies. The K'areshi hastily attempted to construct magical barriers around their cities, powered by the Reshii Ribbons which could convert all forms of energy, including the Void, into arcane.

Although the dark energies were blocked, the prolonged exposure of arcane energy from the shields tore away the K'areshi's corporeal shells and infused their souls with enough energy to transform them into energy beings. This altered state proved to be a blessing in disguise, however, as their enhanced minds and magical abilities allowed the ethereals to fight Dimensius and his limited forces to a standstill. Over the years, however, Dimensius eventually grew powerful enough to summon armies of fellow void creatures, forcing the K'areshi to flee into the Twisting Nether and Shadowlands, becoming ethereals and brokers. Some ethereals continued to live on the shattered remains of the planet, becoming known as Wastelanders.

While many, including most of the ethereals and brokers, believe that K'aresh was destroyed by Dimensius, he did not actually succeed. Seeing that their home was going to be lost either way, the K'areshi Locus-Walker and Nari, along with Dimensius's own harbinger Xal'atath, made the decision to destroy the planet in order to kill Dimensius, preventing him from devouring K'aresh's worldsoul. It was the only way for the K'areshi to survive. Fragments of Dimensius were scattered across the Great Dark Beyond, and the ethereals and brokers would believe that their planet's destruction was his fault.

Some ethereals still continued to live on the shattered remains of the planet, becoming known as Wastelanders. They were led by Soul-Scribe and sworn to Nexus-King Salhadaar. During the final days of the Devouring War, a blue mineral began to surface and was called the Blood of K'aresh.

===The Burning Crusade===

During the invasion of Outland in 26 ADP, the Ethereum still kept a presence on the remains of K'aresh, maintaining a connection to Outland in Bash'ir Landing. Some of the Void creatures who had served under Dimensius during the Devouring War were bound in service to the scattered ethereals, including Nexus-Prince Shaffar's independent faction operating in the Mana Tombs of Auchindoun.

===Cataclysm===

Following the Cataclysm in 28 ADP, the mage Umbranse the Spiritspeaker opened several portals within Mazthoril, which were connected to a number of worlds across both the Great Dark Beyond and Twisting Nether, including K'aresh. Hostile ethereals of unknown affiliation who still resided on the broken world used these portals to access Azeroth. An adventurer was tasked by the blue dragon Haleh to seal these portals, closing off the connection between worlds.

===The War Within===

War on K'aresh
thumb|left|The remains of K'aresh and the Manaforge Omega.
After her time in the Maw and Zereth Mortis, Ve'nari's vision of restoring K'aresh had become a prevailing idea among the brokers, gaining her enough influence to take over Cartel Om and enough fortune to outright purchase Tazavesh as a shipping port, making it a recreation center for Project Eco-Dome -- a revitalization effort led by Ve'nari to restore K'aresh with modified eco-domes purchased from and constructed by the Consortium. With her newfound societal and financial power, Ve'nari founded the K'aresh Trust, reuniting ethereals and brokers for the first time in millenia through the union of various broker cartels, along with the ethereals of the Consortium and Protectorate, with the common purpose of revitalizing the remains of their common homeworld. While Tazavesh was moved back to K'aresh, Nexus-Prince Azir took the Dark Heart to Manaforge Omega, a colossal Shadowguard facility, and began the process to resurrect the void lord Dimensius, whose voice could be heard across the broken world.

During the course of the war on K'aresh, Xal'atath entered into an uneasy alliance with the K'aresh Trust, and discovered that the Wastelanders' true desire was to hear the song of K'aresh's worldsoul once more, as they had once been the Oracles of the Worldsoul. After they succeeded to hear K'aresh' song through a ritual, Soul-Scribe pledged her Wastelanders forces to the restoration of the world and surrendered her Reshii Ribbons to the adventurers. The Trust forces stationed at Shan'dorah later led a counterattack on Shadow Point, where the Shadowguard forces outside Manaforge Omega were stationed, feeding energy from K'aresh itself into Dimensius within the Manaforge.

## Notes and trivia

- K'aresh's solar system had two suns, Meter and Ti'meter. Both were consumed by the Void Lord Dimensius.
- Before the two suns were named in The Doom of K'aresh, they were first mentioned in The Burning Crusade by the Protectorate Demolitionist.
- Before the Void arrived, K'aresh's skies were described as saffron and blood-orange. The Void's arrival turned the skies purple and dark.
- The demonym for K'aresh is K'areshi.
- "Thank K'aresh!" is sometimes used as an interjection to express gratitude or relief.
- Nexus-Princess Ky'veza's loot comes from a treasure chest called Spoils of K'aresh.
- In hs, as an arid planet, K'aresh was also home to creatures that became energy-camels.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Ve'nari** — Ve'nari (/vɛˈnɑːri/ veh-NAH-ree), originally named just Nari, is a broker who seeks to restore K'aresh. Once a K'areshi member of the Ravel, she served as an agent of then High Priest Salhadaar.

**K'aresh Trust** — The K'aresh Trust is the seventh Major Faction of The War Within. The trust is based out of Tazavesh after it has relocated to the remains of K'aresh. It consists of the broker cartels: Ba, Au, So, Xy, Zo, and Om, as well as ethereals from the Consortium.

**Wastelanders** — The Wastelanders are a group of ethereals who have sworn an oath to Nexus-King Salhadaar to destroy the Ravel for what they did to K'aresh. Once known as the K'areshi Oracles prior to the Devouring War, they lead a harsh existence on the shattered planet of K'aresh where survival is a continuous struggle.

**Ethereum** — The Ethereum are a faction of ethereals who once served as the ruling class of K'aresh, the ethereals' homeworld. They swore vengeance upon Dimensius the All-Devouring after the void lord consumed their world.

**Shadowguard** — The Shadowguard are a faction of ethereals who have heeded the call of the Void and become void ethereals. They serve as Nexus-King Salhadaar's military division.

**Ravel** — The Ravel (/ˈrævəl/ RAV-əl) was an organization made of the brightest minds in K'aresh who sought to save their world from Dimensius during the Devouring War. Most K'areshi believe the void lord was responsible for their planet's destruction.

**Council of Oracles** — The Council of Oracles was a group of elite K'areshi Oracles who constituted the ruling authority of K'aresh prior to its destruction, and its members guided the K'areshi people through all facets of life.

**Manaforge Omega** — Manaforge Omega is the third and final raid of The War Within, added in patch 11.2.0 as part of The War Within Season 3. It is located on K'aresh at Shadow Point in the northwestern corner of the zone.

**Eco-Dome Al'dani** — Eco-Dome Al'dani is a dungeon in The War Within, added in patch 11.2.0. According to Ve'nari, it is one of the most important Eco-Domes. It was built upon the former site of the K'areshi's most sacred grounds, the High Temple of K'aresh.

**Oasis** — The Oasis  is the primary eco-dome located on K'aresh, at the northern end of the eastern island. Within this, Project Eco-Dome is carried out by Ve'nari and Cartel Om with the backing of the K'aresh Trust. Most of the  [Ecological Succession] storyline plays out here.

**Azeroth** — Azeroth (/ˈæz.əˌɹɒθ/ AZ-ə-roth) is the name of the world in which the majority of the Warcraft series is set. At its core dwells a slumbering worldsoul, the nascent spirit of a titan.

**Auchindoun** — Auchindoun (/ˈɒ.kɪnˌduːn/ OCK-in-doon) is a mausoleum, a "city of the dead", a former draenei holy site where the dead were laid to rest, placed in the ground. It is located in the middle of the Bone Wastes in the Terokkar Forest within Outland.
