## Opening paragraph

Northern Stranglethorn (also referred to as Stranglethorn Jungle and Northern Stranglethorn Vale) is a zone in southern Eastern Kingdoms. It is part of the greater Stranglethorn Vale region, separated from the Cape of Stranglethorn by The Sundering. This dense, tropical and resource-filled jungle has holdings of many factions whose agendas conflict or intertwine in the zone's story. The jungle troll instance Zul'Gurub is located here.

## History

Stranglethorn Vale
Northern Stranglethorn is a new zone in Cataclysm as the old Stranglethorn Vale was split in half after the Shattering. At the bottom of this zone, the remaining whirlpool can be seen. Players learn about the political changes in Zul'Gurub, rescue an adorable raptor, and partake in Nesingwary's expanded hunting operations.

## Notes and trivia

thumb|The Deadmines water gate, visible from the cove near Yojamba Isle.
- If you travel to a small cove north of Yojamba Isle, you can see a large water gate which leads to the docks inside the Deadmines. However, you cannot enter or leave through this. Interestingly, inside the Deadmines this gate exists to the north, but outside the exit of the gate is to the south.
- For the quest Bwemba's Spirit, the southern part of the area, around Fort Livingston is phased, with the area besieged by level 83 Jungle Serpents, and poisoned NPCs!

## Places and people this zone is about

**Grom'gol Base Camp** — Grom'gol Base Camp (or Grom'Gol) is an orcish camp on the southwestern coast of Northern Stranglethorn. Led by Commander Aggro'gosh, the outpost provides a safe chain of supply to Stonard in the Swamp of Sorrows.

**Fort Livingston** — Fort Livingston is a new Alliance camp in Northern Stranglethorn.

**Bambala** — Bambala is a Horde encampment just outside Zul'Gurub in Northern Stranglethorn. It was founded after the Zandalari tribe departed from Yojamba Isle. Notably, it is the first Darkspear village in Stranglethorn Vale since the tribe departed in exile to the Darkspear Islands.

**Kurzen's Compound** — Kurzen's Compound is an unusual human settlement located east of the Rebel Camp in Northern Stranglethorn. Occupied by Kurzen's Expedition, once led by Colonel Kurzen, until his death, the expedition now takes orders from Chief Anders, Chief Gaulus, Chief Miranda, and Chief Esquivel. 

**Gurubashi tribe** — The Gurubashi tribe (/ˈɡʊ.ruˌbɑː.ʃi/ GOO-roo-BAH-shee) is the remnant of the Gurubashi Empire and is the strongest tribe of jungle trolls. They reside in their capital of Zul'Gurub.

**Bloodscalp tribe** — The Bloodscalp are a tribe (or a clan) of jungle trolls that split off from the Gurubashi Empire after the defeat of Hakkar the Soulflayer. The various tribes claimed territories in the vast jungles of Stranglethorn Vale but quickly began fighting one another.

**Stormwind (kingdom)** — The Kingdom of Stormwind (also called Kingdom of Azeroth) is one of the seven human nations in the Eastern Kingdoms, which controls much of the southern continent of Azeroth, from Elwynn Forest and Westfall to Duskwood and the Redridge Mountains.

**Alliance** — The Alliance (also called the Grand Alliance) is one of two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Horde which the Alliance has traditionally been at war with.

**Darkspear tribe** — The Darkspear tribe are a tribe (or a clan) of jungle trolls (occasionally called island trolls) part of the Horde. During Warchief Thrall's exodus from Lordaeron in the Third War, the Horde rescued the Darkspears from the mysterious naga sea witch Zar'jira.

**Horde** — The Horde (also called the New Horde or Thrall's Horde) is one of the two major political factions of the mortal races in Azeroth, its counterpart being the Alliance which the Horde has traditionally been at war with.

**Venture Company** — The Venture Company or Venture Trading Company, often shortened to simply Venture Co., is a goblin-run cartel notorious for plundering Azeroth's natural resources. Part of the Trade Coalition, the company specializes in gathering raw materials.

**Kurzen's Expedition** — Kurzen's Expedition was an expedition of the Stormwind Army led by Colonel Conrad Kurzen in Northern Stranglethorn.
