## Opening paragraph

The Wandering Isle is the starting area of the playable pandaren in Mists of Pandaria. It is a massive island on the back of a giant turtle named Shen-zin Su, which is inhabited by curious pandaren and/or their descendants who left their homeland of Pandaria centuries after the Great Sundering, in order to discover the other lands and races of Azeroth. The Isle has been weaving about the world's oceans ever since.
After the Burning Legion destroyed the Peak of Serenity in Kun-Lai Summit, the monks of the Order of the Broken Temple relocated to the Wandering Isle.

## History

===Origins===

thumb|Shen-zin Su as seen in [[Pearl of Pandaria.]]
The descendants of Liu Lang's followers, whom Shen-zin Su would dub the "grandchildren" of Liu Lang, still travel on the great turtle's shell to see the world. The turtle has continued to grow since Liu Lang's time to a truly massive size, supporting several villages, forest, and small mountain ranges. The settlers would appropriately call the sea turtle's shell the "Wandering Isle".

Ever since Wugou, the spirit of earth, fell asleep, the dirt of the Dai-Lo Farmstead started drying out and got harder and harder to get new seeds to sprout.

Some daring pandaren explorers such as Chen and Li Li Stormstout left the Isle to explore the greater world.

===Mists of Pandaria===

In Year 30 ADP, Shen-zin Su was swimming as usual in the seas, until the Alliance Skyseeker gunship crashed into his flank because of the thick mist. This injured the giant turtle and caused him to no longer swim straight, dragging him toward the Maelstrom. The island inhabitants felt the change and populations who usually kept to themselves, such as the local Amberleaf sprites and Fe-Feng tribe of hozen, understood something bad was coming, so they started giving trouble to the local pandaren, notably by stealing their supplies.

Sensing that something was abnormal, Master Shang Xi gave his disciples, Ji Firepaw and Aysa Cloudsinger, the task of finding and unite the four ancient elemental spirits, which would help the pandaren talk to Shen-zin Su, a feat not done in centuries.

Realizing that the source of Shen-zin Su's pain was the Skyseeker, Master Shang Xi's disciples headed into the forbidden Pei-Wu Forest. There, the party helped triage members of the Horde and Alliance, as well as fend off the Deepscale saurok from the depths. With the immediate threat taken care of, Ji Firepaw decided to blow up what was left of the Skyseeker, and shockingly, this plan actually worked to a point.

===Legion===

During the third invasion of the Burning Legion in 32 ADP, demonic forces led by Lady Keletress attacked the Peak of Serenity in Kun-Lai Summit, while the Wandering Isle happened to be traveling nearby. Overwhelmed, the monks evacuated to the island and settled at the Temple of Five Dawns, where they founded the Order of the Broken Temple under the leadership of their new Grandmaster.

When a Legion command ship appeared on the horizon, it was encountered by the monk champions who defeated its commander, Adjunct Telios, before she could attack the residents of the Wandering Isle. Lady Keletress eventually launched her last assault against the Broken Temple, with her demonic army invading the back of the great turtle. However, with the power of the Storm Brew, the Grandmaster managed to slay her, while the demons were driven away by the monks.

At some point, Varagol the Coercer poisoned the Celebration Brew and sent it to the isle, in order to weaken the Broken Temple. With the help of Brewer Almai, and the spirit of Brewmaster Blanche, a cure was eventually made and the affected monks were able to recover from the fel poison.

===Pandaren Heritage===

Centuries after he left Pandaria, Shen-zin Su returned to the waters near his home during the Spirit Festival held by the pandaren who lived on him.

Once characters leave the Wandering Isle, they are ineligible for a return journey. Non-pandaren are prevented from ever entering this version of the island, much like the Lost Isles and Kezan were only part of the leveling experience of goblin characters.

Monks of all races later establish the Wandering Isle as their Class Hall for the Order of the Broken Temple. This version of the Isle exists in a separate phase, not interfering with the pandaren starting experience. Notably, the Wreck of the Skyseeker is absent from the new version of the map.

After completing the pandaren heritage armor questline with a pandaren character on your account, non-pandaren can travel to the island by taking a hot-air balloon from Stormwind or Orgrimmar.

## Notes

- "Pandowan" is a term for young pandaren who are training on the Wandering Isle.
- This is the only starting zone where no quests award any equipment of higher quality than Common.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Shang Xi Training Grounds** — The Shang Xi Training Grounds is the initial spawning location for newly-created pandaren. Located on the northeast portion of Shen-zin Su, the Wandering Isle, adventurers learn under the tutelage of Master Shang Xi. The place is a monastery and has its own small temple.

**Mandori Village** — Mandori Village is a pandaren village found just south of the Temple of Five Dawns on the Wandering Isle. This village protects the gates to the forbidden Pei-Wu Forest, gates that do not open until   [1-20] Bidden to Greatness. It contains the Laughing Crane tavern.

**Dai-Lo Farmstead** — The Dai-Lo Farmstead is a large farm in the southeastern area of the Wandering Isle. The local virmen have been stealing the farmstead's crops as of late.

**Morning Breeze Village** — Morning Breeze Village is a pandaren village found west of the Temple of Five Dawns on the Wandering Isle.

**Forlorn Hut** — The Forlorn Hut is a pandaren crafted hut built in the Pei-Wu Forest by Wei Palerage. Many of the Horde survivors from the Skyseeker crash ended up here. Several crewmen are injured, while others are quickly gathering up bamboos in order to craft crude weapons. Horde druids are also trying to heal the wounded.

**Ki-Han Brewery** — Ki-Han Brewery is a small brewery located in the Rows west of the Dai-Lo Farmstead on the Wandering Isle. Raggis, a large virmen, has chased away the workers who had been busy repairing the old structure.

**Fe-Feng Village** — Fe-Feng Village is a Fe-Feng hozen village found just to the northwest of Morning Breeze Village on the Wandering Isle. Ruk-Ruk leads the hozen on this isle.

**Shang Xi's Academy** — Shang Xi's Academy is the in-game faction of starting playable pandaren, who start their adventure on the Wandering Isle of Shen-zin Su.

**Order of the Broken Temple** — The Order of the Broken Temple (also referred to as the Broken Temple) is an order of monks founded after the destruction of the Peak of Serenity in Pandaria. **Fe-Feng tribe** — The Fe-Feng are a tribe of hozen located on the Wandering Isle.

**Plump** — The Plumps are a brood of virmen found in the Wandering Isle, in the Rows and at the Teeming Burrow.

**Amberleaf** — The Amberleaf forest sprites are found on the Wandering Isle. They interrupted a meditation of Aysa Cloudsinger in the Cave of Meditation and overturned a supply cart in Dawning Valley on its way to the training grounds.
