## Opening paragraph

The Isle of Quel'Danas (/ˈkwɛlˌdə.nɑs/ KWEL də-nahs) (also known as the Sunwell Isle or simply Quel'Danas) is an island located north of the Eastern Kingdoms and one of three areas that make up Quel'Thalas. It is the home of the Sunwell and Magisters' Terrace.

## History

===Creation of the Sunwell===
Sunwell
thumb|upright|Dath'Remar Sunstrider and the [[Highborne creating Sunwell Plateau.]]
Around 6,800 BDP, the Highborne exiles under the leadership of Dath'Remar Sunstrider arrived at the forested woodlands one day known as Quel'Thalas. Dath'Remar had long sought a suitable homeland for his people, practitioners of arcane magic abhorred by their brethren across the Great Sea. Unveiling a stolen vial of water from the Well of Eternity, he poured its contents into a lake situated at a convergence of powerful ley lines, the Sunwell Grove. A brilliant fount of energy tore through the skies of Azeroth, and Dath'Remar proclaimed that this new source of power would one day surpass its predecessor. The Sunwell was named after Dath'Remar, in honor of the Highborne leader's bold quest to reignite Highborne culture, and over time, the Highborne lost their characteristic kaldorei looks and shunned the moon to embrace the sun. They would become known as the high elves, or quel'dorei, and the Sunwell became the heart of their culture. The Sunwell's power allowed the elves to build their cities and shape their land, as well as defend their city; during the Second War, the elven Magisters notably used its power to erect a shield around Silvermoon City to hold off the Old Horde's invasion.

===Fall of Quel'Thalas===

thumb|left|The Isle of Quel'Danas prior to the Third War.
During the Third War in 20 ADP, when the corrupted Prince Arthas Menethil led the Scourge in its rampage through Quel'Thalas. During the siege of Silvermoon City, Grand Magister Belo'vir Salonar came to acknowledge that defending the capital was impossible, and tasked Archmage Rommath with the evacuation, instructing him to transport as many elves as he could to the safety of Quel'Danas.

Overwhelmed, High King Anasterian Sunstrider and the survivors gathered aboard their fleet and retreated to Quel'Danas. Protecting the Sunwell became their primary focus; they could always rebuild their capital, but the fount of arcane magic was irreplaceable. When the Scourge shattered the capital's defenses, Arthas used the power of Frostmourne to create a bridge of ice linking Quel'Danas to the mainland, giving the elves little time to marshal a counter-attack. On the southern shores of Quel'Danas, the wielder of Quel'Delar, Thalorien Dawnseeker, made a last stand and held off waves of Scourge so his men could safely reach the Sunwell. He was killed by the death knight Morlen Coldgrip, and the legendary elven blade was lost for the time being.

In his relentless advance, Arthas was confronted by Anasterian who used Felo'melorn to destroy the path of frost with his fiery magics, forcing the undead to the ocean. After a fierce duel, however, the Lich King's champion ultimately shattered Felo'melorn and pierced the king's heart to steal his soul. With his most powerful obstacles all removed, the dark prince began his final assault on the Sunwell Plateau. Some of the Scourge split off from the main army to attack Sunstrider Isle, with nothing else left in sight to destroy, while their main forces were slaughtering the population and several prominent figures, including High Priest Vandellor, Grand Magister Belo'vir, and the Convocation of Silvermoon.

Victorious, Arthas reached the Sunwell and submerged Kel'Thuzad's remains within the fount of power, resurrecting the necromancer into a lich. However, it came at great cost, as the ritual befouled the Sunwell with corrupted energies that gradually permeated the lands of Quel'Thalas, the surviving quel'dorei, and everything they had ever built. The death knight then gathered the Scourge and marched south toward Dalaran, leaving only ruins and death in his wake.  The brothers Solanar and Falon later managed to secure the king's body during the battle, taking it away from the carnage and sparing Anasterian the fate of undeath.

When Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider returned from Dalaran, he discovered that the greatest danger to his people was not the Scourge remnants, but the Sunwell itself. Given enough time, the necromantic energies would kill them all, and the prince painfully concluded that the Sunwell, the heart of their society, had to be destroyed. With the aid of the most powerful remaining magi, who had come to the same conclusion, the prince executed a plan that would irrevocably change the fate of his people, and succeeded in destroying the Sunwell through a great ritual. After a three-way struggle between the elves, the Scourge remnants, and an armada of emboldened Amani trolls seizing an opportunity to secure the Sunwell for their own purposes, the elven magi detonated the Sunwell and escaped, leaving the undead and trolls alike to perish in the ensuing blaze.

## In the RPG

Quel'Danas is also the island refuge of the blood elf dragonhawks. It is home to the aeries where the dragonhawks and giant eagles originated and are bred for the elite windriders. Aquel'Luer'Thala is the blood elf in charge of the units stationed on the island.

===Getting there===
Players first visit the Isle of Quel'Danas during the Midnight intro experience, which is an instanced version of the new Quel'Danas, while it is under attack by Xal'atath and her Devouring Host forces, to kickstart Mn. After the intro storyline is complete, the overworld of Quel'Danas becomes freely accessible to show the aftermath of said assault. As players progress through the leveling (namely the Arator's Journey storyline) and the max-level questlines, players are directed back here to progress the story and eventually to witness the showdown in the March on Quel'Danas raid.

## Notes

- Quel'Danas already appeared in very early World of Warcraft maps as the "new elf hangout" as Silvermoon was meant to have been completely destroyed. It was featured naval routes to Valgarde.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Magisters' Terrace** — The Magisters' Terrace is a dungeon in Midnight. It will have new locations including a balcony looking over the Sunwell. The objective will be to acquire the Cynosure of Twilight, the key to unlocking the way into the Voidstorm and take the fight to Xal'atath's Devouring Host.

**Sun's Reach** — Sun's Reach is a blood elven town and the staging area for the Shattered Sun Offensive on the Isle of Quel'Danas.

**Dawnstar Village** — Dawnstar Village is a blood elven village located on the Isle of Quel'Danas.

**Sunwell** — The Sunwell is a fount of mystical power located within the Sunwell Plateau, on the Sunwell Grove of Quel'Danas. While its waters, holy to the high elves, were originally pure arcane power, it has since been restored as a fount of both arcane and Light energy.

**Quel'Thalas (kingdom)** — The kingdom of Quel'Thalas (meaning High Home in Thalassian) is the realm of the blood elves, led by Regent Lord Lor'themar Theron since the fall of the Sunstrider dynasty.

**Shattered Sun Offensive** — The Shattered Sun Offensive (abbreviated as SSO) is an army rallied by the Sha'tar naaru to prevent Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider's mad bid to use the Sunwell as a portal to summon his master, Kil'jaeden.

**Vanguard of the Light** — The Vanguard of the Light, often shortened as the Vanguard, is a coalition of champions summoned by Azeroth's worldsoul, united to oppose Xal'atath and the Devouring Host.

**Sunfury** — The Sunfury (also referred to as Kael'thas' forces and Kael'thas' army) were the armies under Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider's command after he departed from Azeroth and joined Illidan's forces in Outland. The army consisted mostly of blood elves, but also included some fel orcs and demons, numbering in the thousands.

**Burning Legion** — The Burning Legion (simply the Legion, occasionally referred to as the Legion Forces, the Burning Shadow, or the Great Burning Shadow, and the infinite army) is a vast, innumerable army of demons and corrupted races who seek to destroy all life.

**Sunwell Plateau** — The Sunwell Plateau is a raid instance located on the Isle of Quel'Danas north of Silvermoon City.

**Silvermoon City** — Silvermoon City (or the City of Silvermoon and just Silvermoon) is the crown jewel of the blood elves, once the high elves, and the capital city of the kingdom of Quel'Thalas.

**Lor'themar Theron** — Lor'themar Theron (/ˈlɔɹ.θɛˌmɑːr ˌθɛ.ˈroʊn/ LOHR-theh-MAHR theh-ROHN) is the Regent Lord of Quel'Thalas and leader of the blood elves since the fall of Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider.
