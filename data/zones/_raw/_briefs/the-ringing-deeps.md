## Places and people this zone is about

**Brinthe** — High Speaker Brinthe is the current leader of the Machine Speakers and their representative in the Council of Dornogal. In the past she held the position of Speaker, overseeing Machine Speakers for millennia. After asking too many questions and speaking up too often, High Speaker Eirich sent her away to Dornogal.

**Skitter** — Skitter is a kobold located in the Ringing Deeps. She is the leader of the Quickwick tribe of kobolds.

**Camp Murroch** — Camp Murroch  is a quest hub in south-central Ringing Deeps. The camp is nestled between the Living Grotto to the west, Taelloch to the east, the Rumbling Wastes to the north, and the Ancient Falls to the south.

**Gundargaz** — Gundargaz  is the quest hub and largest settlement of the Ringing Deeps, found in the west-central part of the zone. It also serves as the Assembly of the Deeps renown headquarters.

**Gutterville** — Gutterville is located in the Verdant Gutters in the Ringing Deeps.

**Opportunity Point** — Opportunity Point  is the southernmost quest hub of the Ringing Deeps. The area (and elevator to the Orecreg up on the Isle of Dorn!) has been occupied by goblins of Opportunity Acquisitions, who work for Jastor Gallywix.

**Waterworks** — The Waterworks  is a kobold-infested dam in west-central Ringing Deeps. It provides power to various machines across the cavern. The interior area is a delve, with the entrance found at  on the southern end of the exterior area.

**Cataract River** — The Cataract River runs from the Waterworks at  south to the Living Grotto at  along the central western wall of the Ringing Deeps. The headwaters are at Shimmermist Falls and the river drains through the Living Grotto into Azj-Kahet.

**Chittering Den** — The Chittering Den  is a kobold- and undercrawler- filled area in the Ringing Deeps. Located on the mid-eastern wall, the area is bordered by the Obsidian Hollow to the southwest, the Fetid Grotto, Shadowvein Point and the Shadowvein Extraction Site to the west, and the Warrens to the northwest.

**Earthenworks** — The Earthenworks  is located in north-central Ringing Deeps. The area is bordered by the Stonevault Exterior to the north, Nibelgaz Mine to the east, Gundargaz to the south, Ironhaul Station and Hallowfall Gate to the southwest, and Candleslag Quarry to the west.

**Obsidian Hollow** — Obsidian Hollow  is a max-level shadow-filled corner of the Ringing Deeps, located centrally on the eastern wall of the zone.

**Taelloch Mine** — Taelloch Mine  is a lost mine in southeastern Ringing Deeps, accessed from either side of Camp Murroch.
