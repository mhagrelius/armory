## Opening paragraph

The Dread Wastes are an area of Pandaria, located south of Townlong Steppes and west of the Valley of the Four Winds and Vale of Eternal Blossoms. Separated from the rest of Pandaria by the Serpent's Spine constructed during the rule of Lei Shen, the Dread Wastes are the home of the Mantid. The landscape is dotted with massive Kypari trees that produce the amber which the Mantid utilize in many ways. Klaxxi'vess serves as the main quest hub for the zone, but there are two Pandaren settlements, Soggy's Gamble and Sunset Brewgarden, that provide additional quests and are necessary for unlocking all of the Paragons.
Following the arrival of the Alliance and Horde and the subsequent release of the Sha, the Sha of Fear possessed Grand Empress Shek'zeer and began corrupting both the Mantid and the land itself. Even the great Kypari trees began succumbing to the corruption and dying. Faced with no other choice, the Klaxxi ordered the death of the empress and her fear-driven lieutenants and were successful with the aid of adventurers.

## History

thumb|The Dread Wastes in World of Warcraft.
The Dread Wastes are the mantid homeland. From here, Grand Empress Shek'zeer commands a seemingly endless swarm of mantid. They hurl themselves against the Serpent's Spine wall, nearly overwhelming the Shado-Pan defenders. Historically, the mantid have swarmed in regular and predictable intervals, but this swarm is about ten years too soon, and the pandaren are caught off-guard. It's not that the Empress is getting more strategic; in fact, her whole society is in the middle of an upheaval.

Shek'zeer has been overwhelmed and possessed by the Sha of Fear, and she's running her empire into the ground. At her command, the mantid are embracing the sha, willfully corrupting themselves, their sacred trees, and the entire ecology of their land. Blind fear has driven the empress onto a warpath, and it's tearing her civilization apart. The Klaxxi, however, recognize that the empress has lost her mind, and that swarming Pandaria will mean little if their entire civilization is given over to the sha in the process. Most of the zone focuses around this conflict between the mantid swarm and the Klaxxi, showcasing both the terrible scope of a full-on swarm and the fallout of unchecked sha corruption.

The Stormstout family has come to bring the fight directly to the mantid, and have fallen in with the brewmasters of the Sunset Brewgarden who specialize in brewing with sap from the native kypari trees. Meanwhile, the crew of a wrecked fishing boat has built Soggy's Gamble, a ramshackle collection of huts on the southern coast, and is reaping the benefits of being the only Anglers beyond the wall.

## Geography

The Dread Wastes are the southern portion of Townlong region separated from the rest of Pandaria mainland by the great wall, Serpent's Spine. It is very similar to its northern counterpart, except that it apparently became twisted by sinister energies and a dark gloom blankets over the land along with a swath of sha-corrupted soil that has even managed to break through the wall into the Valley of the Four Winds and set the path of war towards the Gate of the Setting Sun, with mantid's relentless armies pouring through to conquer and devour everything that stands in their way. In the center of the twisted path stands the imperial palace of the mantid race, known as the Heart of Fear, a paragon to the Sha of Fear which took the entire race into its sway through their empress, Shek'zeer. The mantid Kyparis along the path have also been corrupted by the unstable energies. There is one Vess of the mantid in the heart of the Wastes, held by ones that openly rebel against their corrupted empress' madness, the Klaxxi - keepers of the mantid culture.

## Places and people this zone is about

**Klaxxi'vess** — Klaxxi'vess is the main base of the Klaxxi in central Dread Wastes. The Horrid March surrounds Klaxxi'vess on the north and south, while the Clutches of Shek'zeer are on the vess's western doorstep.

**Kor'vess** — Kor'vess is a mantid settlement in Kypari Kor, the giant kypari tree on the northern border of the Dread Wastes with Townlong Steppes. It provides the mantid swarm with most of its amber.

**Zan'vess** — Zan'vess is a kypari tree that lies offshore of southwestern Dread Wastes across the Briny Muck. The Amber Quarry and the Whispering Stones are on the southern side of the island.

**Rikkitun Village** — Rikkitun Village is nestled on the northwest side of Kor'vess along the northern border of Dread Wastes with Townlong Steppes. Chief Rikkitun leads the Rikkitun sprite village.

**Soggy's Gamble** — Soggy's Gamble is a town on the southeastern coast of the Dread Wastes founded by the shipwrecked crew of the Mist-Hopper, an Anglers vessel under the command of Captain "Soggy" Su-Dao.

**Mistblade Den** — Mistblade Den is a cave found at the south-eastern edge of the Lake of Stars in the Dread Wastes. It serves as a base of operations for the Mistblade tribe.

**Heart of Fear** — Heart of Fear is one of the three launch raids that opened in World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria. This is the seat of the mantid dominion, and a place where the queen of the mantid currently resides, possessed by the Sha of Fear.

**Mantid Empire** — The Mantid Empire is the mantid civilization ruled by Grand Empress Shek'zara.

**Vale of Eternal Blossoms** — The Vale of Eternal Blossoms is a region at the center of the continent of Pandaria, and has great cultural importance to both the pandaren and mogu, particularly due to the Mogu'shan Palace.

**Serpent's Spine** — The Serpent's Spine (or the Pandaren Wall and the Great Wall) is the great wall that separates the mantid territories - the Townlong Steppes and the Dread Wastes - from the Pandaren Empire. The wall is guarded by many Shado-Pan wallwatchers and has several garrisons.

**Sunset Brewgarden** — The Sunset Brewgarden can be found immediately southeast of the Dusklight Bridge on the northern border of Dread Wastes from Townlong Steppes. Nestled in an unnamed kypari tree, the Amber Vault can also be found inside, accessed via the south side of the tree on the Horrid March.

**Townlong Steppes** — Only the bravest pandaren dare enter these highlands. Although the area remains uncorrupted by the Sha, only the nomadic yaungols and the mantid choose to make the region their home.
